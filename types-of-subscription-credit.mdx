---
title: "Types of Subscription Credit"
---

Latchel applies several types of credits, each for a specific situation. This page defines each credit type, explains when to use it, and gives a real example, so AR can select the correct type when applying a credit memo (see [_How to Apply Credit Memos_](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-apply-credit-memos)). Some credit types require approval before they are applied — these are flagged in the table.

| **Type of Credit** | **Definition** | **Scenario / Slack reference** | **Credit Memo Product** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Overpayment Credit | Applied when a customer pays more than they were invoiced. | Citygate Property Management paid \$297 when invoiced only \$99. | Overpayment |
| Duplicate Payment Credit _(a form of overpayment)_ | Applied when a customer unintentionally processes two payments. | Sunflower Management Group was on ACH autopay and also paid manually, causing a double payment. | Overpayment |
| Credit Card Processing Fee Credit | Applied when a customer is charged a credit card processing fee they should not have been, or when a customer switches from credit card to bank account (that month's card fee is credited to the next invoice). **Approval required from @Amrit Parajuli.** | Pam T Property Management paid their October invoice via bank account but was charged a 3.5% processing fee. | Credit |
| Concession | Applied during the guarantee period for customers with Assisted Ledgering benefits, when the invoiced amount exceeds the property manager's actual collected revenue for that month. | Managed by the CPT Team; referenced in the Billing Inputs Master Document. | Credit |
| Goodwill Credit | Applied to address an inconvenience, maintain a good customer relationship, or compensate for unfulfilled services. | \$100 goodwill credit for Post Real Estate Group; one week's goodwill credit for HouseGuys USA after two missed emergencies | Credit |
| Active Unit Count Credit | Applied when a customer disputes the unit count and the case meets the criteria in the Active Unit Count Credit Request Policy. | Real Property Management Diamond: billed for 88 properties in July when it should have been 27. Synergy Facility Group, LLC: identified 322 new condo units that should not have been active in Latchel. | Credit |
| Monthly Minimum Credit | Applied to zero out the Unbilled invoice due to the monthly minimum Commitment | This is applied to customers with minimum commitment: <ul><li>If the contract is cancelled mid-term</li><li>If the subscription invoice is manually created</li></ul> | Credit |
| Termination Fee Credit | Applied when the CSM requests a waiver of the termination fee. **Approval:** if the total adjustment exceeds \$250, the head of CS must approve. | — | Credit |
