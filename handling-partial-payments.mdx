title: "Handling Partial Payments"
---

## **What Is a Partial Payment?**

A partial payment happens when the amount paid does not cover the full amount due on an invoice. For example, if an invoice totals \$100 and the customer sends \$50, the remaining \$50 stays open.

Partial payments can be intentional or unintentional, and most often occur with check or direct-deposit payments.

A common intentional case is customers billed by portfolio, who send payments split according to how their portfolios are divided on their end — either as several deposits that together cover the invoice, or as a partial amount now with the balance to follow later.

## **Process**

Use these steps to match a partial payment in the Cash Reconciliation tab.

1. **Highlight the payment.** In the Cash Reconciliation tab, highlight the payment line item on the left.
2. **Find the invoice.** In the search field, search for the customer's open invoice.
3. **Partial match.** Click **Partial match**.
4. **Review the result.** Confirm the payment matches the correct invoice. The invoice will show the difference — the remaining balance due after the payment is applied.

**Example 1: Partial Payment - Brick & Vine**

When a customer sends multiple deposits at the same time to cover a single invoice, repeat steps 1–3 for each payment line item until every one is matched to that invoice. If the payments come out slightly over by a few dollars, follow the _How to Handle Overpayment_ SOP.

This customer receives a single invoice but pays by direct deposit, sending the payment as several smaller deposits that arrive at the same time and together cover the full invoice.

For instance, a September invoice totaling \$5,414.02 might be covered by 9 direct deposits of varying amounts.

1. In the Cash Reconciliation tab, you'll see several direct deposits under this customer's name. Highlight one payment line item on the left.
2. Search for the customer's open invoice.
3. Click Partial match.
4. Review the match; the invoice shows the remaining balance after that deposit is applied.
5. Repeat steps 1–3 for each remaining deposit until all payment line items are matched and the invoice is fully covered.

If the deposits total slightly more than the invoice (over by a few dollars), follow the _How to Handle Overpayment_ SOP.

**Example 2: Partial Payment - Intrepid Professional Group**

When a customer intentionally pays only part of an invoice (for example, part now and the rest later), partial-match the payment and leave the remaining amount due as is. The partially paid invoice stays on the AR aging report, showing only the remaining balance due.

This customer intentionally pays only part of the invoice in a given month, for example, half now and pays the remaining balance the following month.

1. In the Cash Reconciliation tab, highlight the payment line item on the left.
2. Search for the customer's open invoice.
3. Click Partial match.
4. Review the match; the invoice shows the remaining balance after the payment is applied.
5. Leave the remaining amount due open. The partially paid invoice stays on the AR aging report, showing only the remaining balance, until the rest is paid.

When the next payment arrives, match it to the same invoice the same way to close out the remaining balance.
