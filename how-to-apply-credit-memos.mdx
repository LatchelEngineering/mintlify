---
title: "How to Apply Credit Memos "
---

A credit memo reduces an invoice amount after correcting or changing a previous transaction.

## **When to Apply a Credit Memo**

Apply a credit memo to reduce an invoice amount after a correction or change to a previous transaction. Common situations include:

- Unit count adjustment
- Overpayment
- Retrospective discount
- Contract change
- Invoice cancellation
- Bad debt write-off

The applicable type of credit depends on the situation. See the [credit types reference page](https://help.latchel.com/types-of-subscription-credit)for details on which to use.

## **Creating and Applying a Credit Memo **

1. From the customer's draft invoice page, click the three dots in the upper-right corner, then click Create credit memo.
2. Use the trash can icon to remove the product(s) that appear in the credit memo by default.
3. From the Add product dropdown, add a product and choose Credit.
4. Enter an amount that covers the entire amount of the unbilled invoice.
5. Review the credit memo in the preview to confirm it is correct, then click Save.
6. A success message appears in the bottom-right corner, and the new credit memo shows as a line item on the Credit Memos page.
