## Purpose

<Note>
  **Purpose of this document**

  Every Monday morning, Claude (Latchel's AI agent) runs the weekly Service Ops CSAT review automatically. It logs each new Typeform response in the **QA Review** tab, flags investigations, posts SDDIs and a summary to Slack, and updates the Customer Churn '26 spreadsheet. Claude does the first pass; **the Quality team is the human verification step.** This SOP explains what Claude produces, how to validate it, which columns you own, and how to close out each callout.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Who should read this document?**

  Quality Associates and Managers
</Note>

## Content

### Background and Deviation Guidelines

<Note>
  **What This Process Is Trying to Solve:**

  Claude speeds up the CSAT review by doing the lookups (BigQuery, Wavelength, Slack) and the first categorization. But Claude can misread a comment, miss context, or pick the wrong category. A Quality associate validates its output before anyone acts on it, so the data we report is accurate and every customer callout is reviewed by a person who decides the right next step.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **When to break this SOP**

  - Claude's run fails or doesn't post by mid-morning Monday. Tell Jennifer Lye and run the review manually using [How to: Update and Report CSAT Feedback](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Quality-Program/How-to-Update-and-Report-CSAT-Feedback).
  - You find an emergency or safety issue Claude didn't flag. Escalate it right away; don't wait for the weekly cycle.
</Warning>

### What Claude does every Monday

Claude runs at about **7:48 AM PT** every Monday and:

- Adds one row per new survey response to the **QA Review** tab of the [Ops CSAT Results](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1YW7plF1nwkS-I2U1DyAmHWsDoR3uojEY0Pss2DA-QGU/edit#gid=0) sheet (columns A–Z). Each new row has today's date in **column Y (Reviewed on)**.
- Looks up identifiable customers (only when they gave an email or WO number) in BigQuery and Wavelength, and writes what it found in **column Q (Investigation notes)**.
- Posts an SDDI to **#ps-dd-sddi** for each row that needs one (L = Yes), and links the post in column Z.
- Moves AI/Lucia feedback to the Lucia CSAT sheet.
- Posts one summary to **#svc-ops-sync**.
- Fills the CSAT rows (57–65) for the week that just ended in the **Customer Churn '26** tab of Svc Operations Team Level Metrics.

<Danger>
  Claude never contacts customers, never decides who should contact them, and never opens ASM tickets. Those decisions belong to people.
</Danger>

### Who owns each column

| Column(s) | Filled by | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A–F, R–U, W–Y | Claude (copied or looked up) | Spot-check only |
| G–K (associate feedback, theme, Ops controllable) | Claude | **Validate and correct** |
| L–M (SDDI needed, priority) | Claude; validated by Product Support | Leave to Product Support |
| N (ASM link) | Product Support | Leave as is |
| O–P (product opportunity) | Claude; validated by Product Support | Leave to Product Support |
| Q (Investigation notes) | Claude | **Validate the facts** |
| V (Route to) | Claude | **Validate** |
| Z (SDDI Slack post) | Claude | Leave as is |
| **AA (Quality review status)** | **Quality** | Set to "Resolved" when your review is done |

### Step by step guide

#### Step 1 - Open Monday's output

1. Open the Claude summary in **#svc-ops-sync**. Its sections are: 1. Associate feedback, 2. Product feedback, 3. Investigations required (SDDI and Quality to review), 4. Investigations without an ASM link, 5. Other routing, and 6. Actionable but untraceable.
2. Open the **QA Review** tab and filter **column Y (Reviewed on)** to today's date. These are the rows to validate.

#### Step 2 - Validate the categorization on every new row

1. Read the feedback in **column A**.
2. Check **column G**: is it about how a Latchel associate handled the interaction? Blank feedback is always "Yes".
3. Check **column H (Theme)** against the comment.
4. Check **column I (Ops Controllable / Non-Ops Controllable)**.
   - Ops Controllable: something Service Ops can change through process, training or resources.
   - Non-Ops Controllable: PM decisions, vendor delays or quality, payments, platform issues.
5. Check **columns J and K** for associate feedback. If K names an associate with negative, Ops-controllable feedback, report it in **#svc-ops-feedback** if it's valid.
6. If any value is wrong, overwrite the cell with the correct value.

<Warning>
  Column I feeds the Customer Churn '26 spreadsheet. If you change I on a row, recount rows 57, 58, 60 and 61 for that week's column and correct them.
</Warning>

#### Step 3 - Validate the investigation notes (column Q)

1. For each row with notes in **column Q**, open the WO in the admin app.
2. Confirm the WO number, state, days in state and PM company match what Claude wrote.
3. If something is wrong or out of date, correct column Q.

<Tip>
  Claude's notes are a snapshot from Monday morning, and the WO may have moved since. If they differ, go with what you see in the admin app.
</Tip>

#### Step 4 - SDDIs and product feedback (Product Support)

Product Support owns SDDIs (L = Yes) and product feedback (O = YES): validating them, opening ASM tickets and routing product feedback. See [SOP: Weekly CSAT QA Review - SDDIs and Product Feedback](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Weekly-CSAT-QA-Review-SDDIs-and-Product-Feedback).

1. If you spot an SDDI that looks wrong, or a low score that should have been an SDDI, reply in the #ps-dd-sddi thread (or post there) so Product Support can review it.

#### Step 5 - Review "Quality to review" callouts

<Danger>
  **Untraceable rows: skip them.** If a row has no property, resident or WO you can find (no email or WO was given, or the email, phone or WO given doesn't match anything in the platform), there's nothing to review or investigate. Don't search for the customer some other way, and don't contact anyone. Leave the row as is and move on; it doesn't need a column AA status.

  Claude marks these rows with **column Q starting "Untraceable:"** and **V = "No action"**. They show up in the summary only under **6. Actionable but untraceable**, as a count for trend tracking. If you find an untraceable row routed to "Quality to review", change V to "No action", start Q with "Untraceable:", and tell **Jennifer Lye**.
</Danger>

Rows with **V = "Quality to review"** are traceable callouts that don't need an SDDI (for example, already rescued, WO moving normally, or the issue isn't clear). They are flagged in the summary under **Quality to review (no SDDI)**. Claude only lists the facts and what it couldn't verify; it does not recommend next steps.

1. Validate the facts in column Q against the WO and any related ASM ticket.
2. Decide whether anyone needs to contact the customer.
   - If yes, escalate to the right team (usually **Ops Support**) using your normal escalation process.
   - If no, nothing else is needed.
3. Add a short note at the end of **column Q** with your decision (for example, "QA: visit confirmed completed 10/19, no contact needed" or "QA: escalated to Ops Support").
4. Set **column AA** to **"Resolved"**.

<Danger>
  Contact with customers for investigations is by email only. Never call a customer for a CSAT investigation.
</Danger>

#### Step 6 - Check routing

1. Confirm **column V (Route to)** is right for every row. Rows routed to "Lucia/AI sheet" should also appear on the Lucia CSAT sheet.

#### Step 7 - Close out your review

1. Set **column AA** to **"Resolved"** on every row you reviewed that needed an action: "Quality to review" rows, Billing/Payments rows and rows with associate feedback. Untraceable rows (Q starts "Untraceable:") don't need a status.
2. Leave AA blank on any row you haven't finished, so it stays visible as open.

<Warning>
  Claude never writes to column AA, so anything you set there stays as you left it.
</Warning>

### Correcting Claude's mistakes

- Fix the cell directly in the sheet. Claude doesn't change earlier weeks' rows on later runs (except obvious errors, which it calls out in the Slack summary).
- If you see the same mistake more than once (for example, a type of comment that keeps getting the wrong theme), tell **Jennifer Lye** so Claude's instructions can be updated.
- Never paste customer emails or phone numbers into #svc-ops-sync. Use the customer name and WO number.
