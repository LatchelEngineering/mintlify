# Documentation Updates

<Info>
  ## Major Updates/Training

  <Accordion title="🚨 URGENT: Spanish Process in Service Ops">
    **Why it matters:**<br />Spanish-speaking customers shouldn't face delays while their request waits for a Spanish-speaking associate. Every associate now handles Spanish contacts directly.

    **What changed:**<br />Associates handle Spanish SMS, emails, troubleshooting tasks and follow-up tasks themselves using the [Spanish SMS Translator](https://claude.ai/public/artifacts/35daba8e-9963-4de9-82bb-74d3999cb190). Inbound Spanish calls are transferred to our Spanish call center partner (Sunshine Call Center). **No troubleshooting call is required for Spanish-speaking residents.**

    **What you need to do:**

    1. **Inbound Calls:** transfer any Spanish-speaking caller through **Quick Connects > Spanish Queue**, press **2**, then click **Join**.
    2. **SMS/Email:** reply right away with the Spanish SMS Translator, starting with the translation disclosure message.
    3. **TS tasks:** don't defer or wait for a Spanish speaker. Review the intake (click **Translate**). If the details are clear and match the PM's intake settings, move forward. If you need more info, SMS the resident within **20 minutes**, wait 3 minutes, send 1 reminder, wait 3 more minutes, then follow **Option 3 – Can't reach resident**.
    4. **Follow-up tasks:** check who the task is for first. For Spanish speakers, skip the call and SMS via the translator. On emergency WOs, apply the Stale Emergency rule after 3 minutes with no reply.
    5. **No SMS or email:** follow **Spanish Speaker Without SMS** in the SOP.

    **When it takes effect:**<br />Effective immediately

    **Resources impacted:**

    - [How to: Handle Spanish SMS/Emails/Calls](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Spanish-SMS-Emails-Calls)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="PM Troubleshooting Instructions (Beta)">
    **Why it matters:**<br />PMs can now add their own troubleshooting instructions in Intake Settings, and our AI is expected to follow them during intake. Troubleshooters need to catch anything the AI missed and respect PM restrictions on every call.

    **What changed:**<br />Previously there was no PM-specific troubleshooting guidance to check. Now **Troubleshooting Instructions** appear at the top of **Critical notes & escalation** in the Ops Assistant plug-in (v0.6.9), and they follow the same rules as critical notes.

    **What you need to do:**

    1. Before every TS call, check Troubleshooting Instructions against the intake transcript. Don’t re-ask what the AI already covered. Ask any PM questions it missed, note them in the call notes, and submit a bug report. Follow every “do not” instruction, even if the Question Bank says otherwise.

    **When it takes effect:**<br />Monday, 11/02/2026

    **Resources impacted:**

    - [How to: Properly Diagnose Emergency Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Properly-Diagnose-Emergency-Situations)
    - [Job Aid: Critical, Pre-dispatch, Warranty, Special Notes & EEC (Q10)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-Critical-Pre-dispatch-Warranty-Special-Notes-Emergency-Escalation-Contacts)
    - [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders): Reworded the 20-minute call SLA to start when the WO enters Troubleshooting, matching the plugin’s SLA countdown.
    - [Job-Aid: Ops Assistant Plug-in](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Operations-Associate-Job-Aid-Latchel-Service-Ops-Assistant-Plug-in): Updated to v0.6.9 and added Troubleshooting Instructions and the new SLA countdown.
  </Accordion>
