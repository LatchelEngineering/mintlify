  ## Major Updates/Training

  ### 1. AI Messaging Beta — Handling Resident & SP Conversations

  **What changed:**<br />For beta customers, resident and service provider texts are moving from SMS to **Conversations**. These are browser-based chat threads that **Latchel AI** (formerly Lucia) answers first. Latchel AI transfers the conversation to a person when the customer asks for one or when it can't handle the request. You'll see a new **Conversations** icon next to the SMS icon.

  **What you need to do:**

  1. Work **both queues** (SMS and Conversations). They have **equal priority**, so always handle the **oldest message first** by timestamp.

  **Beta customers:** [Scarpa Properties](https://app.latchel.com/admin/pm-company/33410)

  **When it takes effect:**<br />**Monday, 9/28/2026.**

  **Resources impacted:**

  - [Job Aid: Handling Resident & Service Provider Conversations (AI Messaging Beta)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-Handling-Resident-and-SP-Conversations-AI-Messaging-Beta)

  ### 2. Preferred SP Retry Process (Emergency Dispatch Deferral)

  **What changed:**<br />For **non-PD/EH emergencies (24/7 customers)**, associates do **one final retry** before we escalate to the PM. This applies when no preferred SP (or other eligible SP) can be secured.

  **What you need to do:**

  1. **24/7 Emergency Only subscription (any day):**
     - **Before 2PM** property time → create a task due in **3 hours**.
     - **At or after 2PM** property time → create a task due **9AM property time the next morning**.
     - When the task comes due, re-call each preferred SP **once** as a final attempt. Skip any SP who declined.
  2. If the retry is unsuccessful, escalate to the PM per the SOP.

  **When it takes effect:**<br />**Monday, 9/28/2026.**

  **Resources impacted:**

  - Preferred SP Retry Process section of [(EMERGENCY) How to: Assign a Preferred + Latchel Network Service Provider](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/EMERGENCY-How-to-Assign-a-Preferred-Latchel-Network-Service-Provider#preferred-sp-retry-process)
  - How to Handle Emergency Work Orders

  ---

  ### 3. Sheriff — Replacing Deputy for Time Tracking

  **What changed:**<br />**Sheriff** is our new app for clocking in and out, viewing shifts, and requesting time off. It replaces **Deputy**. You sign in with your **@latchel.com Google account**, so there's no password to remember.

  **What you need to do:**

  1. Bookmark the app: [sheriff-110204726220.us-central1.run.app](https://sheriff-110204726220.us-central1.run.app)
  2. **Until the 9/28 go-live, use BOTH Sheriff and Deputy.**

  1. For questions or issues, reach out to your supervisor. If something is broken, message **Matias** with what you clicked, what happened, and a screenshot.

  **When it takes effect:**<br />**Monday, 9/28/2026.**

  **Resources impacted:**
