# Documentation Updates

<Info>
  ## Major Updates/Training

  ### Stale Emergencies — One Rule for De-escalating vs. Forwarding to PM

  **What changed:**<br />Stale emergency handling now lives in one place, the **Stale Emergency rule**, and all related SOPs point to it.

  - **Not PD/EH:** de-escalate to Urgent, keep "(EMERGENCY)" in the title, and keep the WO in a status the vendor can schedule in. Don't forward just because the WO is stale.
  - **PD/EH:** don't de-escalate. Call the escalation contacts, and if they're unresponsive, Forward to PM.
  - **Only Forward to PM** when the PM says they'll take over, or when the case matches an approved scenario in How to: Put a Job on Hold.
  - **New exception:** on a PD/EH WO, if the PM-assigned SP is unresponsive and the PM was already notified that it's PD/EH, de-escalate, keep the WO schedulable, and create a PM task.

  **What you need to do:**

  1. Check the exceptions in the Stale Emergency rule before you de-escalate or forward.
  2. When a PM asks you to assign a specific SP, ask on the same call whether they want a call back if the SP doesn't answer, and let them know we'll de-escalate if we can't reach either of them. Use the script in the PM Outbound job aid.

  **When it takes effect:**<br />**Monday, 10/12/26**
