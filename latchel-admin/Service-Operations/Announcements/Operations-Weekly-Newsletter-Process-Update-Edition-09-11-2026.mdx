# Documentation Updates

<Info>
  ## Major Update/Training

  ### Handling Emergency Work Orders — Extended Training

  **What changed:**<br />We now have explicit criteria for when an emergency work order is considered **stale**, plus a required same-day decision once that happens. The decision isn't binary — there are three possible paths, and which one applies depends on the reason. PD/EH work orders are the exception and are never de-escalated or forwarded away from active handling.

  **What you need to do:**

  1. **What you need to do:**
     1. When you identify an emergency WO has gone stale (a follow-up went unanswered, the PM took ownership, the immediate issue resolved but the WO hasn't, a PM constraint is blocking progress, or there's simply no concrete next step today) — act the **same day** you spot it.
     2. For emergency follow-ups specifically: you get **one (1) attempt**. If it's unsuccessful, do not defer to the next day — choose one of the three outcomes below immediately:
        - **Intervene** — take direct action yourself to keep it moving (schedule the appointment, reassign the vendor).
        - **De-escalate to Urgent** — if there's no active PD/EH condition and the emergency path has stalled.
        - **Forward to the PM** — if they've taken ownership or their instructions/constraints are what's blocking progress.
     3. If you de-escalate or forward, keep **"(EMERGENCY)"** in the WO title and leave the **mandatory internal note** documenting why.
     4. **Exception:** if the WO involves active PD/EH, never de-escalate or forward — call the Emergency Escalation Contacts instead if progress stalls.

  **When it takes effect:**<br />**Monday, 9/14/2026.**
