<Info>
  ## Major Updates/Training

  #### Call-Ahead SMS — Helping Residents Recognize Our Troubleshooting Calls

  **What changed:**<br />Some residents’ phones label the Latchel number “Possible Scam,” so they don’t pick up our troubleshooting calls. There’s now a call-ahead SMS step between the 1st and 2nd TS call attempts.

  - **When:** only if the resident doesn’t answer the 1st call. If they pick up, no SMS is needed.
  - **Not sent when:** SMS isn’t enabled for the resident.
  - **After the 3rd attempt:** nothing changes. Still leave the voicemail and send the SMS Blurb as usual.

  **What you need to do:**

  1. If the resident doesn’t answer your 1st TS call, send this SMS before calling again:
     1. _“Hi [resident name]! This is Latchel, the maintenance team for [PM Company Name]. We’re calling you to discuss your work order request. Please pick up when you see our call.”_
  2. Then make your 2nd and 3rd call attempts.

  **When it takes effect:**<br />Monday, 10/19/2026

  **Resources impacted:**

  - [How to: Properly Diagnose Emergency Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Properly-Diagnose-Emergency-Situations) (step 2, Call the resident)
  - [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders) (Step 1, item 3; the “Can’t reach resident” steps now reference it)
