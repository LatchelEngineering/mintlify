## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
  We want to ensure that work orders are handled quickly and correctly when intake processing fails during custom emergency intake settings testing. The goal is to provide a clear process for Troubleshooters to follow when the system cannot automatically triage a work order—so that emergencies are not missed, residents receive prompt help, and operational errors are minimized, protecting both customer safety and Latchel’s service quality.
</Info>

<Info>
  ## What to Expect

  - During the test, if **intake processing fails** for a work order, the system will **automatically create a task for Troubleshooters**.
  - These tasks require immediate action to ensure resident safety and proper escalation.
</Info>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### 1. Task Notification

- Monitor for new tasks labeled as “Intake Processing Failed”
- **Review Complete Intake & Lucia Transcripts:**
  - **Complete Intake Transcript** — Open the full intake conversation in the Latchel Console or Service Ops Assistant plug-in. Do not rely solely on work order details or AI summary cards. Reviewing the complete transcript ensures you see all resident statements, specific symptom descriptions, and prior troubleshooting attempts that may have been missed during the intake processing failure.
  - **Lucia Transcript (if applicable)** — If the work order was handled or transferred by Lucia, read the full Lucia conversation to understand what questions were asked and what responses the resident provided before the system failed to process the intake.
  - **Emergencies** - Proceed directly to **Step 2**.
  - **Urgent / Normal** – Review the full intake details and confirm the **Work Order Title**, **Description**, and **Skill/Category** accurately match the resident’s reported issue.

    - **If the details align and the next step is clear:** Proceed to **Step 3**. A troubleshooting call may be skipped when the title, description, and skill/category clearly match the reported issue.
    - **If details are missing, unclear, or inconsistent:** Call the resident first and troubleshoot.
      - After the call, update the **Work Order Title**, **Description**, and/or **Skill/Category** as needed.
      - Then proceed to **Step 3**.
  - **On-call Work Orders -** cancel the work order and recreate the work order following the same flow the resident went through.

### 2. Resident Troubleshooting Call

- Call the resident as soon as possible.
- Use the troubleshooting script or checklist provided for emergency situations.
  - Refer to [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders#how-to-handle-emergency-work-orders)

### 3. Determine Next Steps

Based on the outcome of the call, take one of the following actions:

- **Dispatch a Service Provider:** If the issue is confirmed as an emergency or requires urgent attention.
- **Forward to Property Manager:** If the situation requires PM approval or input.
  - Refer to approved scenarios when a work order can be moved to Waiting for PM. [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#how-to-put-a-job-on-hold)
  - **\$1 placeholder for approval holds:** If moving a non-emergency work order out of **Intake Processing** into **Needs PM Approval** / **Needs Owner Approval** requires an amount but there is no estimate to approve, enter **\$1** as the required nominal placeholder.

    - Use a clear approval note so the PM/owner understands this is not an estimate. Example: _“Approval requested to dispatch a service provider for [issue]. \$1 entered as a nominal placeholder for initial scope/dispatch authorization.”_
- **De-escalate to Non-Emergency:** If the issue does not meet emergency criteria after troubleshooting.
- **Other Actions:** Follow standard procedures for any other outcomes (e.g., resident unavailable, issue resolved, etc.).
  - Unresponsive residents - move the work order to Pending with a task to the Property Manager

### 4. Documentation

- Document all actions taken in the work order notes.
  - Refer to [How to: Write Effective Notes](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Write-Effective-Notes#how-to-write-effective-notes)

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

- If you make a mistake or cannot complete the required action or if you are unsure how to proceed, or if the scenario is not covered, escalate to Operations Support Specialists for guidance.
