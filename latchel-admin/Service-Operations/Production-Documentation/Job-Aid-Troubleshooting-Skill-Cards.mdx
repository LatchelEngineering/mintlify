## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want every Troubleshooting (TS) trainee to learn each skill from the current SOPs, not from outdated course material. Each card points to the live Question Bank steps, the emergency and PD/EH rules for that skill, the safety stops, and the videos that are safe to use.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Who should read this document?** Troubleshooting cross-trainees, shadow partners, and Service Ops Supervisors. These cards replace the 16 skill-specific Troubleshooting courses in [How to: Perform Cross-trainings and Refreshers](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Perform-Cross-trainings-and-Refreshers).
</Note>

## 📋 How to Use the Cards

1. **Before every skill:** check the PM's Harmonia intake settings first, then run the PD/EH check ([How to: Properly Diagnose Emergency Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Properly-Diagnose-Emergency-Situations)).
2. **Steps** always come from the linked [Job Aid: Troubleshooter Question Bank](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-Troubleshooter-Question-Bank). The cards do not repeat the steps, so they never go out of date.
3. **Videos** come from [(EMERGENCY) Job Aid: Troubleshooting Videos](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/EMERGENCY-Job-Aid-Troubleshooting-Videos):
   - ✅ **OK to guide** — the resident can follow these steps.
   - ⚠️ **Knowledge only** — optional; helps you understand the issue; never ask the resident to do it. Not required for cross-training.
   - ❌ **Do not use** — conflicts with current SOPs.
4. **Skills not seen live** during shadowing are covered as walk-throughs during reverse shadowing.

**Abbreviations:** QB = [Troubleshooter Question Bank](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-Troubleshooter-Question-Bank) · QRG = [Quick Reference Guide: Emergency and Non-Emergency Triggers](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Quick-Reference-Guide-Emergency-and-Non-Emergency-Triggers) · PD/EH = [How to: Identify Property Damage and Extreme Habitability Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Identify-Property-Damage-and-Extreme-Habitability-Situations) · Tips = [Job Aid: Troubleshooter Tips for Tricky Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-Troubleshooter-Tips-for-Tricky-Situations)

  <Accordion title="1. Air Conditioning / No Cooling">
    **Steps:** QB → **Repair A/C & General HVAC**. Probing questions, safety shutoff if the unit can't be controlled, then thermostat, batteries, filter, breaker reset, vents, photos, dispatch.

    **Emergency when:**

    - "No A/C in extreme heat" (QRG)
    - PD/EH if it reaches above 100°F outside in the next 3 days, or the PM's shorter timeframe (PD/EH)
    - Latchel defaults: 95°F standard, 85°F for infants, elderly or disabled residents (Tips)
    - Always ask the indoor temperature. Within about 20°F of outdoors with no failure signs → de-escalate (Tips)

    **Safety stops:**

    - Unit can't be controlled → breaker OFF, never reset (QB)
    - Thermostat "off" is not power off. Never ask a resident to open a panel or cover (PD/EH)

    **Videos:**

    - ✅ [AC not cooling](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_b6p0fkWhCY) (filter, vents, thermostat only)

    - ⚠️ [Central Air Troubleshooting - Top 10 AC Problems](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mc8tnEymAys)

  <Accordion title="2. Heating / Furnace / Heat Pump">
    **Steps:** QB → **No heat (Furnace)**, plus **No heat (Heat pump/Mini-split)**, **No heat (Boiler)** and **No heat (Electrical baseboard heaters)**.

    **Emergency when:**

    - "No heat in winter" (QRG)
    - PD/EH if below 32°F outside in the next 3 days. No EEC call if a confirmed appointment lands on or before the cold day (PD/EH)
    - Latchel defaults: 45°F standard, 55°F vulnerable; indoor 64°F / 60°F (Tips)
    - A space heater or fireplace can defer the visit overnight but never de-escalates the WO (Tips)

    **Safety stops:**

    - Gas smell → evacuate, call the gas company or fire department, never relight (QB)
    - Relight only with a push-button ignitor, never a match or lighter (QB)
    - Abnormal behavior → breaker OFF, not reset (QB)

    **Videos:**

    - ✅ [Furnace Blowing Cold Air, Where to Start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QJAMWlJ0tbk) (error codes)
    - ✅ [Furnace Not Keeping Up, Fan is loud](https://youtu.be/gj7HAxS9nX0) (filters)
    - ✅ [Furnace Filter FAQs](https://youtu.be/uPaZvHp4L2Y)
    - ⚠️ [Top 5 Furnace Problems and How to Fix Them](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T4IWtKy--Vk)
    - ⚠️ [Flame Sensor Troubleshooting](https://youtu.be/-QKHz1Mv9Ew)
    - ⚠️ [How to tell if your home thermostat is bad](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lacAkjVJxdY)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="3. Water Heater">
    **Steps:** QB → **Water heater not working** and **Leaking water heater**. No hot water: breaker and push-button pilot. Leak: gas or electric, shut off gas or power, shut off the cold-water supply, mop up, photos, dispatch.

    **Emergency when:**

    - Any active or continuous leak (QRG)
    - PD/EH if it fills more than a bucket a day and isn't draining, or can't be contained (PD/EH)
    - No hot water alone: follow the PM's intake settings ([How to: Properly Diagnose Emergency Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Properly-Diagnose-Emergency-Situations))

    **Safety stops:**

    - Gas smell → leave the home, call the gas company (QB)
    - No open-flame relighting; push-button ignitor only (QB)
    - A dial set to "off" still has power (QB)

    **Videos:**

    - ✅ [Leaking Water Heater - Diagnostics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1YpJg3q7hRk)
    - ✅ [How to drain a water heater](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W5nuZj3wPwA) (only if the resident is comfortable)
    - ⚠️ [Hot Water Heater - How It Works](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gQIatogID5c)
    - ⚠️ [How to Light Your Water Heater Pilot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kmI8HjMH5vs)
    - ⚠️ [How To Reset Flammable Vapor Sensor on Water Heater](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6t1nsqiU20Y)
    - ⚠️ [Top 10 Water Heater Problems](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wSrk1zB4EWk)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="4. Leaks and Water Shut-Off">
    **Steps:** QB → **Water leak** (source, severity, individual valve, bucket test, main valve, mop-up, photos). Also QB → **Roof leak during a storm**, QB → **Repair internal leaking (HVAC)**, and Tips → basement water during active rain.

    **Emergency when:**

    - Active or continuous leak, ceiling water, burst pipe, standing water that won't drain (QRG)
    - PD if it fills more than a bucket a day and isn't draining, or the source is unknown or can't be contained (PD/EH)
    - Water shut off but damage present → remediation WO now, leak repair in parallel (Tips)
    - Basement water in rain that the resident can contain is not PD/EH; don't dispatch extraction by default (Tips)

    **Safety stops:**

    - Major flooding → 911 (PD/EH)
    - Water near outlets or appliances → the resident stays away (Tips)
    - Never share the reporting resident's details with a neighbor (QB)

    **Videos:**

    - ✅ [Best Ways to Find Water Leaks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rgkq63IubS4)
    - ✅ [How To Locate Your Home's Water Meter & Water Shut Off Valve](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gBUGCBLPERc)
    - ✅ [Water shut-off valve - different types and locations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRxiLRZnaGU)
    - ⚠️ [How Does a Sump Pump Work?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6hlVpngipCo) (the resident never approaches water near wiring)
    - ⚠️ [How To Clean Out Your Flooded Home In The Event Of a Hurricane Like Harvey](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SlQ4xtizrfg)
    - ⚠️ [Flex Seal Works! Leaking Roof!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s2vfMj9W2II&amp;t=95s) (small leaks only, when no vendor is available; don't promise tarping until an SP is confirmed)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="5. Leaking Toilet">
    **Steps:** QB → **Leaking toilet**. Severity, source (tank, base, internal), individual shut-off, bucket test, main valve, mop-up, photos, dispatch.

    **Emergency when:**

    - Active leak or standing water that won't drain (QRG)
    - PD/EH if the resident can't or won't clean it up with a towel (PD/EH)
    - Stopping use of the toilet doesn't by itself make it an emergency ([How to: Properly Diagnose Emergency Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Properly-Diagnose-Emergency-Situations))

    **Safety stops:**

    - Cracked tank → drain it safely first (QB)
    - Even if other toilets work, still check whether water remediation is needed (QB)

    **Videos:**

    - ✅ [Leaking water around the toilet (floor)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzjc0j99ZC0) (shut-off only, not the wax ring)
    - ✅ [Internal Leaking Diagnostics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYoCgIO4OlQ)
    - ✅ [How To Detect A Toilet Leak](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yT8FYDB43a0)
    - ✅ [How toilet works and common problems](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qSUv6rAez9o)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="6. Clogged Drain / Clogged Toilet">
    **Steps:** QB → **Clogged drain** and **Clogged Toilet**. Drain Pack check in the Resident Critical Note, plunge or snake, P-trap clean if the resident is comfortable, one alternative only (soap and hot water, or baking soda and vinegar).

    **Emergency when:**

    - Completely blocked drain, toilet overflowing non-stop, or sewage smell (QRG)
    - A slow drain is non-emergency (QRG)
    - All drains affected → use card 7 (Sewage Backup)

    **Safety stops:**

    - No harsh chemicals; gloves and eye protection (QB)
    - Shut the toilet supply valve before plunging (QB)
    - Don't hang up to wait on a fix; dispatch and ask them to call back to cancel if it clears (QB)

    **Videos:**

    - ✅ [How to unclog a toilet using hot water & dish soap](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BLLF6-5kxqY)
    - ✅ [How to unclog a kitchen sink using baking soda and vinegar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4u0sMRKb0NY)
    - ✅ [How to Unclog a Toilet: Pro Techniques](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEMdOD9mqa0)
    - ✅ [How to Unclog a Bathtub Drain](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BXPJ7BfqpVQ)
    - ✅ [How to unclog a kitchen sink](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JeurinbyqhQ) (P-trap clean only if the resident is comfortable)
    - ⚠️ [How to Unclog a Bathtub and Shower Drain](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BccZWAcXswY) (chemicals, hand auger)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="7. Sewage Backup">
    **Steps:** QB → **Clear Sewage Backup**. One drain or all drains, city sewer or septic, photos if visible, resident stops using all water, dispatch.

    **Emergency when:**

    - Sewage coming up from drains or a strong sewage smell (QRG)
    - PD if continuous and overflowing, or the resident can't or won't clean it up (PD/EH)
    - Only one drain affected → check intake settings and de-escalate if allowed (QB)

    **Safety stops:**

    - Major or uncontrolled flooding → [911-Severity process](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-911-Severity-Type-Work-Orders)

    **Videos:** None yet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="8. Power / Electrical">
    **Steps:** QB → **Power outage** (neighbors and utility, reset breakers with dry hands, bulbs for partial outages) and QB → **Outlets not working** (GFCI, then breaker box).

    **Emergency when:**

    - Burning smell, sparks, exposed wiring, or no power in most or all rooms (QRG)
    - A sparking outlet is PD/EH only if power to that area can't be shut off (PD/EH)
    - Fire Hazard / Power Shutoff: signs present now = Fire Hazard/PD; past signs only + breaker confirmed off = follow PM settings (Tips)

    **Safety stops:**

    - Flames, smoke or sparking → [911-Severity process](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-911-Severity-Type-Work-Orders) now
    - Burning smell or damage near the panel → don't reset the breaker (QB)
    - Never ask a resident to open a panel or equipment cover (PD/EH)

    **Videos:**

    - ✅ [How to Reset Your Breakers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5eZFZXJRnKE)
    - ✅ [How to Reset a Tripped Circuit Breaker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZKo_yZhQv8)
    - ✅ [Breaker panel & partial power outages](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XftSn_T6AOs)
    - ✅ [Is your outlet not working? See how to reset your GFCI outlets and circuit breaker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trLeLJdMWWI)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="9. Lockout and Doors">
    **Steps:** QB → **Locked out** (other doors and windows, smart-lock power, someone with a key, card method only without a deadbolt, photos, locksmith) and QB → **Unsecure door** (photos, alignment, latch, strike plate, electronics, door brace).

    **Emergency when:**

    - The door can't be locked or won't stay closed (QRG)
    - Lockout below 32°F with no other shelter = EH (Tips)
    - Unsecured exterior door = EH only with direct access into the unit; gates and common-area doors don't count (PD/EH)

    **Safety stops:**

    - Break-in in progress or suspect on site → 911
    - Card method only on doors without a deadbolt, with a card the resident doesn't need (QB)

    **Videos:**

    - ✅ [How to Fix a Door that Won't Close - Two Things to Try with a Screwdriver](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7_xFNIjCKW4) (use a door brace, not furniture, to secure the door)
    - ❌ 10 Ways To Open A Door Without A Key (goes beyond the QB lockout steps)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="10. Broken Window">
    **Steps:** QB → **Broken window**. What happened, window type, cracked or shattered, clear the area, gloves and tape, temporary cover, photos.

    **Emergency when:**

    - The window can't be locked (QRG)
    - Doesn't fully close but can be secured → non-emergency (QRG)

    **Safety stops:**

    - Resident is uncomfortable → stop and gather information only (QB)
    - Attempted break-in or injury → 911

    **Videos:** None yet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="11. Garage Door">
    **Steps:** QB → **Garage door**. Tracks and rollers, sensors, GFCI reset if the opener is fully unresponsive, springs, cables, emergency release, security questions, photos.

    **Emergency when:**

    - The home can't be secured or is accessible (QB)
    - PM intake settings decide severity ([How to: Properly Diagnose Emergency Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Properly-Diagnose-Emergency-Situations))

    **Safety stops:**

    - GFCI reset only with the resident's consent (QB)
    - Visible damage → don't close the door or use the emergency release (QB)

    **Videos:**

    - ✅ [How to manually open and close your garage door](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rtpb9Pa8ong) (only with no visible damage)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="12. Utility Issues (No Water / No Power / No Gas)">
    **Steps:** [(Emergency) How to: Handle a Utility Issue](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-a-Utility-Issue): check for same-building WOs, resident calls the utility, mark "Issue IS an emergency" + "Work order does not need immediate dispatch", Pending, TS follow-up task for 12 PM the next day. Also QB → **Water Supply issue ( No water)**. No gas: the resident calls the gas company and the WO is cancelled (QB → **No heat (Boiler)**, Step 5).

    **Emergency when:**

    - No running water = EH (PD/EH)
    - No power in most or all rooms (QRG)
    - PD/EH → call the PM or escalation contacts at the same time (Utility Issue SOP)

    **Safety stops:**

    - Gas smell → 911, unless the gas company has confirmed it's safe ([911-Severity process](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-911-Severity-Type-Work-Orders))
    - Power pole falling or leaning on the building → 911 (PD/EH)

    **Videos:**

    - ✅ [How To Locate Your Home's Water Meter & Water Shut Off Valve](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gBUGCBLPERc)
    - ✅ Breaker videos (see card 8)
  </Accordion>
