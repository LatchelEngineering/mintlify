## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to ensure that urgent scheduling requests for emergency work orders are managed quickly, consistently, and with clear communication. The goal of this SOP is to provide a standardized, step-by-step process for securing appointments with service providers and residents when emergencies arise. This process prevents delays, miscommunication, and missed appointments—reducing risk to residents and property, and ensuring that emergencies are resolved as fast as possible.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The following process applies when a task is created for Troubleshooters to schedule a work order. Scheduling during dispatch must adhere to the guidelines outlined in [this document](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders), ensuring that a service provider is assigned to a work order only after they confirm an appointment.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **What does it mean?**<br />The resident has reported an emergency that requires immediate scheduling with a service provider. Ops troubleshooters and associates must recognize the urgency of these issues and prioritize securing an appointment with the service provider as quickly as possible.
</Note>

<Danger>
  **⚠️ Still sourcing? Not here.**

  If the SP has **not yet been assigned** and you're trying to get them to commit to an appointment during your sourcing call, this is not the right SOP. Go to [Job Aid: Job Needs a Service Provider](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-Job-Needs-a-Service-Provider#job-aid-job-needs-a-service-provider) or [Premium:  How to Assign a Preferred Service Provider](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/PREMIUM-How-to-Assign-a-Preferred-Service-Provider) and [(EMERGENCY) How to: Assign a Preferred + Latchel Network Service Provider](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/EMERGENCY-How-to-Assign-a-Preferred-Latchel-Network-Service-Provider) for more details.
</Danger>

#### For LCN service providers exhibiting observable behaviors

<Accordion title="What to do?">
  For LCN service providers exhibiting observable behaviors—such as missed appointments or unreasonable excuses—that result in scheduling delays, ensure to update their critical note to reflect the missed appointment or scenario encountered. Follow the format below, as it will be used in the future to determine whether we can continue offering jobs to the service provider.

  **Critical Note Format:**

  📌 _Date | WO# | Missed Appointment/Unreasonable Behavior_

  If they miss **three consecutive WO appointments or unacceptable behaviors**, their profile can be disabled. Associates are expected to check recent work orders to identify any recently completed WOs, which may indicate a successful appointment. Refer to the 3rd step in [this guide](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Navigate-Service-Provider-Profiles#how-to-navigate-service-provider-profiles) to disable a service provider’s account. Ensure to leave notes as well why they were disabled.
</Accordion>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

Emergency work orders typically do not have the status of "Scheduling with Service Provider," as these tasks are manually created based on the specific needs of the work order. Scheduling tasks usually arise from recent changes, such as a service provider being assigned by the Property Manager, an appointment being rescheduled, budget approval, or when maintenance is rescheduled after materials or parts are received. To ensure these scheduling requests are being addressed, a task is created and assigned to the troubleshooting team for action. Below are the steps the team can follow when scheduling with residents and service providers.

<Check>
  ### **Step 0: Pre-Check Before Scheduling**

  Before initiating any scheduling call or action:

  1. **Review all open tasks** on the work order to confirm no parallel scheduling, sourcing, or follow-up task is already in progress.
     - If another associate is actively working a related task, do not duplicate the effort. Add a note and move on.
     - If a conflicting task exists but appears stale with no update in 24+ hours, proceed with scheduling and note the conflict in the WO activity log.
  2. **Verify the current work order severity** is still accurate.
     - Is the current severity classification still accurate based on the resident's most recent information?
     - Has the resident provided new information that changes the severity?
     - If severity should change, follow the [Guidelines for Evaluating and Reassessing Work Order Information](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Properly-Diagnose-Emergency-Situations#guidelines-for-evaluating-and-reassessing-work-order-information "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2475851825") SOP to reclassify before proceeding with scheduling.

  **Why this matters:** Scheduling actions, especially PD/EH escalations, are severity-dependent. Starting with the wrong classification wastes time and may trigger unnecessary emergency escalations.
</Check>

### Step 1. **Calling the Service Provider and Resident**

1. **Check Resident Availability**: Verify if the resident has provided their availability in the Resident’s Schedule tab.
   1. Contact the Resident (if needed):
      1. If no availability is listed, or the “**Permission for Service Provider to Access Property”** toggle is enabled, but the access instructions are vague or unclear (e.g., “please knock on the door”), reach out to the resident to gather their preferred scheduling times and access details before proceeding.
         1. **If availability dates have already passed (stale data):**
            1. Do NOT schedule based on expired availability windows.
            2. Contact the resident to obtain **current** availability.
               1. Use the same outreach method as "no availability listed" (call → SMS if no answer).
               2. In your message, reference the previously listed dates: _"We had [dates] on file but those have passed — what works for you now?"_
            3. If the resident is unresponsive → follow Outcome 4 (Resident Unresponsive).
2. Call the Service Provider using the Latchel Console ( [<u>How to: Make Outbound Calls Within the Latchel Console</u>](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/TECH-101-How-to-Make-Outbound-Calls-Within-the-Latchel-Console#tech-101-how-to-make-outbound-calls-within-the-latchel-console "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/757235750")) and follow the provided script below, replacing the placeholder fields with specific details.
3. **📝** **NOTE:** Refer to [Informational: Basic Service Ops Guidelines and Rules](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Informational-Basic-Service-Ops-Guidelines-and-Rules#informational-basic-service-ops-guidelines-and-rules) on our policy for follow up attempts frequency.
   1. **Preferred**:
      1. **Say something like**: _Hello! This is (insert Associate Name), I'm calling on behalf of (insert PM Company Name). I am reaching out to you today because I see that we have a job assigned to you, but we still need to confirm an appointment with you. The resident has availability at (Date and Time from Resident availability list) Do any of those times work for you?_
   2. **LCN/LVN**:
      1. **Say something like**: _Hello! This is (insert Associate Name), I'm a maintenance coordinator calling on behalf of (insert PM Company Name). I am reaching out to you today because I see that we have a job assigned to you, but we still need to confirm an appointment with you. The resident has availability at (Date and Time from Resident availability list) Do any of those times work for you?_

#### OUTCOME 2: **Availability Mismatch Between Service Provider and Resident (Applies Vice Versa)**

1. Collect the Service Provider's availability.
2. Contact the resident and request additional availability based on the service provider’s schedule.
   1. If the resident is available during the updated service provider availability, schedule the appointment via the Work Order interface by following the steps outlined in[ **Outcome 1**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests)**.**
   2. If the Resident is **not available** during the newly updated Service Provider's availability, proceed as follows:
      1. If the service provider is locked in **or has already visited the property**:
         1. Escalate the work order to the Property Manager.
            1. **For property-damaging or extreme habitability situations,** refer to [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs#job-aid-pm-outbound-communication-for-emergency-wos)
               1. Manually trigger the "**Who Ya Gonna Call**" tool from the Action Menu. Associates should review the AI recommendation first, while also verifying the information against the original escalation instructions displayed below.
               2. Call the property manager**.** If the PM responds, follow their instructions and proceed accordingly.
               3. If there is no response from the escalation contacts, escalate to Operations Support Specialists and request them to move the **WO to Waiting for PM status** following approved scenario **8**, refer to [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#how-to-put-a-job-on-hold)
            - **For non-property-damaging or extreme habitability situations**
              1. Refer to the [<u>Escalating to the PM</u>](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#escalating-to-the-pm "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3308060699/Emergency+How+to+Handle+Scheduling+Requests#%F0%9F%97%93%EF%B8%8FGeneral-Scheduling-Rules") section and follow the subscription-based next action from the Basic Service Ops Guidelines. Do not use the old “leave as Pending + create PM task” path unless that is the applicable subscription-based action.
      2. If the service provider is **not** locked in **and has NOT already visited the property**:
         1. Follow the process outlined in [How to: Reassign a Service Provider](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Reassign-a-Service-Provider#how-to-reassign-a-service-provider)
3. Ask the resident if they have any new or alternative time slots available and update this information in the Latchel Console.
4. Proceed to [Step 3: Write Effective Notes](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Reassign-a-Service-Provider#step-4-write-effective-notes "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/edit-v2/3308060699?draftShareId=0a57cbfc-1cd0-44d0-b85a-e24e045fda28#Step-3%3A-Write-Effective-Notes").

**📝Note**: The same process applies if the resident’s availability is confirmed first, and the service provider cannot accommodate it. In all cases, attempt to reconcile availability from both parties before escalating or reassigning.

#### **<u>OUTCOME 3:</u>** **The Service Provider is Unresponsive or Cannot Commit to an Appointment** _(covers no answer / voicemail / won't commit)_

1. Check whether this SP is locked-in, PM-assigned, or has already visited the property.
2. **If NOT locked-in and has NOT visited:** advise the SP that this is an emergency work order that must be scheduled today. If they cannot commit to at least a placeholder window, let them know the job may need to be reassigned to another provider.
   - **If they give a placeholder window** → enter it per Outcome 1. Note in the WO that it's a placeholder pending confirmation.
   - **If they still won't commit to anything** → this call counts as the one attempt. Follow the reassignment process in [How to reassign a service provider](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Reassign-a-Service-Provider#how-to-reassign-a-service-provider).
3. **If locked-in, PM-assigned, or already visited:** advise the SP that this is an emergency work order that must be scheduled today. Since reassignment isn't an option here, let them know that without a commitment, we'll need to escalate to the Property Manager for direction.

   - **If they give a placeholder window** → enter it per Outcome 1. Note in the WO that it's a placeholder pending confirmation.
   - **If they still won't commit to anything** → this call counts as the one attempt.
     - **PD/EH:** Trigger WYGC, call the escalation contacts, and follow [this guide](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs#job-aid-pm-outbound-communication-for-emergency-wos). If there is no response, escalate to Operations Support Specialists and request the WO be moved to **Waiting for PM** following approved scenario **8**. Refer to [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#how-to-put-a-job-on-hold).
     - **Non-PD/EH:** Refer to the [Escalating to the PM / Handling Stale Emergencies](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#escalating-to-the-pm) section and follow the subscription-based next action from the Basic Service Ops Guidelines.
3. Ensure that a notification is sent to the current service provider to avoid them going out to the property (for reassignment cases).

- Proceed to [Step 3: Write Effective Notes](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#step-3-write-effective-notes "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/edit-v2/3308060699?draftShareId=0a57cbfc-1cd0-44d0-b85a-e24e045fda28#Step-3%3A-Write-Effective-Notes").

#### **OUTCOME 4.** The resident was unresponsive to the attempts to obtain availability or the service provider reported that the resident was not responsive.

1. Call the resident (in cases where the service provider has reported the resident as unresponsive).
   1. If the resident answers, gather their availability and proceed to schedule the appointment.
      1. Follow the necessary steps based on the outcome of the call.
   - If the resident remains unresponsive, document the outreach attempt and escalate to the PM for an unresponsive resident when we are attempting to set an appointment.
     1. **For property-damaging or extreme habitability (PD/EH) situations:** Do **not** de-escalate. Trigger **Who Ya Gonna Call**, call the escalation contacts, and if there is no response, escalate to Operations Support Specialists to move the WO to **Waiting for PM** following approved scenario **8**. Refer to [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold).
        1. **For non-property-damaging / non-extreme habitability (non-PD/EH) situations:** Refer to the [Escalating to the PM / Handling Stale Emergencies](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#escalating-to-the-pm) section and follow the subscription-based next action from the Basic Service Ops Guidelines.
   - Proceed to [Step 3: Write Effective Notes](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#step-3-write-effective-notes "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/edit-v2/3308060699?draftShareId=0a57cbfc-1cd0-44d0-b85a-e24e045fda28#Step-3%3A-Write-Effective-Notes").

**OUTCOME 5.** When the PM directs us to assign a vendor, then the PM schedules an appointment in the Latchel system.

1. Leave the scheduled appointment as is. No further scheduling action is needed.
2. **If the service provider reached out and requested to reschedule:** Proceed to schedule the work order following our emergency timeframes.
2. Proceed to [Step 3: Write Effective Notes](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#step-3-write-effective-notes "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/edit-v2/3308060699?draftShareId=0a57cbfc-1cd0-44d0-b85a-e24e045fda28#Step-3%3A-Write-Effective-Notes").

**OUTCOME 6.** When either the resident or the service provider notifies us that they have scheduled the appointment together internally (for rescheduling due to missed appointments, please refer to the appropriate [**guide**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-Solving-Appointment-or-Issue-Resolution-Disputes-Between-Resident-and-Service-Provider#job-aid-solving-appointment-or-issue-resolution-disputes-between-resident-and-service-provider))

1. If we have the complete appointment details (date and time):
   1. Enter the appointment information on the work order.
   2. Proceed to [Step 3: Write Effective Notes](#Step-3-Write-Effective-Notes).
2. If any appointment details are missing:
   1. Contact the service provider or resident to confirm the missing information.
   2. **If they provide the missing appointment details:** Enter the appointment information on the work order and proceed to Step 3.
   3. **If they are unresponsive:** Document the outreach attempt and follow the applicable scheduling escalation path.

      - **For property-damaging or extreme habitability (PD/EH) situations:** Do **not** de-escalate. Trigger **Who Ya Gonna Call**, call the escalation contacts, and if there is no response, escalate to Operations Support Specialists to move the WO to **Waiting for PM** following approved scenario **8**.
      - **For non-property-damaging / non-extreme habitability (non-PD/EH) situations:** Refer to the [Escalating to the PM / Handling Stale Emergencies](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#escalating-to-the-pm) section and follow the subscription-based next action from the Basic Service Ops Guidelines.
3. Proceed to [Step 3: Write Effective Notes](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#step-3-write-effective-notes).

### **Step 3: Write Effective Notes**

1. Write a Note in the existing Work Order, including the Assembly Ticket # (if available).
   1. The note should include the following information:
      1. Summarize the conversation.
      2. Where you left the message (i.e. voicemail, SMS, email, webchat).
      3. Any actions you took to change and assign a new Preferred Service Provider.
      4. If you have escalated, transferred, or deferred the task, summarize what led you to execute one of these actions.

## 💰Scheduling after Budget Approval

In many cases, Service Providers may require budget approval to proceed with additional work on a job. Once the budget is approved, a work order must be scheduled. Below are the guidelines to follow when scheduling after budget approval. These steps also apply if there is a note on the work order indicating that the Property Manager has already approved the estimate.

1. From the Existing Work Order Interface, change the numbers in the "Approved Budget" to reflect the requested amount

<Frame>
  ![](/images/SVC-942.png)
</Frame>

2. If the issue **is** property damaging or extreme habitability, an appointment should be scheduled for that **same day**.
   1. In case the resident or the service provider doesn’t have availability within this timeframe and permission to enter was not granted, proceed to contact the emergency escalation contacts.
      1. Refer to [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs)
   2. If no response from the escalation contacts, escalate to Operations Support Specialists (refer to [How to: Escalate Between Teams](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams) ) and request them to **move the WO to Waiting for PM status**.
3. If the issue is not property damaging or extreme habitability, an appointment should be scheduled for that **same day or the next day**.
   1. In case the resident or the service provider does not have availability within this timeframe and permission to enter was not granted, refer to the [Escalating to the PM / Handling Stale Emergencies](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#escalating-to-the-pm) section and follow the subscription-based next action from the Basic Service Ops Guidelines.
4. What If the Originally Assigned SP Is No Longer Available Post-Approval?
   1. After budget approval, if the originally assigned SP declines, is unresponsive per follow-up frequency, or states they cannot accommodate the timeline, first check whether there is any indication that the issue is unresolved. If the SP, resident, or both are unresponsive but there is **no indication the issue is unresolved**, move the work order to **Completed** status and document the unverified completion decision. If there is any indication the issue is unresolved, proceed with the guidance below:
      - **SP is NOT locked in:**
        1. Follow the reassignment process in [How to: Reassign a Service Provider](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#step-3-write-effective-notes).
        2. Note in the WO: _Original SP [name] unavailable after budget approval. Reassigning per SOP._
      - **SP IS locked/PM-assigned:**
        1. **PD/EH:** Trigger WYGC → Call EEC → If no response, escalate to Ops Support Specialists and request Waiting for PM status following approved scenario 8.
        - **ER (non-PD/EH):** Refer to the [<u>Escalating to the PM</u>](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#escalating-to-the-pm "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3308060699/Emergency+How+to+Handle+Scheduling+Requests#%F0%9F%97%93%EF%B8%8FGeneral-Scheduling-Rules") section and follow the subscription-based next action from the Basic Service Ops Guidelines. Include that budget was approved but [SP Name] is no longer available and PM direction is needed.
        1. Do not reassign without PM approval.
      - Proceed with scheduling the new SP per the standard process once reassignment or PM instruction is confirmed.

## 📅➡️📅Handling a multi-day work order

1. If a job is expected to take multiple days and the service provider has confirmed the start and completion date, create a single appointment window that covers the entire duration, up to a maximum of 7 days.
   1. **📝** NOTE: If the service provider is unable to provide an exact time, negotiate an estimated duration and set a placeholder appointment accordingly.

<Frame>
  ![image-20250409-231204.png](/images/SVC-943.png)
</Frame>

2. Let the Service Provider know they are expected to coordinate with the resident on a daily basis.
   1. **Say something like**: _“Hey [SP Name], we've set a multi-day appointment window to give you flexibility. Please reach out to the resident directly each day to confirm your arrival time. Let us know if anything changes so we can keep the job notes up to date!"_
3. Add a special note to the work order to indicate it’s a multi-day job, so any follow-up agents are aware. If the SP shares an update (delay, schedule change, etc.), log it in the notes so future agents are in the loop.

## 🗓️General Scheduling Rules

<Accordion title="Escalating to the PM">
  Refer to the [<u>General Rule: Recognizing and Handling a Stale Emergency</u>](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Informational-Basic-Service-Ops-Guidelines-and-Rules#general-rule-recognizing-and-handling-a-stale-emergency "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/776701198/Informational+Basic+Service+Ops+Guidelines+and+Rules#%E2%9A%A0%EF%B8%8F-General-Rule%3A-Recognizing-and-Handling-a-Stale-Emergency") in the Basic Service Ops Guidelines.

  1. **For property-damaging or extreme habitability (PD/EH) situations:**
     - **Do NOT de-escalate.** Severity must remain Emergency.
     - Manually trigger the **“Who Ya Gonna Call”** tool from the Action Menu. Associates should review the AI recommendation first while also verifying the information against the original escalation instructions.
     - Call the escalation contacts per [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs#job-aid-pm-outbound-communication-for-emergency-wos).
     - If escalation contacts are unresponsive, escalate to Operations Support Specialists and request that the WO be moved to **Waiting for PM** status following approved scenario **8**. Refer to [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#how-to-put-a-job-on-hold).
  2. **For non-property-damaging / non-extreme habitability (non-PD/EH) situations:**
     - Follow the **1 Attempt, Then Act** rule. If progress is blocked, follow the stale emergency guidance in the Basic Service Ops Guidelines and **de-escalate to Urgent** when appropriate.
     - Keep **“(EMERGENCY)”** in the work order title.
     - Add the mandatory internal note: _“De-escalated from Emergency to Urgent — [Reason]. Flagging for team visibility — reach out before re-escalating.”_
     - **Execute next steps based on subscription type:**
       - **Premium:** Create or reassign the task to the **Operations Associate** task group.
       - **Autopilot:** Keep the work order status on the correct state. Only create a task to PM if there are necessary updates.
       - **24/7 / After Hours:** Allow the system to auto-forward to PM, or click **“Forward to PM”** via the Service Ops Assistant plugin. If unavailable, escalate to Support to move the WO to **Waiting for PM**.
</Accordion>

For LVN-assigned service providers, refer to [this guide](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Handling-Work-Orders-Assigned-to-LVN-Service-Providers#handling-work-orders-assigned-to-lvn-service-providers).

        2. **For non-property-damaging / non-extreme habitability (non-PD/EH) situations:** refer to the [Escalating to the PM / Handling Stale Emergencies](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#escalating-to-the-pm) section and follow the subscription-based next action from the Basic Service Ops Guidelines.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Reassessing Work Order Priority During Scheduling">
  In some cases, the **severity of a work order** may change during the scheduling process. This can occur in scenarios such as:

  - **Temperature updates** indicating that the situation is no longer an emergency.
  - **Additional information provided** by the resident that alters the initial assessment.

  In such situations, it is essential to follow the steps outlined in the guide on [**Evaluating Resident-Provided Information for Issue Resolution**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Properly-Diagnose-Emergency-Situations).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The service provider has a critical note not to call them for work order follow-ups and only contact them via email. What should I do? (Applicable for PM-assigned and non-PM-assigned work orders)">
  **During Dispatch**

  - ✅ You **may call** the service provider to dispatch the work order. The “Do not call” note applies **only to follow-ups**, not initial dispatch.

  **During Follow-Ups**

  - Follow the service provider’s critical note. Send the required email and/or SMS immediately and document the outreach attempt in the WO notes. This counts as the follow-up attempt for this edge case.
  - Do not leave the work order sitting in an active emergency path solely while waiting for a slower email/SMS response. Take the next action based on severity and unresolved indicators.

  **1. Property-Damaging / Extreme Habitability (PD/EH) Situations**

  - Do **not** de-escalate. The WO must remain Emergency.
  - Send the email/SMS to the service provider per the critical note and document the attempt.
  - Trigger **Who Ya Gonna Call**, call the escalation contacts, and follow [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs#job-aid-pm-outbound-communication-for-emergency-wos).
  - If escalation contacts are unresponsive, escalate to Operations Support Specialists and request that the WO be moved to **Waiting for PM** following approved scenario **8**. Refer to [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#how-to-put-a-job-on-hold).

  **2. Non-Property-Damaging / Non-Extreme Habitability (non-PD/EH) Situations**

  - Send the email/SMS to the service provider per the critical note and document the attempt.
  - **If the SP is not locked in and has NOT visited the property:** follow the reassignment process in [How to: Reassign a Service Provider](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Reassign-a-Service-Provider#how-to-reassign-a-service-provider).
  - **If the SP is PM-assigned, locked in, or has already visited the property:** refer to the [Escalating to the PM / Handling Stale Emergencies](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#escalating-to-the-pm) section and follow the subscription-based next action from the Basic Service Ops Guidelines.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What should I do if a preferred vendor or a PM-assigned vendor accepts a non-PD/EH emergency work order and schedules the job for a later date (e.g., 2–3 days out)?">
  If the preferred vendor accepts the job via public screens or is appointed by the Property Manager and schedules their own appointment, **no further action is required** from our end. There is **no need to contact the tenant or create a task for the PM**. We should only step in if **tenant concerns** or other issues are reported.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How should we handle situations where there is enough time to contact service providers, but the remaining time window does not reasonably allow them to arrive within the resident’s availability?">
  Answer: First, attempt to **reconcile the availability between the resident and the service provider**. Assess whether a service provider can **realistically arrive and complete the visit within the resident’s available timeframe**. If the remaining window is too short to allow for a reasonable arrival, follow the guidance below.

  1. **For property-damaging or extreme habitability (PD/EH) situations:** same-day scheduling should always be prioritized. If the next availability does not align, do **not** de-escalate. Trigger **Who Ya Gonna Call**, call the escalation contacts, and follow [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs#job-aid-pm-outbound-communication-for-emergency-wos). If there is no response from escalation contacts, escalate to Operations Support Specialists and request that the WO be moved to **Waiting for PM** following approved scenario **8**. Refer to [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#how-to-put-a-job-on-hold).
  2. **For non-property-damaging / non-extreme habitability (non-PD/EH) situations:** refer to the [Escalating to the PM / Handling Stale Emergencies](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#escalating-to-the-pm) section and follow the subscription-based next action from the Basic Service Ops Guidelines. Do not use the old “leave as Pending + create PM task” path unless that is the applicable subscription-based action.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="PM directs us to assign a preferred vendor and instructs us to let the SP schedule directly with the resident (applies to ER and PD/EH issues)">
  This applies when, during PM outreach for an ER or PD/EH work order, the PM:

  - Instructs us to assign the work order to one of their preferred vendors, **and**
  - Explicitly advises that the SP should coordinate the appointment directly with the resident.

  **Process:** Because the PM has taken ownership of SP/resident scheduling coordination, Latchel should assign the preferred vendor, document the instruction, and move/forward the work order to **Waiting for PM** using the applicable PM-routing path.

  1. **Assign the WO to the PM’s specified preferred vendor.**
     - Update the assignment in the system and ensure the SP receives the work order details through the appropriate dispatch channel.
     - Add a clear WO note documenting the PM’s instruction, including the PM’s name, date/time of the instruction, the named vendor, and the direction that the SP will schedule directly with the resident.
  2. **Move/forward the WO to Waiting for PM.**
     - **PD/EH:** Do **not** de-escalate. Keep the WO as Emergency and follow the approved Waiting for PM / PM escalation path. If needed, escalate to Operations Support Specialists to move the WO to **Waiting for PM** following approved scenario **8**. Refer to [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#how-to-put-a-job-on-hold).
     - **ER non-PD/EH:** Follow the subscription-based next action in the [Escalating to the PM / Handling Stale Emergencies](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#escalating-to-the-pm) section and the [General Rule: Recognizing and Handling a Stale Emergency](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Informational-Basic-Service-Ops-Guidelines-and-Rules#general-rule-recognizing-and-handling-a-stale-emergency) in the Basic Service Ops Guidelines.
  3. **Do not keep an ongoing Troubleshooter scheduling follow-up open.**
     - Once the PM-directed vendor is assigned and the WO is routed to Waiting for PM / PM ownership, Latchel is no longer actively coordinating the appointment.
     - Re-engage only if the resident reports a concern, the SP contacts us for help, or the PM provides additional instructions.
  4. Proceed to [Step 3: Write Effective Notes](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#step-3-write-effective-notes).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What should I do if a PM-assigned service provider's earliest availability is outside the 24-hour emergency timeframe (e.g., 2–3+ days out)?">
  This applies when the PM has assigned a service provider to an emergency work order, and during the scheduling call the SP confirms their earliest availability is **more than 24 hours out** — exceeding the emergency timeframe.

  1. **Confirm and document the SP's earliest availability.**
     1. Note the SP's stated soonest date/time in the work order.
  2. **Attempt to reconcile availability.**
     1. If the resident has provided availability, check whether any of the resident's windows fall within the 24-hour emergency timeframe. If the SP cannot accommodate any of them, proceed to Step 3.
     2. If the resident has not provided availability (or was unresponsive), note this and proceed to Step 3.
  3. **Escalate to the Property Manager based on severity:**
     1. **For property-damaging or extreme habitability (PD/EH) situations:**
        1. Call the emergency escalation contacts immediately. Refer to [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs#job-aid-pm-outbound-communication-for-emergency-wos).
        2. Inform the PM that the assigned SP's soonest availability is outside the 24-hour emergency window, and that the property damage/extreme habitability condition remains active and unaddressed during that gap. Request instructions — e.g., whether to wait for the SP, reassign to another provider, or take alternative action.
        3. If the PM responds, follow their instructions and proceed accordingly.
        4. If escalation contacts are unresponsive, escalate to Operations Support Specialists and request them to move the WO to **Waiting for PM** status following approved scenario **8**. Refer to [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#how-to-put-a-job-on-hold).
     2. For non-property-damaging or extreme habitability situations: 
        1. Schedule the appointment for the SP's proposed date/time directly — do not wait for PM instruction before booking it, since the PM already chose this provider.
        2. Follow [General Scheduling Rules](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#escalating-to-the-pm) to de-escalate the severity to Urgent, keep "(EMERGENCY)" in the work order title, and execute the appropriate next step for the account's subscription type (Premium/Autopilot/24-7).
        3. Add a note documenting: the PM-assigned SP's earliest availability was outside the 24-hour emergency window, the appointment was booked for [DATE/TIME], and the severity was de-escalated to Urgent as a result.
        4. Set expectations with the SP about non-emergency rates per [How to: Set expectations with Service Providers when dispatching](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Set-expectations-with-Service-Providers-when-dispatching#how-to-set-expectations-with-service-providers-when-dispatching).
  4. **Set rate expectations.**

  - Regardless of severity, since the appointment will fall outside the 24-hour emergency window, inform the SP that **standard (non-emergency) rates and budgets will apply**. Follow the guidelines in [How to: Set expectations with Service Providers when dispatching](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Set-expectations-with-Service-Providers-when-dispatching#how-to-set-expectations-with-service-providers-when-dispatching).

  5. **Proceed to** [(Emergency) How to Handle Scheduling Requests](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests#emergency-how-to-handle-scheduling-requests)**.**

  📝 **NOTE:** Do **not** reassign a PM-assigned/locked-in SP without PM approval. The PM chose this provider for a reason — always get their input before making changes. If the SP is **not** locked in, follow the reassignment process in [How to: Reassign a Service Provider](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Reassign-a-Service-Provider#how-to-reassign-a-service-provider).
</Accordion>
