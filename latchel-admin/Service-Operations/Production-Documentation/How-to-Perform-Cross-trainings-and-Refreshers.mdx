<Note>
**Purpose of this document**

Provide a clear guide for supervisors to perform self-service cross-training and refresher sessions.

**Who should read this document?**

Service Ops Supervisors
</Note>

## Process Overview

Every cross-training and refresher program follows the same five steps. The associate should complete each step in order to be considered trained on the queue.

**SOP Reading → Shadowing → Reverse Shadowing → Practice → Final Quiz**

<Info>
  While the Coassemble courses are being updated, cross-training and refreshers use one combined program per queue. Associates new to a queue complete all five steps. Associates who were trained on the queue before complete the shorter refresher track. The two programs will be separated again once updated training courses or videos are available.
</Info>

Once an associate begins their program, they should not interrupt it to perform any other task. Exception: when leadership calls an [All Hands on Deck](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Ops-Policy-All-Hands-on-Deck) because queues are overloaded, training and cross-training are paused for every associate past their first 21 days at Latchel, and shadowing sessions stop. Only new hires in their first 21 days continue training. Resume the program once the All Hands on Deck is lifted.

The supervisor should make sure that associates meet the minimum time for each step while grouping the trainee with experienced, top-performing associates. The supervisor can allow more time if necessary, but should not reduce the minimum times.

To begin a program, the supervisor goes to the **#training** Slack channel and uses the **"Cross-Training Request"** workflow to advise the team. Supervisors track each associate's progress in the **"Training Report Spreadsheet"**.

## FAQ

<Accordion title="How do I set up a shadowing session?">

Shadowing sessions should be divided into time blocks that allow sufficient time to perform several activities and respond to questions while making sure we don't overload shadow partners. The recommended time windows should be 2 or 4-hour time blocks. A trainee with a full day of shadowing should ideally have two 4-hr sessions, two 2-hr sessions + one 4-hr session, four 2-hr sessions, etc. to cover their 8-hour shift. A shadow partner should not exceed 4 hours per day of shadowing/reverse shadowing sessions.

There are 4 main steps to coordinating a successful shadowing plan for your trainee:

### Step 1: Coordinate availability

Follow the coordination guidelines in the **Job Aid: Shadowing Phases & Expectations** SOP to identify potential shadow partners making sure they meet all the necessary requirements and align with your trainee's schedule.

### Step 2: Communicate and confirm the session with shadow partners

Once you've identified your potential shadow partners, message the associates letting them know you have considered them for the shadowing session and are confirming their awareness.

<Frame>
  ![Step 2 - Confirm shadow partners](/images/TC-1.png)
</Frame>

Feel free to communicate however you prefer with shadow partners but always make sure they know the time window, trainee, and shadowing phase for the session.

Shadowing phases and expectations for shadowing partners and trainees are documented in the **Job Aid: Shadowing Phases & Expectations** SOP. Share this with associates if they are unsure of their responsibilities.

### Step 3: Lock in the session in Google Calendar

<Frame>
  ![Step 3 - Lock in session in Google Calendar](/images/TC-2.png)
</Frame>

### Step 4: Communicate with Operations Support Specialists

Once you have your session locked in, make sure Operations Support Specialists are aware and can make adjustments to the rest of the team if needed. Make sure you notify Operations Support Specialists on the day the session is happening; if the session you're setting up is for a future day, you can schedule a Slack message for that day.

To inform Operations Support Specialists of the session, simply send a message in the **#operations\_support** Slack channel advising them of the people involved, type of shadowing session, and time.

<Frame>
  ![Step 4 - Communicate with Ops Support Specialists](/images/TC-3.png)
</Frame>

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I track an associate's cross-training progress?">

The main key to setting up a successful program is making sure we communicate everything the associate needs to do, where they need to be (SOP reading, in a shadowing session, etc.) and the time frames to complete each activity.

Follow the steps below to track compliance:

### Set up your trainee

Go to the **#training** Slack channel and head over to the workflows. Click on the **"Cross-Training Request"** workflow and fill out the information required. (Make sure you follow the correct format for the date.)

<Frame>
  ![Cross-Training workflow in Slack](/images/TC-4.gif)
</Frame>

Once you submit your request, you will get a message confirming your submission and linking you to the Cross-Training SOP.

<Frame>
  ![Cross-Training submission confirmation](/images/TC-5.png)
</Frame>

You've now successfully added your associate to the cross-training list! You will find an empty checkbox for each one of the stages of cross-training. These will help you monitor the trainee's progress. For the trainee to appear in the "Cross-training requests" tab they need to have all checkboxes completed. As the trainee progresses to the next stage in their program you should check the boxes they've completed already.

</Accordion>

## Activity Roster

Cross-trained associates are agents that have been with Latchel and know the type of work that is required from them. Because of this, we do not expect cross-trainees to have major difficulties or behavioral issues. If the supervisors suspect something is not working properly for their associate or something needs to be changed they should communicate this immediately to the Service Ops Trainer and work with them to find the best solution.

All associates who have completed a program should use the **"Cross-Training and Refresher Updates"** workflow to submit a daily summary for their supervisors, including any requests for additional help on specific topics. This workflow is available in the **#svc-ops-requests** channel. Supervisors should review responses for their associates each day in the Training Report Spreadsheet under the "Cross-Training and Refresher Updates" tab.
