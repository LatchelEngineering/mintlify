## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
  We want to prevent delays, missed follow-ups, and miscommunication when work orders are awaiting Property Manager (PM) action. The goal is to ensure timely updates, avoid unresolved maintenance issues, and maintain clear accountability between Latchel, residents, and PMs—ultimately reducing risk, improving customer satisfaction, and ensuring that no work order is left unaddressed due to lack of PM response.
</Info>

<Info>
  **What are WOs Awaiting PM Action?**

  These are work orders that either:

  - Have a status of **“Forwarded to PM”** or **“Waiting for PM”**,**“Needs Approval”**, **or**
  - Have **a status where we are awaiting the Property Manager’s input, approval, or response before we can proceed**, including work orders with open tasks assigned to the Property Manager.

  **Note: For scenarios where a Non-emergency WO in Needs Approval status (due to initial intake) escalated to an emergency, please follow the guidelines outlined in** [How to: Properly Diagnose Emergency Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Properly-Diagnose-Emergency-Situations#how-to-properly-diagnose-emergency-situations).
</Info>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### **📤The WO was “Forwarded to PM” (also applicable to “Waiting for PM” status) because the resident(s) was unresponsive to our troubleshooting attempts.**

1. If the resident contacts Latchel to follow up on the missed troubleshooting attempts:
   1. Proceed to call back the resident to troubleshoot.
      1. Attempt up to 3 times to get in contact.
         1. New issues
            1. If the resident responds, proceed to Step B.
            2. If the resident remains unresponsive after multiple attempts, there is no need to create a task—leave the work order as is.
         2. Recall WOs
            1. Refer to [How to: Identify and Handle a Recall WO](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Identify-and-Handle-a-Recall-WO#what-is-a-recall)
   2. Based on the call outcome, reassess the work order and proceed with the appropriate next steps:
      1. To evaluate and reassess the situation during the return call, refer to the guidelines [How to: Properly Diagnose Emergency Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Properly-Diagnose-Emergency-Situations#how-to-properly-diagnose-emergency-situations)
      2. **📝** **NOTE**: _Update the work order description based on the Intake Settings._
      3. **‼️IMPORTANT:** The step above is not applicable if the PM is on an after-hours subscription and it’s between 9:00 am-5:00 pm Monday through Friday Property time.

#### **A. The service provider, resident or owner wants a general update/reached out about the WO.**

_Applicable when someone reaches out for a status update **with no new information** or **changes in the resident’s situation**. In this case, simply **create a task for the PM**._

_For **PD/EH** situations, always review the [SOP **Key Reminders**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs#key-reminders). #### **B. The severity of the issue changes (or new information is received)**

If new information from a resident, Service Provider, or owner changes the urgency of the work order, you must act based on the new severity immediately. Do not simply create a task and wait for a PM response.

1. **If it escalates to an Emergency:**
   - **The "No-Task" Rule:** Never create a task to ask a PM if they "want Latchel to handle" an active emergency.
   - **Immediate Action (Check Ownership & Status):**
     - **If PM is Active:** Use the **Forward to PM** tool immediately to stop the Latchel clock.
     - **If PM is NOT Active, act based on the current status:**
       - **Finding SP / No SP:** Immediately begin sourcing a Service Provider for **today**.
       - **Scheduling / Pending (SP assigned):** Call the SP immediately (1-Attempt). If they cannot attend **today**, reassign immediately.
       - **Awaiting Estimate Approval / Needs Approval:**
         - _PD/EH:_ Call escalation contacts for immediate approval.
         - _Non-PD/EH:_ Forward to PM immediately to stop the clock.
       - **Work in Progress:** Call the SP to confirm same-day completion.
   - _Why:_ Leaving an emergency in an active status while waiting for a PM's "permission" or "approval" creates a **Bad Status** (stale job).
2. **If it de-escalates (e.g., Emergency to Normal):**

   - **Update Severity Immediately:** Change the severity in the system to reflect the new, non-emergency status.
   - **Decide on the Follow-up:**
     - **No Task Needed:** If the de-escalation is straightforward (e.g., the resident fixed it themselves) and the work order can now follow standard automation, **do not create a task.**
     - **Task Required:** Only create a task for the PM if the de-escalation requires a specific **decision** (e.g., asking if they want to use a preferred vendor now that the emergency is over).
   - **Note the Change:** Always add a note to the WO: _"Issue de-escalated to Normal per resident/SP. No longer an emergency."_
3. **Reclassification & Title:** When updating severity, ensure the title is updated correctly. If it remains an emergency but is being forwarded, keep **"(EMERGENCY)"** in the title to maintain vendor urgency.

#### **C. Vendor Calls Back After Work Order Is Forwarded to PM (or while PM is Active)**

If a previously unresponsive vendor calls back asking if the job is still available, and you have determined the PM is actively managing the work order:

1. **Do Not Assign the Vendor:** Do not move the work order back to an active status (Scheduling/Pending) unless the PM explicitly instructs you to take the job back.
2. **Notify the PM (Facilitate Communication):**

   - **If the WO is NOT yet in "Forwarded" or "Waiting for PM" status:** Use the **Forward to PM** tool immediately. This stops the Latchel clock and notifies the PM that the vendor is now reaching out.
   - **If the WO IS already in "Forwarded" or "Waiting for PM" status:** Create a task for the PM User Group titled: _"Vendor Callback: [SP Name] is now available."_
3. **Advise the Vendor:** Inform the Service Provider that the Property Manager is managing the coordination and that we are notifying the PM of their availability now.
4. **Note the Callback:** Add a note to the work order: _"Vendor [Name] called back. Notified PM via [Forwarding/Task]. Advised SP that PM will reach out if they wish to proceed."_

#### D. Resident Non-Maintenance Inquiries

1. A non-maintenance request would be a request that is not related to a Work Order.
   1. Refer to [How to: Intake Non-Resident Caller Requests (Non-maintenance Requests)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Intake-Non-Resident-Requests-Non-maintenance-Requests#how-to-handle-a-non-resident-caller)

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

Break this SOP in the following situations:

- The **resident’s request or behavior violates Latchel policies** (e.g., abusive behavior, illegal requests). Follow Latchel’s escalation and reporting protocol instead of proceeding with normal task creation or callbacks.
- The **property is on an after-hours subscription** and the situation occurs **during business hours (9:00 AM–5:00 PM PT, M–F)** — do not follow this SOP; instead, defer to the PM per standard after-hours subscription guidance → [Informational: Latchel Customers & Customer Subscriptions](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Latchel-Library/Informational-Latchel-Customers-Customer-Subscriptions#informational-latchel-customers-and-customer-subscriptions)
- The issue has already been **escalated via a different SOP** (e.g., emergency escalation protocols, special handling for high-risk properties).
