## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
This process ensures that work order statuses accurately reflect the current state of the repair. For emergencies, it is critical that a job does not remain in an active status (like "Confirming Completion") if the repair is not actually finished or if we cannot verify its status. This prevents "stale" work orders and ensures that unresolved issues are addressed immediately.
</Note>

<Note>
### What are Work Orders with "Confirming Completion" Status?

Work orders enter the **Confirming Completion** status automatically once the scheduled appointment window has passed. This status serves as a checkpoint to verify if the Service Provider attended the home and performed the requested work.

**How Verification Happens:**<br />Residents or Service Providers can confirm or deny completion through:

- The automated Latchel Service Provider Interface.
- Text message (most popular).
- Email or phone call.

**Attention:** Before you begin working on any task, **ensure you view all open tasks for the customer** (resident, service provider, owner) to avoid duplicating efforts and to ensure the issue is handled correctly the first time.
</Note>

# Content

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### **What are Work Orders with "Confirming Completion" Status?**

Work orders enter this status automatically once the scheduled appointment window has passed. This is the system’s way of asking, _"The time is up—did the work actually get done?"_

**🛑 The Status Accuracy Rule:**<br />For emergency work orders, you must ensure the status accurately reflects the state of the repair. Do not allow a work order to remain in "Confirming Completion" if you cannot verify that the issue is resolved.

- **If the repair is verified:** Move to the next step to close the work order.
- **If the repair is NOT verified:** You must immediately update the status to reflect the current reality. This means either moving it back to an active repair status (e.g., Scheduling) or moving it to the Property Manager (e.g., Waiting for PM).

**Key Reminder:**<br />A work order in "Confirming Completion" is technically "stale" if the appointment has passed and the emergency is still active. Your goal is to resolve this status as soon as the task appears in your console

### Step 2: Read the Work Order

1. Before contacting anyone, check if the resolution has already been confirmed through other channels to avoid duplicating efforts.
   1. **Check for Existing Confirmations:**
      - Review the **Work Order Notes** for updates from other agents.
      - Check **Text Messages** from the resident for "Yes" or "Done" responses.
   2. **Evaluate the Findings:**
      - **If Resolution is Confirmed:** Click **Action** > **The job is complete** and request the invoice from the Service Provider.
      - **If Resolution is NOT Confirmed:**
        - **Emergency Rule:** You must proceed immediately to contact the resident. Do not wait for the system to prompt you again. If the notes show the appointment was missed, you must secure a same-day resolution or move the ball to the PM.
        - **Non-Emergency Rule:** Proceed to the next step (Outreach) as prompted by your console tasks. You may rely on standard automated follow-ups if the resident has not yet responded.

### Step 3 - **Confirming Completion**

For Emergency work orders, we use a high-velocity verification process. You are allowed **one attempt** to reach the Service Provider and **one attempt** to reach the Resident today to verify the status of the repair.

1. **Call the Resident.**
   - **If they confirm COMPLETE:** Click **Action** > **The job is complete** and request the invoice.
   - **If they say UNRESOLVED:** Immediately proceed to **Step 4** to investigate with the Service Provider.
   - **If they are UNRESPONSIVE:** Leave a voicemail and proceed immediately to **Step 4**.
2. **Call the Service Provider.**
   - **If they confirm COMPLETE:** Click **Action** > **The job is complete** and request the invoice.
   - **If they confirm UNRESOLVED:** Investigate the reason (e.g., missing parts, scope change). If they cannot finish **today**, move the work order to **Waiting for PM**.
   - **If they are UNRESPONSIVE:** Do not leave the job in "Confirming Completion."
  1. **The "Unverified Emergency" Action (Both Unresponsive):**
     - **🛑 Emergency Rule:** If you have attempted to reach both the SP and the Resident and neither has responded to verify the repair:
     - **Action:** Use the **"Forward to PM"** tool immediately.
     - **Why:** This ensures the work order does not sit in a "stale" status. By forwarding, you are notifying the Property Manager that the appointment has passed but the resolution could not be verified.
    1. **The "Blocked Coordination" Exception:**
       - If you are **blocked** from verifying the job (e.g., a critical note forbids contact or you are waiting on a budget approval):
       - **Action:** Use the **"Forward to PM"** tool immediately to transfer ownership of the next step to the PM.

## [**❓**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders)**Frequently Asked Questions**

<Accordion title="How should we handle situations where the service provider who completes the work is different from the one currently assigned to the work order?">
1. **Update the Assignment Immediately:**
   - **If the provider is in our system:** Reassign the work order to them.
   - **If the provider is NOT in our system:** You must use **"Resident Resolved"** as the closure method. This is a system workaround that allows you to close the job properly when no assignable provider exists.
  1. **Verify and Close (High-Velocity Rule):**
     - If you have the new provider on the phone, confirm completion and mark the job as **Complete** immediately.
     - If you cannot reach the new provider, follow the **1-Attempt Rule**: Call the resident. If the resident is also unresponsive and there are no indications the job is unresolved, move the work order to **Complete** (Assume Done).
2. **Document the Switch:**
   - Add a note stating:
     - Who actually completed the work.
     - Why the assignment was changed (or why "Resident Resolved" was used).
     - How completion was verified.
  1. **Notify the Property Manager:**
     - Create a task for the PM to ensure they are aware of the provider switch for billing and warranty purposes.
</Accordion>

## **🎯 When to Break This SOP**

You may **deviate** from the SOP only in specific, documented scenarios. Here’s when deviation is allowed:

- **Unresponsive Emergency:** If you cannot confirm completion for an Emergency after one attempt to the SP and one attempt to the Resident today, do not wait. You **must** break the standard follow-up cycle and move to **Complete** (Assume Done) or **Forward to PM** immediately.
- **Property Damage/Extreme Habitability:** If the issue involves active property damage or extreme habitability (PD/EH), do **not** move to "Complete" based on silence. You must follow the [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs#-general-pm-outbound-script) and continue calling escalation contacts until mitigation is confirmed.
- **PM Ownership:** If the PM has explicitly taken ownership of the job (e.g., assigned their own SP or requested to handle completion), skip standard follow-ups and use the **"Forward to PM"** tool immediately.
- **Safety Risks:** If at any point you identify a 911 situation or a severe safety risk, stop this SOP and follow the [How to: Handle 911-Severity Type Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-911-Severity-Type-Work-Orders#step-1-when-the-resident-should-call-911).
