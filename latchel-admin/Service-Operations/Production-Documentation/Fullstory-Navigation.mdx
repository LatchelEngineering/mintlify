As Product Specialists who are dedicated to comprehending both the what and why of every situation, it's important that we step into the shoes of our system users. This is not just limited to our agents but also includes service providers, tenants, and property managers. While listening to their phone call conversations or reading notes on work orders provides valuable insights, there are occasions when these methods may fall short of truly understanding a particular event. This is precisely where FullStory emerges as a pivotal tool. 

## **Benefits of FullStory**

-   FullStory grants us a unique perspective by allowing us to see precisely what users see when they perform actions or engage in conversations, thereby providing a comprehensive understanding of their experiences and actions within our system.
    
-   FullStory enables us to step into the users’ shoes by recording and replaying their actions. This offers a firsthand view of what the user encountered, including the screens they visited, the buttons clicked, and their navigation within the application. By watching user sessions, we can identify patterns and behaviors, pinpoint areas for improvement, and facilitate further investigation.
    

## **Accessing Screen Recordings**

These steps will guide you in efficiently locating the screen recordings you require. To access screen recordings, you can follow these initial steps:

1.  Click on the "Home" button to access Quick Actions.
    
2.  Within the Quick Actions menu, locate and click on the "Search for sessions" option.
    
3.  A search bar will appear; here, you can input specific information about the session you're looking for.

To perform more advanced and refined searches in FullStory, you can utilize both User Filters and Event Filters simultaneously. Meaning, we can make use of User Identification and Engagement Search Filters at the same time!

For example, if you need to find sessions from a particular agent for a work order that was handled by multiple agents, using these filters in conjunction allows you to achieve a more precise and tailored set of session results. This approach enhances your ability to extract specific insights and information from the recorded sessions efficiently.

### **Session Playlist**

Below the Filters Tab, you'll find the Session Playlist. Depending on your filters, it provides sessions based on your criteria, including user, date and time, session count, device used, and location.

![x4w3SutMuRatKf8uHeDZ8swYsO6ca4a1-5tQ1E5JHqd86HC9DWUH_gvyh-4q0966AG8ulb6K8xzQaXoMkMCQHmrsq6-0d2O14FUYa6ew1viVuXKBf_SRKOvhLOoU7eRxpslyOe62ArDDQWm8OWtRwK4](/images/SVC-990.png)

For the example above, you'll find a list of seven sessions from user Matthew Stroman. In the photo below, the exact dates and times when Matthew accessed the work order will show on the right side of the screen. To review the actions taken by Matthew during those specific sessions, you can simply click on the play button associated with each session. This action will initiate the playback of the screen recording, allowing you to observe the sequence of actions and interactions that occurred on that particular day and time.

![CaXnHIAYCxzkgnPo3_xfeiKrZca0SAxKP36vQbEwxIt7am-XSe2WiFQdfwZYQrffE1btqBqPUGuaUfpJYPvdH1WMXiN3QpBAVRb5jOZ62DM7fhNwDiYzZ5Y4JF2eOCzxD5mvb2IxZdyj1tjj0jR1ELc](/images/SVC-989.png)

Upon clicking the play button, you'll enter a session view that offers a range of controls for your convenience. Here's an overview of the available controls:

1.  **Tab and URL:** This shows how many tabs the user has open and the URL of the tab in display
    
2.  **Play and Pause:** These buttons allow you to start or pause the playback of the session, giving you full control over when to review the actions.
    
3.  **Note and Share:** This allows copying the session's URL for reference or sharing with colleagues. Also allows you to add notes or annotations to specific points in the session, making it easier to highlight important details or issues.
    
4.  **Control Speed:** You can adjust the playback speed to view the session at a faster or slower pace, depending on your preference.
    
5.  **Skipping Inactivity:** This feature allows you to skip idle sessions to save time enabling you to focus on specific moments or actions. However, this is useful if one needs to measure the length of time being inactive. 
    
6.  **User Details:** It shows the user’s name, time of session, device used, location, and IP Address
    
7.  **Closed Tabs:** You can choose to include or exclude inactivity periods and tabs that were closed during the session, tailoring your viewing experience.
