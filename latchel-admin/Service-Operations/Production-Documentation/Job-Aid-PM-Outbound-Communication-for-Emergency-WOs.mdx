## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  _We aim to ensure timely and effective communication with Property Managers during emergency work orders. The goal is to provide clear and immediate updates to prevent miscommunication and delays, ensuring swift resolution of critical issues and maintaining property safety and resident satisfaction._
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Overriding Rule: Severe Damage & 911 Situations**
</Warning>

<Accordion title="Steps in handling Severe Damage Escalation/911 Situations">
  To ensure immediate escalation and communication in cases of severe structural damage reported by residents or identified through work order descriptions, follow the below guidelines.

  <Warning>
    **Attention:**

    This is not only applicable to emergency work orders. It must be followed anytime severe structural damage is identified, regardless of the work order type or timing. Escalation must occur even if the damage is not the primary reason for the resident’s report. Protecting the asset and minimizing risks are the top priorities.
  </Warning>

  **Identification of Severe Damage:**

  1. Assess for severe structural damage at any point in the workflow, whether during troubleshooting, dispatch, or review of work orders.
     1. ![Download 2026 10 14T232308 811](/images/download---2026-10-14T232308.811.png "Download 2026 10 14T232308 811")
     2. Severe damage poses significant risks to the property, residents, or surrounding environment. Examples include but are not limited to:
        1. **Major water leaks** (Rapidly spreading leaks that threaten structural integrity or habitability. Water intrusion affecting multiple units or critical infrastructure, such as electrical systems.)
        2. **Fires** (Any fire damage compromising the structural safety of a building.)
        3. **Uninhabitable units**:
           1. Situations rendering a unit unsafe for occupancy, such as:
              1. **Powerlines causing unsafe conditions:** Electrical hazards like live wires near or within the property that risk injury or fire
              2. **Ground-level breaches compromising security:** _Structural damage (e.g., a collapsed wall or broken door) creating direct outside access, exposing the property to safety or theft risks._
              3. **Vehicles colliding with properties:** _Impact causing damage to walls, foundation, or utilities, leaving the unit unsecure or unsafe._
  2. **Escalation to Property Manager:**

     1. Call the Property Manager escalation contact immediately.
        1. **If you were able to reach an Escalation Contact**, you will take action based on what the Escalation Contact asked you to do.
        2. If the PM is unavailable, leave a detailed voicemail explaining the situation.
           1. **Voicemail script for _all but the last person on the Escalation Contacts list_**: _Hi, [Escalation Contact name]! This is [your name] calling from Latchel regarding WO number [provide number] at [property address, including the unit #]. I’m reaching out to notify you about a situation involving severe damage at this property. Specifically, [provide a brief description of the issue, e.g., a major water leak, fire damage, or structural breach]. We’ve attempted to reach you and your escalation contacts but have not received a response yet. We will place the work order into “[Forwarded to PM/Waiting for PM]” status for your input. Please don’t hesitate to send us a Webchat or text us back at (206)-900-9466 if we can assist you with this or any other work orders. Have a nice [day/evening]!_
           2. **Use the Forward to PM tool if it is available.** Otherwise, escalate to Operations Support Specialists in Slack. If escalation to Operations Support Specialists is needed, let them know we need more information from the PM to move forward. **_If you use the Forward to PM tool and no email is sent to the PM_** about the WO being forwarded, manually email the PM using the generic template below. Whether you complete the task through the tool or Operations Support Specialists complete it, use the following:
           3. **Select a reason (destination status) for putting this job on hold:** Waiting for PM
              1. **Describe why you moved the job to this status:** Provide a brief description of what you need from the PM. Ex: _There is severe structural damage reported (e.g., a major water leak), and we need confirmation from you to proceed with the necessary actions on this work order._
              2. **Who is this task about?** PM
              3. **Set a date and time for the follow-up task:** [leave blank]
              4. **Task priority:** Highest
              5. **User group\*:** Choose the user group with “PM” in the title
              6. **Assign to a User:** [leave blank]
              7. **When should this cancel?** Cancel on any state change
              8. **What action or information is required from the Property Manager?:** Provide a brief description of what you need from the PM. Ex: _We need your approval or guidance to address the severe damage reported in WO number [provide number]. Please confirm the next steps or let us know how to proceed._

  4. **Document the Escalation:**
     1. Ensure to link the call to the applicable work order. Refer to [How to: Link Calls to a WO](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Link-Calls-to-a-WO#how-to-link-calls-to-a-wo).
        1. Include relevant details such as:
        2. Date and time of the call
        3. Summary of the reported damage
        4. Actions taken
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**🔑**Key Reminders">
  - Latchel Troubleshooters will **only** call a PM Company via Escalation Contacts in the scenarios outlined in this Job Aid. If the scenario does not fit one of the listed prompts, contact Operations Support Specialists for guidance.
  - Make **one attempt per emergency escalation contact** in sequential order whenever one of the scenarios below occurs. Refer to the WYGC section below for when to use the tool versus calling manually.
  - **General Rule:** If you already called about the same reason and there are no new updates or changes, do **not** call again.
  - Always tag escalation calls to the related work order. For details, refer to [How to: Link Calls to a WO](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Link-Calls-to-a-WO#how-to-link-calls-to-a-wo).
  - **Always provide complete property details** when calling, leaving voicemails, sending SMS, or sending Webchats. Include the **full property address with unit number**, **WO number**, issue summary, what you have done, why the issue is PD/EH, and what you need from the PM.
  - **No After-Hours Contact:** If EEC or critical notes explicitly state there are no after-hours contacts for a PD/EH issue, do not attempt EEC calls. Move the WO to the appropriate hold status, submit a [PS ticket](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support) for configuration review, and document why calls were not attempted.
  - **SMS-Sourced Work Orders:** SMS-sourced WOs follow the same EEC escalation rules as any other intake channel. Do not treat an SMS-sourced PD/EH WO differently when determining whether to call EECs.
  - **PM tells you to assign a specific SP:** Before ending the call, ask their preference and set expectations: "_If [SP Name] doesn't answer, would you like us to call you back, or do you trust them to handle it from here? Just so you know, if we can't reach [SP Name] and we're also unable to reach you on the call back, we'll move the work order to Urgent on our end, but we'll keep 'EMERGENCY' in the title so [SP Name] still treats it as an emergency. We'll also create a task for you on the work order with the details._"
    - Document their answer, and that expectations were set, in the WO notes, and make sure the PM knows if the issue is property-damaging or extreme habitability. If that SP later becomes unresponsive, see exception B of the **Stale Emergency rule** in [<u>Informational: Basic Service Ops Guidelines and Rules</u>](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Informational-Basic-Service-Ops-Guidelines-and-Rules "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/776701198").
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="🔧 Understanding the Who Ya Gonna Call (WYGC) Tool">
  <Note>
    The **Who Ya Gonna Call (WYGC)** tool pulls the data from the **Emergency Escalation Contacts (EEC) list** configured in the system. When a step says to call escalation contacts, WYGC should detect who to call and the order to call them in based on that EEC list.

    **Use WYGC when:**

    - The scenario or guided workflow tells you to call escalation contacts.
    - Critical notes, pre-dispatch notes, or other instructions say to call **Emergency Escalation Contacts**, **EEC**, or the **full EEC list**.

    **Call manually instead of using WYGC when:**

    - Notes say to call the **specific** people.
    - Notes provide a contact order or sequence that may be different from the configured EEC list.

    For workflows where there is a guided flow to determine whether an issue is **PD/EH** — for example, when completing TS prompts — **WYGC automatically populates** with the configured EEC list once the flow reaches the escalation contact step. In this job aid, that applies to **Scenarios 1–3**. For **Scenarios 4–6**, WYGC does **not** auto-populate; manually access WYGC from the **Action Menu** if the scenario is tied to a work order. If there is no work order tied to the scenario, access WYGC from the **PM Company** or **Property** page.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="📞 General PM Outbound Script">

  ### Scenario 2: Validate Unclear Situations for a [**Property Damaging or Extreme Habitability Issue**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Identify-Property-Damage-and-Extreme-Habitability-Situations) & [**911 Situations**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-911-Severity-Type-Work-Orders)

  <Warning>
    📝 **NOTE — SMS-Originated Unclear Situations:**<br />If a resident describes an issue via text that _could_ be PD/EH but you're not certain based on the text description alone (e.g., "there's water on the floor" without clarity on whether it's an active leak or a spill), this qualifies as an unclear situation requiring PM input. Follow Scenario 2 — do not default to treating it as non-PD/EH simply because the information came via text and is incomplete. When in doubt, escalate as PD/EH and let the PM make the determination.
  </Warning>

  <Note>
    If access information is needed before dispatch can proceed and only the PM can provide or authorize it, select “PM Input Needed Before Dispatch” and follow the applicable PD/EH or Non-PD/EH process. This applies to both common-area and unit-specific access issues. 
  </Note>

  This scenario will use the same guided workflow for 2 types of escalations:

  1. Validate Unclear Situations for a Property Damaging or Extreme Habitability Issue
     1. Unclear situations are ones for which we need the Escalation Contacts’ input to handle a property damaging or extreme habitability issue. This means **_the only person who can answer the question blocking us from moving the WO forward is the PM_**. Some examples include:
        1. During the troubleshooting call, it was mentioned that the property manager/owner/resident would take care of the issue or have already sent a Technician/Vendor/Service Provider to the site.
        2. If there’s a no watery issue and the resident doesn’t have the utility company information and can’t contact them or refuses to do so, refer to [this guide](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-a-Utility-Issue) for details.
        3. Contradicting information between the intake setting and the PM’s critical notes.
        4. A new tenant was provided a lockbox code that is not working, or access credentials (key, fob, code) do not work and the PM is the only party who can provide or authorize correct access.
        5. Access information is needed to proceed with dispatch for a confirmed emergency — whether for a common-area entry device or an individual unit lockout (e.g., no lockbox code available, building entry authorization required, no vendor access instructions). Use this path when the PM is the only party who can unblock dispatch by providing access details.
        6. Inaccessible boiler room or utility room, such cases are common when dealing with buildings.
        7. The resident is not available to let a Service Provider in and refuses to give permission to enter.
        8. There is a leak coming from a different unit and we need to confirm if that unit is part of the PM company
  2. 911 situations
     1. Learn more about handling 911 situations [here](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-911-Severity-Type-Work-Orders).

## **Part 2: If Escalation Contacts Don't Respond**

<Info>
  Use this section any time you've made a required EEC/escalation call and haven't gotten a response. This applies no matter which job aid told you to make the call.
</Info>

<Warning>
  **Important:** Any time an EEC or escalation contact call is required and the contact is unresponsive, Latchel cannot move the work order forward without PM instruction. This applies whether the call was required by a scenario in this job aid or by another SOP.

  - This applies to **both** the first contact and **any follow-up contact** about the same scenario.
  - A follow-up SMS in these scenarios is **only** to remind or notify the PM. It does **not** give you permission to go ahead without their response.
  - If the escalation contacts are unresponsive and you've already sent an SMS:
    - If the WO is already forwarded or in Waiting for PM status, create a follow-up task assigned to the PM.
    - If it isn't, use the Forward to PM tool, or if it isn’t available, escalate to Operations Support Specialists to move the WO to Forwarded to PM/Waiting for PM. If you use the Forward to PM tool and no email is sent to the PM, manually email the PM using the **Generic Manual Email Template for Forwarded WOs** above.
    - Refer to [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#how-to-put-a-job-on-hold).
  - **Exception:** if exception B of the **Stale Emergency rule** in [Informational: Basic Service Ops Guidelines and Rules](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Informational-Basic-Service-Ops-Guidelines-and-Rules) applies, do not request Forwarded to PM/Waiting for PM. Follow exception B instead.
</Warning>

For **all other concerns when contacting escalation contacts**, follow the guidelines below after **unsuccessful call attempts**. These are situations where the core SOP allows Latchel to move forward without PM input.

1. **First, check the WO notes, call transcripts, SMS/webchat/email, and critical notes.**

2. **Then follow the branch that applies:**

**A. If the PM Has Taken Over or Is Actively Managing the Work Order**

Treat the WO as PM-managed if **either** of these is true:

1. **The PM has taken over (exception A):** At any point, the PM explicitly said they will handle the work order from now on, or asked us to forward it. See exception A of the **Stale Emergency rule** in [Informational: Basic Service Ops Guidelines and Rules](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Informational-Basic-Service-Ops-Guidelines-and-Rules).
2. **The PM is actively managing the WO:** There is evidence of PM activity from **today or yesterday (property time)** showing they are taking control. Examples:
   - A call, email or message says the PM is handling the request.
   - A coordination note says the PM is taking ownership (for example, "We've assigned this to ABC Plumbing, scheduled Thursday 3 PM").
   - The PM is handling it outside Latchel (for example, their own vendor).
   - The PM asked Latchel to hold off while they investigate.
   - The PM created a task for themselves or the PM group (for example, "PM to ask owner for approval").

<Warning>
  _A PM assigning or requesting a specific Service Provider does **not** count as PM-managed on its own. Follow the “PM assigned a specific SP, and that SP is unresponsive or can’t schedule in time” item under **Follow Up Procedures** in [<u>SOP: Work Order Follow-ups and Updates</u>](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/SOP-Work-Order-Follow-ups-and-Updates "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2511372328")._

  **Act immediately.** Do not wait for an SMS reply before transferring ownership.

  If the issue is **not** PD/EH (for example, you called EEC only because an EEC or pre-dispatch note required it), PM activity alone does not mean forwarding. Follow path 2 of the **Stale Emergency rule**, or exception A if the PM explicitly took over.
</Warning>

For PD/EH issues, the work order stays **Emergency** and keeps "(EMERGENCY)" in the title. Do not de-escalate to Urgent.

1. Use the **Forward to PM** tool immediately to move the work order to Forwarded to PM/Waiting for PM. This stops the SLA clock and transfers ownership. If no email is sent to the PM after using the tool, manually email the PM using the **Generic Manual Email Template for Forwarded WOs** above.
2. Send an SMS to every escalation contact who has SMS enabled, confirming the work order has been transferred to the PM.
   1. _Say something like: "Hi [Escalation Contact name], this is [your name] from Latchel. We're reaching out regarding WO #[number] at [property address, including unit #] for a [brief WO description] issue. We attempted to reach all escalation contacts on file by phone but were unable to connect. Since your team is handling this work order, we've forwarded it to you, and the work order is now in [Forwarded to PM / Waiting for PM] status. Please contact us via Webchat or text us at (206)-900-9466 if we can assist further."_
3. If the Forward to PM option is not available, escalate to Operations Support Specialists to move the WO to Forwarded to PM/Waiting for PM.
4. In the work order notes, document the SMS, the ownership transfer, and what made the WO PM-managed (the explicit takeover, or the PM activity you found and its date).

**B. If the PM Has NOT Taken Over and Is NOT Actively Managing the Work Order**

Use this branch when any of these is true:

- There is no PM activity on the WO.
- The most recent PM activity is **older than yesterday (property time)** and the PM never explicitly said they would take over.
- The only PM activity is assigning or requesting a specific Service Provider. Follow the “PM assigned a specific SP…” item under **Follow Up Procedures** in [<u>SOP: Work Order Follow-ups and Updates</u>](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/SOP-Work-Order-Follow-ups-and-Updates "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2511372328"). If that item sent you here to call the escalation contacts and they don’t respond, do not remove the PM-assigned SP. Follow the **Important** section at the top of Part 2 and request Forwarded to PM/Waiting for PM.

In any of these cases, Latchel keeps the WO moving, unless the SP bullet above sends you to the **Important** section.

1. **If No Response from Escalation Contacts:**
   1. Send an SMS to every escalation contact who has SMS enabled.
      1. Tell them that because they didn't respond, you'll move on to the next phase of the emergency work order process.
         1. _Say something like: "Hi [Escalation Contact name], this is [your name] from Latchel. We're reaching out regarding WO #[number] at [property address, including unit #] for a [brief WO description] issue. We attempted to reach all escalation contacts on file by phone but were unable to connect. [Briefly state what's been done so far, e.g., "We've dispatched a service provider from our network to address the issue" / "The resident was unresponsive, so we proceeded with dispatching"]. [If PD/EH: The issue is considered a property damaging/extreme habitability situation.] Please contact us via Webchat or text us at (206)-900-9466 if you have any questions."_
2. Document the SMS in the work order notes.
3. **Proceed to the Next Phase**
   1. Follow the SOP for the next escalation step.
      1. For **dispatching** a work order, refer to [(EMERGENCY) How to: Assign a Preferred + Latchel Network Service Provider](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/EMERGENCY-How-to-Assign-a-Preferred-Latchel-Network-Service-Provider)
      2. For **setting expectations during dispatch**, refer to [How to: Set expectations with Service Providers when dispatching](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Set-expectations-with-Service-Providers-when-dispatching)
      3. For **scheduling** a work order, refer to [(Emergency) How to Handle Scheduling Requests](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests)
      4. For **follow-up procedures**, refer to [SOP: Work Order Follow-ups and Updates](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2511372328)

 

## [❓](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders)Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Q: What should we do if a Work Order (WO) was updated by a PM who is not part of the EEC list, but we later need to contact the EEC team (e.g., when the assigned SP cannot schedule a PD/EH WO, or an SP mentions they already spoke with a specific PM)? Should we contact that PM even if they are not on the EEC list?">
  A: We should always follow the official EEC contact list when reaching out to the EEC team, regardless of which PM last updated the WO.<br />The only exception is if a PM explicitly instructs us to contact them directly or to reach out to a different PM/EEC.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: What do I do if someone (resident, fire department, police, or other emergency services) reaches out about a 911 or PD/EH situation, but the PM/property management company is disabled or inactive (no subscription)?">
  Resident safety always comes first — even without an active subscription.

  1. **Determine the severity of the situation:**
     1. **If the issue is PD/EH only (not 911):** Route the customer to their Property Manager.
     2. **If the issue is a 911 emergency:** Route the customer to call 911 immediately.
  2. **Customer Script:** 
     1. 🗨️ **For PD/EH issues (not 911), say something like:** _"I understand you're dealing with an urgent situation. Since your property management company is currently inactive in our system, we're unable to create a work order at this time. I'd recommend reaching out to your Property Manager directly for assistance. In the meantime, we will send a notification to your Property Manager to make them aware of the situation."_
     2. 🗨️ **For 911 emergencies, say something like:** _"Your safety is our top priority. Please call 911 immediately for emergency assistance. Since your property management company is currently inactive in our system, we're unable to create a work order, but we will send a notification to your Property Manager to make them aware of the emergency."_
  3. **Send a courtesy email to the PM** using the **Failed Escalation Contacts** email under the Emergency escalation contacts tab.
     1. Refer to [Job Aid: Critical, Pre-dispatch, Warranty, Special Notes & Emergency Escalation Contacts](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-Critical-Pre-dispatch-Warranty-Special-Notes-Emergency-Escalation-Contacts)
  4. **Document the interaction** (call notes, email sent) so there's a record of what was reported and what action was taken.

  **Email Template**

  **Subject:** Urgent: (911 Emergency / PD/EH Issue) Reported at (Property Address) — Action Required

  **Body:**

  Hi (Property Management Company)! This is (Associate Name) from Latchel. We received (a call from the fire department / a report from the resident / a call from emergency services / a report regarding a property damage or environmental hazard issue) regarding (brief description of the emergency — e.g., an active fire / a gas leak / a police-related incident / property damage / environmental hazard) at (Property Address).

  (If 911: We advised (the resident / emergency services) to contact 911, and (emergency services are on-site / have been dispatched / 911 has already responded).)

  (If PD/EH only: We advised the resident to contact you directly regarding this property damage/environmental hazard issue.)

  Please note that your account is currently inactive in our system, so we are unable to process a work order for this property. However, we wanted to ensure you are aware of the situation so you can take any necessary steps to follow up.

  If you have any questions or would like to reactivate your account with Latchel, please feel free to reach out to us.

  Best regards,<br />(Associate Name)<br />Latchel Support Team

  📝 **NOTE:** No subscription = no work order can be created, but resident safety and PM awareness still take priority. For 911 emergencies, do NOT delay routing the resident or assisting emergency services while attempting to reach a disabled PM. For PD/EH issues, ensure the customer is directed to their PM and that a notification is sent.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: The EEC/PM is requesting information via SMS, but they do not have a profile in the platform. What should I do?">
  **A:** We are unable to send SMS or Webchat messages to contacts who are not registered users in the platform. Do **not** create temporary profiles as a workaround.

  1. Politely let the EEC/PM know that they are not currently set up as a user in our system, so we are unable to text or message them through the platform. **Say something like:** _“I’d be happy to send that over, but it looks like you’re not currently set up in our system, so I’m unable to text you through our platform. I can provide the information verbally right now or send it via email — whichever works best for you.”_
  2. Offer an alternative way to relay the requested information:
     1. Provide the information verbally while you are on the call.
     2. Send the information via email.
  3. Submit a [PS ticket](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support) to have the EEC/PM added as a user so they can receive platform communications going forward.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: I reached an EEC but they said the issue isn't something they handle and routed me to another EEC on the list who was previously unresponsive. What do I do?">
  Follow the **unresponsive EEC steps** outlined in this SOP.

  1. Document what the reached EEC confirmed (that the issue is outside their scope and who they said handles it).
  2. If the responsible EEC was **already contacted and unresponsive** — skip the repeat call/voicemail/SMS because those steps were already completed. Follow the **Important** section in Part 2: if the WO is not already forwarded or in Waiting for PM status, use the Forward to PM tool, or escalate to Operations Support Specialists if the tool is not available, to move the WO to Forwarded to PM/Waiting for PM.
  3. If the responsible EEC has **not yet been contacted** — proceed with the full outbound communication steps in this SOP (call, voicemail, SMS). If still unresponsive, follow the **Important** section in Part 2.
</Accordion>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

<Note>
  You can only break or make exceptions to this SOP in very limited and specific scenarios. The SOP is strict: Latchel Troubleshooters are to call a PM Company (via their Escalation Contacts) **only** in the scenarios outlined in the Job Aid. If you encounter a situation that does **not** fit any of the listed scenarios or prompts, you **must contact Operations Support Specialists for guidance**—do not proceed on your own
</Note>

- If strictly following the SOP may lead to confusion or delay in the Work Order that could result in a negative experience for the resident or client, especially in urgent scenarios, associates are permitted to use discretion and act accordingly.
  - Be sure to document the reason for the deviation and notify the appropriate lead or team for visibility.
- Regardless of **Work Order severity** (emergency or non-emergency)—or even if there is no WO related—if a reported issue can be clearly defined as **property-damaging** or an **extreme habitability concern**, you **must call the PM**. This applies even if the specific scenario is not listed in the Job Aid. The only exemption is FAQ 2.
  - Use sound judgment based on the potential **impact to property** or **resident safety**, and ensure the **appropriate lead or team is notified** for visibility.
- **On-call customers:** This SOP does not apply. For On-call customers, always refer to the separate instructions provided in the [<u>linked documentation</u>](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3132325894 "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3132325894").
- **Do not break the SOP on your own judgment.** If your scenario is not covered, escalate to Operations Support Specialists.
