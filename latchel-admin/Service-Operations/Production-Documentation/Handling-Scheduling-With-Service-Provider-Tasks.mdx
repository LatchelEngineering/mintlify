## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to prevent delays, duplicate outreach, and miscommunication when scheduling appointments with service providers. The goal is to ensure every work order in "Scheduling With Service Provider" status is handled efficiently and consistently—so appointments are confirmed quickly, all parties are kept informed, and customers experience smooth, frustration-free service from Latchel.
</Note>

<Note>
  **What are Work Orders with "Scheduling Service Provider Status"**

  Those are work orders that are waiting on a vendor to schedule the maintenance appointment after they were assigned to the Work Order by the Property Manager or Latchel. The purpose of this SOP is to guide Latchel Operators around when and how to handle Work Orders in the "Scheduling with Service Provider" status.
</Note>

<Warning>
  If you encounter one of the following scenarios, please leave a note on this page and transfer the work order _to the Property Manager_. [(Operations) How to: Escalate Between Teams](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams#how-to-escalate-between-teams).

  This may include:

  - If a property has permission to access but no access instructions or is vacant.
  - If the Service Provider says that they'll contact the resident directly and refuses to update Latchel.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  Attention: For agents handling tasks for both emergencies and non-emergencies.

  Before you begin working on any task, ensure you view all open tasks for the customer you are following up on (resident, service provider, owner). This step is crucial to avoid duplicating efforts and customers complaints, and to ensure we always get it right the first time.

  For detailed instructions on how to handle the tasks queue and view all open tasks, please refer to the following link: [How to: Work the Tasks Queue](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Work-the-Tasks-Queue#how-to-work-the-tasks-queue).

  For information on the frequency and number of times you should contact customers, please refer to [Informational: Basic Service Ops Guidelines and Rules](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Work-the-Tasks-Queue).
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **LVN Service Provider**

  For guidance on managing unresponsive LVN service providers or on acceptable scenarios for reassigning an LVN service provider (such as PM requests or missed appointments), refer to [Handling Work Orders Assigned to LVN Service Providers](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Handling-Work-Orders-Assigned-to-LVN-Service-Providers#unresponsive-lvn-service-providers-active-work-orders).
</Warning>

<Warning>
  For LCN service providers exhibiting observable behaviors—such as missed appointments or unreasonable excuses—that result in scheduling delays, ensure to update their critical note to reflect the missed appointment or scenario encountered. Follow the format below, as it will be used in the future to determine whether we can continue offering jobs to the service provider.

  **Critical Note Format:**

  📌 _Date | WO# | Missed Appointment/Unreasonable Behavior_

  If they miss **three consecutive WO appointments or unacceptable behaviors**, their profile can be disabled. Associates are expected to check recent work orders to identify any recently completed WOs, which may indicate a successful appointment. Refer to the 3rd step in [How to: Navigate Service Provider Profiles](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Navigate-Service-Provider-Profiles#how-to-navigate-service-provider-profiles) to disable a service provider’s account. Ensure to leave notes as well why they were disabled.
</Warning>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### **Step 1: About the "Scheduling Service Provider" status**

<Note>
  **⚠️ Confirm the Correct Work Order Before Scheduling**

  Before reaching out to the resident/service provider to schedule, verify that the task is linked to the correct Work Order. Scheduling against the wrong WO can lead to a service provider being dispatched to the wrong property or for the wrong issue.
</Note>

1. The Service Provider Work Queue populates with a Work Order whose status is: "Scheduling Service Provider"
   1. **_What does it mean?_** Prior to this stage of the Work Order, the resident has given their time availability and the job has been assigned to a service provider.
      1. The Latchel Console system automatically moves the work order into "Scheduling Service Provider".
   2. When a Work Order is in this status, Operations Associates have the following responsibilities:
      1. Checking the progress with contacting the Service Provider by reading notes on the Work Order.
      2. Making a phone call to the Service Provider via our Latchel Console.
         1. Keep in mind that some service providers may have critical notes with special instructions outlining how they prefer to interact with Latchel.
      3. Depending on the scenario, executing one of the following:
         1. Escalating the Work Order to the Property Manager
         2. Sourcing/Reassignment
         4. Moving the Work Order status forward by scheduling an appointment.
2. Open the Work Order
   1. Proceed to Step 2 below

### **Step 2: Read the Work Order**

1. Read the Work Order and check to see if there is availability from the resident. You can see this by clicking on the residents' profile and selecting the "Schedule" tab.
   1. If there is no additional Resident availability, return the work order to "Rescheduling with Resident" status. You can do this through the following steps:
      1. From the Action button, select "Reschedule This Job".
      2. The system will then ask the resident to update their availability.
   2. If there is more Resident availability, proceed to check the notes to see **if a contact attempt** has already been made to the assigned Service Provider.
      1. **📝 NOTE:** For Emergency Work Orders, contact attempts are determined by severity:
         - **Property Damage (PD) / Extreme Habitability (EH):** Follow the **Triple Dial Procedure** (3 attempts). These must remain as Emergencies.
         - **Non-PD/EH Emergencies:** Capped at **1 attempt**. If unsuccessful or if a callback is requested, apply the **Same-Day Decision Guide** (Intervene, De-escalate, or Forward) immediately. Do not defer to the next day.
2. Review the Work Order notes to see if another Latchel Associate has already tried to contact this Service Provider to schedule the appointment.
   1. If Latchel has contacted this Service Provider **0x** (for Non-PD/EH) or **0x-2x** (for PD/EH), please proceed to <u>Step 3</u>.
      1. ⚠️ **Before removing the SP or de-escalating, first confirm whether the SP is locked in or has already visited the property.** If either is true, **do not remove** — proceed to Step 4, section 2.a (Apply Same-Day Decision Guide). If neither is true, proceed to Step 4, section 2.b (Reassign/Intervene).
   2. If Latchel has contacted this Service Provider **1x** (for Non-PD/EH) or **3x** (for PD/EH), please proceed to <u>Step 4</u>.
      1. **For Non-PD/EH:** Do not defer to the next day. Apply the **Same-Day Decision Guide** (Intervene, De-escalate to Urgent, or Forward to PM) immediately.
      2. **For PD/EH:** Do not de-escalate. You must follow the **Emergency Escalation Contact** protocol (Triple Dial + calling PM emergency lines).
      3. **📝 NOTE:** If removing a Service Provider from a Non-PD/EH job after 1 attempt, ensure you leave the mandatory de-escalation or intervention note for team visibility.

### **Step 3: Calling the Service Provider and Resident**

Call the Service Provider **one time (1x)** for Non-PD/EH emergencies (or **3x** for PD/EH) using the Latchel Console ( [↔ How to: Make Outbound Calls Within the Latchel Console](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/TECH-101-How-to-Make-Outbound-Calls-Within-the-Latchel-Console#tech-101-how-to-make-outbound-calls-within-the-latchel-console)) and follow the script below (replacing the fields in purple with specific information).

- **📝 NOTE** Always attempt to contact the service provider first via phone call. **For Non-PD/EH emergencies, if the phone call is unsuccessful, do not wait. Send an SMS/Email immediately and then apply the Same-Day Decision Guide.** Exceptions include cases where they don't have a valid phone number, it's outside of 9:00 am-5:00 pm, or if there are special communication instructions in the critical notes.

<Tip>
  **Service Provider Call Script**

  **Preferred**

  _Hello! This is **(insert Associate Name)**, I'm calling from Latchel on behalf of **(insert PM Company Name)**. I am reaching out to you today because I see that we have a job assigned to you, but we still need to confirm an appointment with you. The resident has availability at (Date and Time from Resident availability list) Do any of those times work for you?_

  **LCN/LVN**

  _Hello! This is (**insert Associate Name**), a Maintenance Coordinator calling on behalf of **(insert PM Company Name)**. I am reaching out to you today because I see that we have a job assigned to you, but we still need to confirm an appointment with you. The resident has availability at (Date and Time from Resident availability list) Do any of those times work for you?_
</Tip>

From the call, you will encounter 1 of the 6 outcomes below.

#### **<u>OUTCOME 1</u>**: If the Service Provider answers the phone call and he/she agrees to set an appointment in the windows of availability, then:

1. Select "Action, and Select from Resident Availability". Then say:

<Tip>
  **Service Provider Confirmed Schedule Script**

  I have assigned the work order to you. In the event the scheduled appointment is missed, we will only attempt to contact you <u>once</u> before reassigning.
</Tip>

2. Select the agreed upon window and then select continue
3. In the notes section, explain why you took this action, i.e. "per phone call with vendor confirmed scheduled appointment"
   1. **_Why?_** This removes this Work Order from the queue and moves it into the " Awaiting for Work Date" status. Please note that when this process happens, this will cancel out the deferred task and the task will not return to the "Scheduling" task queue.
4. Proceed to writing an Effective Note in [Step 5](#).

#### <u>OUTCOME 2:</u> If the Service Provider answers the phone call and he/she cannot schedule within the resident's availability, then:

1. Follow the script below:

<Tip>
  **Service Provider Additional Openings Script**

  Ok, I understand, may I get your openings for the next couple of days to pass along to the resident? We can't guarantee that they will accept it, but I can try. Please note that if the resident cannot adjust their availability, then the job will be reassigned to another provider without notice. Thank you!
</Tip>

2. Collect the Service Provider's availability.
3. Then contact the resident and ask for further availability.
   1. If the Resident is **<u>available</u>** for the newly updated Service Provider's availability, then schedule the appointment with the Resident. You can do this from the Work Order interface by:
      1. Clicking the secondary action button.
      2. Choose "Set a New Confirmed Work Date".
      3. Follow system prompts until you successfully schedule the new Work Date and Time Window.
   2. If the Resident is **<u>not available</u>** during the newly updated Service Provider's availability, then:
      1. Reassign to another Service Provider (proceed to Step 4)
      2. Ask the Resident, if they have any new available time slots. Update this new time slot information in the Latchel Console.
   3. Proceed to writing an Effective Note in [Step 5](#).

#### **<u>OUTCOME 3:</u>** If the Service Provider answers, but says they cannot talk now and will call back.

1. **Action based on severity:**
   1. **Non-PD/EH Emergencies:** Do not defer to the next business day. Inform the SP that we need a commitment now to ensure resident safety. If they cannot talk, send the SMS/Email and **apply the Same-Day Decision Guide (Intervene, De-escalate, or Forward) immediately.**
   2. **PD/EH:** Do not defer to the next business day. Continue to follow the **Triple Dial Procedure** and call the PM's emergency escalation contacts until a vendor is secured.

#### **<u>OUTCOME 4</u>**<u>:</u> If the Service Provider does not answer the phone call, then:

1. **Leave a voicemail and send an SMS/Email immediately.**
   - Choose the script that matches the action you are about to take in **Step 4**:

#### **Option A: If Reassigning (Latchel-Assigned/Not Locked In)**

> **SMS/Email Template:**<br />_"This is a reminder from Latchel regarding the emergency job at **(insert Property Address)**. Because we were unable to reach you to confirm a same-day appointment, **we are now reassigning this job to another provider** to ensure the resident is taken care of immediately. If you are still available, please call us back at **(insert phone number)** right away, but please note the job may no longer be assigned to you. Thank you."_

#### **Option B: If Escalating to PM (PM-Assigned / Locked-In / Visited)**

> **SMS/Email Template:**<br />_"This is a reminder from Latchel regarding the emergency job at **(insert Property Address)**. Because we were unable to reach you to confirm a same-day appointment, **we are now escalating this request to the Property Manager for further instructions**. Please call us back at **(insert phone number)** immediately if you are still able to attend today so we can update the Property Manager. Thank you."_

2. **Action based on severity (Do not defer to the next day):**

- **Non-PD/EH Emergencies:** Do not defer. Proceed to **Step 4** to either **Reassign** or **De-escalate** internally.
- **Property Damage (PD) / Extreme Habitability (EH):** Do not de-escalate. Follow the **Emergency Escalation Contact** protocol (Triple Dial + PM emergency lines).

#### **OUTCOME 5**: If the Service Provider says the job has already been completed, then:

1. Educate the Service Provider about their personalized dashboard.
   1. If the Service Provider has critical feedback, please escalate to the Service Provider Manager.
   2. [How to: Navigate Service Provider Profiles](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Navigate-Service-Provider-Profiles)
2. Make a note in the work order and say the following to the Service Provider:

<Tip>
  **Service Provider Job Completion Confirmation**

  Thank you for informing me! I will update the work order accordingly.
</Tip>

3. Select "Mark this Job as Complete" under the "Action" button.
4. Select "Request Invoice from Vendor"
5. Locate the task on the Work Order and close the task
6. Proceed to writing an Effective Note in [Step 5.](#)

### **Step 4: Execute the Same-Day Decision**

If the Service Provider is unresponsive or cannot confirm an appointment after the required attempts (**1x for Non-PD/EH** or **3x for PD/EH**), apply the **[Basic Service Ops Guidelines / Same-Day Decision Guide]** to determine the final action:

1. **Attempt Reassignment**
   1. Rule: This step only applies to Latchel-Assigned (Auto-Assign/LCN) vendors who have NOT visited the property.
   2. Action: Remove the current SP and manually call the next preferred or LCN vendor to get a same-day commitment.
2. Escalate (For Locked-in, PM-Assigned, or Visited)
   1.   Rule: If the SP is Locked-in, PM-Assigned, or has Already Visited the property, you are not allowed to remove them. You must move to escalation instead.
   2. Action for Non-PD/EH Emergencies:
      1. Forward to PM: Use the "Forward to PM" feature in the console.
      2. Escalation Safeguard: If the "Forward to PM" option is not available, you must escalate the task to Ops Support Specialists to move the Work Order forward.
      3. Manual Email: If the console doesn't show an auto-email was sent, send a manual email to the PM to ensure they are notified.
      4. De-escalate: Change the priority to Urgent (keep the Emergency tag) and move the WO to "Waiting for PM" status.
   3. Action for PD/EH (Property Damage/Extreme Habitability):
      1. Call PM: Initiate the Emergency Escalation Contact (EEC) protocol.
      2. Unresponsive PM: If the PM is unresponsive, follow the Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for hold status instructions. Do not de-escalate PD/EH.

### **Step 5: Write Effective Notes**

1. **Write a Note in the existing Work Order**, including the Assembly Ticket # (if available).
2. **The note must be detailed and actionable, including:**

   - **Summary of Contact:** Who you spoke with or where you left the message (Voicemail, SMS, Email, Webchat).
   - **Assignment Source Verification:** Explicitly state if the SP was **Latchel-Assigned** or **PM-Assigned/Locked-in**.
   - **Action Taken:**
     - If reassigned: Name of the new Service Provider and the commitment time secured.
     - If de-escalated: State that the priority was changed to **Urgent** and why (e.g., "No other vendors available" or "Locked-in vendor unresponsive").
   - **Escalation Details:**
     - If **Forward to PM** was used, note if the manual email was sent.
     - If escalated to **Ops Support Specialists**, include the reason (e.g., "Forward to PM option unavailable").
     - For PD/EH: Note the outcome of the **EEC calls** and any instructions from the **PM Outbound Communication** job aid.

### Handling a multi-day work order

1. If a job is expected to take multiple days and the service provider has confirmed the start and completion date, create a single appointment window that covers the entire duration, up to a maximum of 7 days.
   1. 📝 NOTE: If the service provider is unable to provide an exact time, negotiate an estimated duration and set a placeholder appointment accordingly.
      <Frame>
        ![image-20250409-231204.png](/images/SVC-500.png)
      </Frame>
2. Let the Service Provider know they are expected to coordinate with the resident on a daily basis.
   1. **Say something like**: _“Hey \[SP Name\], we've set a multi-day appointment window to give you flexibility. Please reach out to the resident directly each day to confirm your arrival time. Let us know if anything changes so we can keep the job notes up to date!"_
3. Add a special note to the work order to indicate it’s a multi-day job, so any follow-up agents are aware. If the SP shares an update (delay, schedule change, etc.), log it in the notes so future agents are in the loop.

## FAQs

<Accordion title="How should we handle service providers with critical notes that say SMS and/or email only?">
  Because this is a rare edge case, follow the service provider’s critical note instead of the standard phone-call path.

  **Step 1**: Send the SMS and/or email (depending on what the critical note suggests) immediately and document the attempt in the work order notes.

  **Step 2**: Take action based on the below:

  - **Property Damage (PD) / Extreme Habitability (EH) emergencies:** Do not de-escalate and do not move the work order out of emergency handling. If the service provider does not respond through their required SMS/email contact method, call the PM or emergency escalation contact and follow the unresponsive PM path if they do not respond.
  - For emergency **non-property damage / non-extreme habitability** follow-ups, do not leave the work order sitting in an active follow-up status while waiting for a slower SMS or email response. After the message is sent, apply the same-day decision process based on how the service provider was assigned:
    - **PM-assigned, PM-requested, locked-in, or critical-note preferred SP:** Do not remove the service provider. For emergency non-PD/EH follow-ups, de-escalate to Urgent when appropriate and move/forward the work order to Waiting for PM so the status reflects that we are waiting on the PM or their preferred vendor.
    - **Latchel-sourced SP who has not visited the property or missed the initial appointment:** Reassign to another service provider and continue moving the work order forward.
    - **Latchel-sourced SP who has already visited the property:** Do not remove/reassign automatically. Forward to PM for direction to avoid unnecessary delays, duplicate diagnostics, or billing issues.
</Accordion>

**Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority.** Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
