<Info>
  This library lists every SOP and job aid an Operations Associate uses on shift, grouped by the kind of work you are doing. Each entry says what the document helps you do, so you can pick the right one quickly. If you can't find what you need, ask in your team Slack channel or see [How to: Escalate Between Teams](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams).
</Info>

<Tip>
  **Tags in titles:** **(Emergency)** applies only to emergency work orders, **(Non-Emergency)** only to non-emergency work orders, **(Premium)** to Premium customers, and **(TECH 101)** to basic console how-tos.
</Tip>
