## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to prevent confusion, missed requests, and inconsistent support quality when SMS or webchat conversations are transferred from Lucia, the AI chat assistant, to a live Operations associate. The goal is to provide clear, standardized guidelines for handling these escalations—ensuring that every resident who needs expert help after interacting with Lucia receives prompt, accurate, and professional support, with all relevant context carried over.
</Note>

<Warning>
  🔔 **AI Messaging Beta:** For **beta customers**, resident and SP messages are handled in **Conversations** by **Latchel AI**. Follow [<u>Job Aid: Handling Resident & Service Provider Conversations (AI Messaging Beta)</u>](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-Handling-Resident-and-SP-Conversations-AI-Messaging-Beta#when-can-i-switch-handled-by-to-ai "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/4301914114") instead of this SOP. This SOP still applies to all other customers.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **🔔 Before You Respond — Read This First**<br />When a conversation is transferred from Lucia, send an acknowledgment message within 1 minute, then review both the summary and the full transcript before sending your substantive response. The summary may not always capture the full context of what the resident means — reading through the transcript ensures you fully understand their concern, so they do not have to repeat themselves.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **Lucia (Assistant AI)** **Lucia (Assistant AI)** is a sophisticated AI chat tool designed to assist users with a variety of tasks, providing support in a helpful and friendly manner.

  Lucia will handle initial resident inquiries, efficiently addressing routine questions and tasks. In cases where a complex issue arises, the interaction will be escalated to you, ensuring that customers receive the expert support they need.
</Note>

#### **💬 How to Respond — Two-Step Response Model**

Operations associates can take over and assist the user moving forward by following the two-step response model below.

**Step 1 — Send the Acknowledgment Message Immediately** _(within 1 minute)_

Send an acknowledgment as soon as you accept the conversation so the resident knows they have been connected to a live associate while you review the prior conversation.

> _“Hi [Customer Name]. Thank you for reaching out! You've now been connected with one of our specialists. I'm reviewing your conversation now and will be right with you.”_

**Step 2 — ⚠️ Required: Review the Summary AND Full Transcript Before Your Next Message**

1. Read the Lucia conversation summary provided with the transfer.
2. **Go through the full transcript** to ensure you fully understand what the resident is asking or reporting — the summary may not always capture the complete context.
3. Identify what has already been shared, including the issue reported, any troubleshooting or details already discussed, what the resident is expecting, and what information is genuinely missing.
4. Once you clearly understand the resident’s concern, send your substantive response addressing the issue directly.
5. Only ask for information that is **genuinely missing** from the transcript — never ask the resident to re-explain something already covered.

**Example substantive response after review:**

> _“Thank you for your patience, [Customer Name]. I can see you reported [issue from transcript]. I’m looking into this now — just to confirm, [only ask for genuinely missing information].”_

 

### **Scenario: Resident Attempting to Submit a Work Order via Text**

When a Lucia conversation is handed off and the context shows the resident was trying to submit a maintenance request via text:

1. Greet the resident and acknowledge the handoff.
2. Check if Lucia already collected the resident's property address and issue description. If yes, use that context to skip ahead in intake.
3. If Lucia could not match the resident to a property: be transparent and proceed with collecting the info needed to create a WO. Do not ask the resident to start over if Lucia already collected details — use the summary.
4. Proceed with the standard resident text intake protocol (offer portal link if resident is now matched; offer SMS intake or outbound call if not).
   1. _"Hi [Name], you've been connected with one of our specialists. I can see you were trying to submit a maintenance request — I've got the details from your previous conversation and I'll take it from here. Can you confirm [any missing info]?"_

<Warning>
  Feature request is pending for Lucia to perform property search matching when she can't identify a resident by phone number. Until that ships, live associates handle the match and intake.
</Warning>

<Accordion title="Will AI chats be linked to work order transcripts? ">
  **No**, AI chats will not be automatically linked to work order transcripts. Therefore, Operations associates are reminded of the following important note:

  **Important**

  Please remember to manually assign any chats transferred from Lucia to the appropriate work order. This is essential to ensure that the interaction is properly logged in the transcript for accurate record-keeping and follow-up.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will agents have visibility into the AI conversations that are OPEN when they log in? ">

  **Note**: _Lucia is treated as a real user (an Operations Associate) within the application, just like any other Latchel associate having a conversation._
