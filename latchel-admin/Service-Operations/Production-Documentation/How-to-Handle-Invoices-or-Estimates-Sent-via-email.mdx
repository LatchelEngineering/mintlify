## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

We want to prevent lost, delayed, or mishandled invoices and estimates sent by service providers via email. The goal is to ensure every invoice or estimate received through email is promptly reviewed, matched to the correct work order, uploaded to the system, and processed according to budget and payment guidelines—so that no billing request is overlooked, approvals are properly managed, and payments are issued accurately and on time.

<Note>
  🎓 **Training Content**

  The contents of this page have been included in the Call Center Cross-Training Coassemble course.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

When working on new & unsorted tickets, you will come across emails and estimates from service providers. Email is a very common way for service providers to share these documents.

<Note>
  **🆕 Forward first (default):** Forward any invoice or estimate email from Assembly to [**invoices@inbox.latchel.com**](mailto:invoices@inbox.latchel.com) (invoices) or [**estimates@inbox.latchel.com**](mailto:estimates@inbox.latchel.com) (estimates). AI reads the email and its attachments (WO or invoice number, property address and unit, vendor name, resident name), finds the matching work order and **attaches the file to it**.

  ⚠️ **The AI only attaches the file. It does not start the estimate approval flow, update the approved budget, or approve or pay anything.** You still own the budget check and any budget update (Step 4), and payment or escalation (Steps 5–6). No reply to the vendor is needed if the email only submits an invoice or estimate. If the vendor asks a question or needs a decision, reply with a custom response that answers only that part.
</Note>

### Step 2 - Forward the email to the AI inbox

1. Forward the original email with all attachments to [**invoices@inbox.latchel.com**](mailto:invoices@inbox.latchel.com) or [**estimates@inbox.latchel.com**](mailto:estimates@inbox.latchel.com). Don't strip the attachments or edit the subject.
   - **Estimate and invoice in the same email?** Forward it once to either address. Both inboxes handle multiple submissions: the AI reads every attachment and attaches each one to the matching WO. You don't need to split the email or send it twice. If the AI has trouble with any of them, it routes the email to [team@latchel.com](mailto:team@latchel.com) (see Step 2a).
2. Give it a few minutes, then search the WO in the Latchel console and check that the file is in the WO's Files tab.
3. If the file is attached, move on to Step 4.
4. If the AI can't match the email, it comes back to Assembly as a **new ticket from [team@latchel.com](mailto:team@latchel.com)** with its findings and best guesses. Handle that ticket with Step 2a. **Do not forward it to the inbox again.**

### Step 2a - Handle unmatched invoices or estimates

**How to recognize it:** a new Assembly ticket sent from [**team@latchel.com**](mailto:team@latchel.com) that contains the original vendor email, plus the AI's findings and its best-guess work orders.

1. Review the AI's findings and best-guess WOs.
2. Confirm the right WO, using the guesses as a starting point (see [How to: Upload an Invoice for a Work Order – Step 2](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Upload-an-Invoice-for-a-Work-Order#step-2-find-the-existing-work-order)).

### Step 4 - Confirm the approved budget

Whether the file was attached by the AI or by hand, do this step by hand every time. **The AI does not update the approved budget or send estimates for approval.**

1. Corroborate that the amount quoted in the invoice/estimate is equal to or less than the approved budget.
   1. **NOTE**: All estimates must clearly indicate the exact total amount before proceeding with any approval process. Estimates without the full details must be confirmed prior to moving forward.
   2. Refer to [(⛑EMERGENCY) How to: Handle Changes to the Approved Budget](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/EMERGENCY-How-to-Handle-Changes-to-the-Approved-Budget#emergency-how-to-handle-changes-to-the-approved-budget) or [(Non-Emergency) How to: Handle Over Approved Budget (aka Estimate)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Non-Emergency-How-to-Handle-Over-Approved-Budget-aka-Estimate#non-emergency-how-to-handle-over-approved-budget-aka-estimate) for guidelines.
2. If the amount does not exceed the approved budget, move on to step 5.
3. If the amount is greater than the approved budget, but not greater than the hard budget limit, update the approved budget limit with the amount quoted and move on to step 5.
4. If the amount quoted is greater than both the approved and hard budget limit, move on to step 6.

### Step 5 - Take appropriate actions

<Warning>
  Always confirm that the job has been completed before processing payment. For issued cards SPs, we need either 1) confirmation from the resident or 2) before and after photos from the SP.
</Warning>

1. If the file being shared is an invoice, proceed with the following instructions:
   1. **Preferred vendors:** No reply needed. The property manager issues the payment.
   2. **Issued cards vendors:** If the amount is below $$500 and the work order has been confirmed complete by the resident, you may call the service provider and issue a credit card payment for the quoted amount. Please note that you can also process payments below $$500 via a web link if the SP includes one in their email.
      1. If the amount is \$500 or more or you were unable to contact the vendor, create a task for service provider associates under the "payment processing" group to follow up on the payment. (See [↔ How to: Create a Task](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-What-is-a-task#job-aid-what-is-a-task))
   3. **LVN/LCN direct deposit vendors:** Respond to the vendor's email asking them to submit the invoice/estimate using their Latchel dashboard. (See [How to: Handle Assembly Email Contacts](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Assembly-Email-Contacts#how-to-handle-assembly-email-contacts))

<Info>
  If the email only submits the invoice or estimate, leave an internal note in the Assembly ticket: _No response required. [Invoice/Estimate] attached to WO. [WO link]_

  If the vendor asked a question or needs a decision, reply to answer it before closing the ticket.
</Info>

### Step 6 - Escalate the exceeding quote

1. If the invoice/estimate does not exceed the hard budget limit, move on to step 7.
2. **Estimate for Emergency Work Order:**
   1. Attached the estimate received.
   2. Create a task assigned to the Troubleshooter. Indicate on the task the Assembly Ticket No of the estimate received.
      1. Task notes: **_Please review the attached estimate received via ASM-, check if any additional costs have already been charged, and confirm the exact total cost. Reach out to the service provider if follow-up or additional information is needed, and ensure the total cost is submitted for approval before proceeding._**
   3. Set the task to Never complete on any job state change.
3. **Estimate for Non-Emergency Work Order:**
   1. Move the work order to **Needs Owner Approval** so the owner or PM can decide. If they reply to us by phone or email instead, record it with **Action → Update Owner Approval** or **Action → Approve or Deny** (see [How to: Put a Job on Hold → Needs Owner Approval](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#needs-owner-approval)).
   2. Create the task for the property manager, and leave proper notes. (See - [(SOP) How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#how-to-put-a-job-on-hold))
   3. If the work order is already in "Needs Owner Approval" status, simply uploading the file and creating a task for the PM is sufficient.

4. **Invoice:**
   1. **Before proceeding:** Check whether the work order is an Autopilot WO.
      - **If Autopilot:** Do not assign to Service Provider Followup. Instead, create the task and assign it to the **PM**, per \[How to: Handle Autopilot WOs\]. Note in the task that the SP submitted an over-budget invoice via email.
      - **If not Autopilot:** Continue with the existing steps below (create/update the "Pending invoice" task for Service Provider Followup, etc.).
   2. If the invoice total is above the approved budget, reply to the service provider asking to clarify the amount and confirm if they got approval from Latchel, the Property Manager or the Owner for that amount.
      1. Say something like: _"Before we're able to proceed with this invoice, could you please verify what's the total amount for the job and who approved it? Thank you!"_
      2. If there's a task already created to request the invoice, update the description in the current "Pending invoice" task with the message:
         1. _"The service provider submitted an over-the-budget invoice, kindly check for the Service Provider's reply to email or contact the Service Provider to follow up"_

If there's not a task to collect the invoice, create a task that "never complete on any job state change" and assign it to the Service Provider Followup group with the message _"Service provider submitted an over the budget invoice, kindly check for Service Provider's reply to email or contact the Service Provider to follow up"_

### Step 7 - Update the vendor

1. No reply to the vendor is needed if the email only submits the estimate. The _(Operations) Estimate from SP_ template is no longer in Assembly.
2. If the vendor asks a question or needs a decision (for example, "Can I proceed?"), reply with a custom response that answers it.

### Step 8 - Wrap up

1. Copy the Assembly ticket number and paste it into the note session of the work order with a summary of the update and actions you took (↔ How to: Write Effective Notes). Save notes making them visible to others.
   - Note in the WO whether the file was attached by the AI inbox or placed by hand, e.g., _"Ticket 6027 — Invoice forwarded to invoices@inbox; AI attached to WO. Within budget."_
2. Copy the Latchel work order URL and paste it into the internal notes session of the Assembly ticket. This will enable us to quickly link the call to the work order and facilitate future research. (See [How to: Handle Assembly Email Contacts](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Assembly-Email-Contacts#how-to-handle-assembly-email-contacts))
3. Close the email in Assembly. (See [Service Ops: Assembly Guide](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Service-Ops-Assembly-Guide#service-ops-assembly-guide))

## ❓Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Q: The service provider sent an estimate via email with a link to approve or deny it. Do I (Ops) have the authority to approve or deny the estimate using that link?">
  **A:** Yes — Ops has the authority to approve or deny estimates submitted via a service provider's link, **as long as the standard budget rules are followed**:

  - ✅ **If the estimate is equal to or under the Hard Budget** → You may approve via the link. After approving, update the Approved Budget in the work order and document the action in the WO notes.
  - ❌ **If the estimate exceeds the Hard Budget** → Do **NOT** approve via the link. Escalate to the Property Manager.
  - ⚠️ **If the estimate has two or more options** (e.g., repair vs. replace) → Do **NOT** approve via the link. These are never auto-approved. Send all options to the owner (if the owner approves new budgets) or the PM, regardless of amount.

  **Always remember to:**

  1. Attach the estimate/email to the work order.
  2. Leave clear notes documenting that the estimate was approved/denied via the SP's link, including the amount and reason.

  The submission method (link, email, portal, phone, SMS) does **not** change Ops' approval authority — the same budget-limit rules apply in all cases.
</Accordion>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority. Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
