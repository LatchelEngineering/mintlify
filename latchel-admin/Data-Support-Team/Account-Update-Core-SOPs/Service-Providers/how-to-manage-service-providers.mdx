Service provider (SP) setup decides who Latchel contacts for every work order. When an SP is missing, prioritized wrong, or missing a skill, work orders stall or go to the wrong vendor. These SOPs cover every SP update that comes to Data Support.

<Check>
  **In other words:**

  An SP can only get a PM's work orders if they're added to Latchel, assigned to the PM, given the right skills, and prioritized in the right skill categories.
</Check>

### **How Service Provider Requests Reach Us**

- **Account Update Request** Typeform: **Portfolios > Manage Service Provider**, or service provider updates under the property options
- **New Vendor Self-Onboard** Typeform: a vendor signs up for a PM, such as Nomad
- **Owner-preferred SP** Jotform: an owner asks to add their own vendor
- **Add New Preferred Service Provider for Owners** workflow in **#ops_info_center**: Ops assigned an SP to a live work order and needs DS to finish the setup

### **Before You Start**

<Warning>
  Know which level you're changing. SP prioritization can be set at the **Company**, **Portfolio**, and **Property** level, and lower levels inherit from higher ones unless inheritance is turned off. Check all levels before you make a change. See [Configuration Inheritance](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Configuration-Inheritance-Property-Portfolio-Company-Level).
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Many settings are easiest to reach from the PM's dashboard. To get there, impersonate a user from the PM company in app.latchel.com.
</Tip>

### **Things to Remember**

<Warning>
  Latchel doesn't support warranty companies (for example, GE Home Warranty) as SPs. Let the PM or owner know, because work orders sent to them would end up in Pending.
</Warning>

<Note>
  For property-specific SP requests, like a vendor who should only handle one building's boilers, write your recommendation in the ticket and have the CSM confirm it with the PM before you remove skills from other SPs.
</Note>
