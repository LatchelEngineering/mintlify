title: "How to Turn Invoice Collection On and Off"
sidebarTitle: "Turn Invoice Collection On/Off"

---

When invoice collection is on, Latchel sends the SP daily text reminders to upload an invoice, and the PM gets an **Awaiting Invoice** task. PMs often turn it off for SPs who don't invoice through Latchel, such as supers, onsite techs, or vendors who bill the PM directly.

### **How to Turn Invoice Collection On or Off**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Impersonate the PM." icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={1} titleSize="h4">
    In app.latchel.com, impersonate a user from the PM company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the SP's profile." icon="2" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={2} titleSize="h4">
    Go to **People > Service Providers > My Service Provider Management** and click the SP's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Work Order Settings." icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={3} titleSize="h4">
    You'll find it on the **Details** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Invoice Collection." icon="4" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={4} titleSize="h4">
    Turn it **off** (gray) to stop invoice requests, or **on** to start them again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### **Things to Remember**

<Warning>
  Turning invoice collection off stops new reminders, but work orders already in **Awaiting Invoice** stay there. Let the requester know those need to be closed separately.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Turn this off for each SP in the request. It's a per-SP setting for that PM.
</Note>
