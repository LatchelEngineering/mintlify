---

Before a PM's work orders can go to an SP, the SP must be assigned to that PM company as a preferred service provider. You can do this from the SP's profile or from the PM's profile. Both give the same result.

### **Option A: From the Service Provider's Profile**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SP's profile." icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={1} titleSize="h4">
    Go to **People > Service Providers**, search for the SP, and open their profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Preferred PMs tab." icon="2" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={2} titleSize="h4">
    Add the PM company under **Preferred PMs**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit." icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={3} titleSize="h4">
    The SP is now a preferred SP for that PM company.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### **Option B: From the Property Manager's Profile**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the PM's profile." icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={1} titleSize="h4">
    Go to **People > Property Managers**, search for the PM, and click their name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Vendor Management." icon="2" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={2} titleSize="h4">
    Open the **vendors** tab, then the **Vendor Management** sub-tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + Add Vendor." icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={3} titleSize="h4">
    Search for the SP, select them, and click **submit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### **Next Step: Prioritize the SP**

<Warning>
  Assigning an SP isn't enough. An SP that isn't on the PM's priority list won't appear in the **Assign Service Providers** tool for that skill. Add them to each skill category next. See [How to Update Prioritization](/latchel-admin/Data-Support-Team/Account-Update-Core-SOPs/Service-Providers/how-to-update-service-provider-prioritization).
</Warning>

### **Things to Remember**

<Note>
  If an owner wants their own SP but the owner's property isn't in Latchel yet, you can't finish the setup. Flag it to the requester.
</Note>
