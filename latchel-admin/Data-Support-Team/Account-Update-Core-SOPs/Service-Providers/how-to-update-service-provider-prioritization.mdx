Prioritization sets the order Latchel contacts SPs in each skill category. The #1 SP gets the job first, and the next SP is contacted only after the response buffer runs out. Getting the order right means work orders reach the PM's preferred vendor first.

<Check>
  **In other words:**

  Each skill category has its own ranked list. Lists can be set at the Company, Portfolio, or Property level, and lower levels inherit from higher ones.
</Check>

### **Before You Start**

- Pull the current prioritization with queries **V19–V24** so you can compare it with the PM's request. See [Required Reports](/latchel-admin/Data-Support-Team/Account-Update-Core-SOPs/Reports/required-reports).
- Confirm which level the PM wants to change: the whole company, a portfolio, or specific properties.
- Make sure each SP is already assigned to the PM. See [How to Assign Service Providers to Property Managers](/latchel-admin/Data-Support-Team/Account-Update-Core-SOPs/Service-Providers/how-to-assign-service-providers-to-property-managers).

### **How to Update Company-Level Prioritization**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the PM's Categories & Priorities." icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={1} titleSize="h4">
    Go to **People > Property Managers**, open the PM's profile, then the **vendors** tab and the **Categories & Priorities** sub-tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the SP to a category." icon="2" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={2} titleSize="h4">
    Open the skill category and click **+ include vendor**. Click **+include** next to the SP, select the skills to include, and click **save**.

    New SPs are added to the bottom of the list. If an SP covers several categories, add them to each one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder the list." icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={3} titleSize="h4">
    Click **Edit Priorities**, drag each SP by the six-dot icon into the right order, and click **Done Updating Priorities**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove an SP if needed." icon="4" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={4} titleSize="h4">
    Click the three dots next to the SP and choose **Remove from priority list**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### **How to Update Portfolio-Level Prioritization**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the portfolio's Service Providers tab." icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={1} titleSize="h4">
    Open the portfolio from the PM company's **Portfolios** tab, then go to **Service Providers > Categories & Priorities**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the SP to one or more categories." icon="2" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={2} titleSize="h4">
    - **One category:** choose the category, click **+ Include Service Provider**, then **+ include**, and select the properties in the pop-up. Click **Continue**.
    - **Several categories:** click the three dots and choose **Prioritize Service Provider for Multiple Skills**. Search for the SP, choose the skills or **Check All Categories**, choose top or bottom of the list, and click **Prioritize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off inheritance if the portfolio should differ from the company." icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={3} titleSize="h4">
    If inheritance stays on, company-level changes can overwrite the portfolio's list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  When several portfolios need the same list, use **Copy Service Provider Settings from Another Portfolio** instead of rebuilding each one.
</Tip>

### **How to Update Property-Level Prioritization**

Open the property and go to the **Service Provider** tab. Click the ellipsis next to **Service Provider Prioritization** and choose **Copy Company Priorities** to start from the parent list, then adjust it. Turning this off disables inheritance for that property. See [How to Copy Company or Portfolio Service Provider Prioritization](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Copy-Company-or-Portfolio-Service-Provider-Prioritization).

### **Things to Remember**

<Note>
  If the PM hasn't said who should be first, set a handyman as priority 1 and put the others after them.
</Note>

<Warning>
  After you change the order, check the **response buffer** for the SP who is now last in each category. The last or only SP in a category should have a 72-hour buffer. See [How to Update Response Buffer](/latchel-admin/Data-Support-Team/Account-Update-Core-SOPs/Service-Providers/how-to-update-response-buffer).
</Warning>

<Note>
  For owner-preferred SPs, prioritize only in the owner's portfolio, not at the company level.
</Note>
