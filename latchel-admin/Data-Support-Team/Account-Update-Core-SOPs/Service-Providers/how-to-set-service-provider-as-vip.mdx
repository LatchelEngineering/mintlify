A VIP service provider can accept a work order without scheduling an arrival time. PMs use this for trusted vendors who manage their own schedules.

### **How to Set an SP as VIP**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Impersonate the PM." icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={1} titleSize="h4">
    In app.latchel.com, impersonate a user from the PM company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open My Service Provider Management." icon="2" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={2} titleSize="h4">
    Go to **People > Service Providers > My Service Provider Management** and search for the SP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the SP's settings." icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={3} titleSize="h4">
    Click the three dots next to the SP and choose **Preferred Service Provider Settings**. You can also click the SP's name and go to **Work Order Settings** on the **Details** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle VIP Service Provider on or off." icon="4" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={4} titleSize="h4">
    Turn it on to make the SP a VIP, or off to remove VIP status. Then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### **Things to Remember**

<Note>
  VIP requests often come with a response buffer change, for example "enable VIP and set the buffer to 3 hours." Update both. See [How to Update Response Buffer](/latchel-admin/Data-Support-Team/Account-Update-Core-SOPs/Service-Providers/how-to-update-response-buffer).
</Note>
