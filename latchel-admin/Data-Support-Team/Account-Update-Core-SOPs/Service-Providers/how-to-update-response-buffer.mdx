On non-emergency work orders, each preferred SP gets a window to accept the job before Latchel moves to the next SP on the priority list. This window is the **Response Buffer**. The default is 1 hour.

<Check>
  **In other words:**

  The response buffer is set per SP, per skill category. It only counts business hours, from 8 AM to 5 PM.
</Check>

### **How to Update the Response Buffer**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Impersonate the PM." icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={1} titleSize="h4">
    In app.latchel.com, impersonate a user from the PM company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Categories & Prioritization." icon="2" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={2} titleSize="h4">
    Go to **People > Service Providers > Categories & Prioritization** and expand the skill category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the ellipsis next to the SP and choose Edit Settings." icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={3} titleSize="h4">
    Change the **Response Buffer** to the requested time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save." icon="4" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={4} titleSize="h4">
    Repeat for each category the request covers. The buffer doesn't carry over between categories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check portfolio and property levels." icon="5" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={5} titleSize="h4">
    If the PM has portfolio- or property-level prioritization, update the buffer there too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### **Things to Remember**

<Warning>
  The **last or only SP** in every skill category must have a **72-hour** response buffer. Check this whenever you add, remove, or reorder SPs.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The timer starts when the job text is sent and only runs during business hours. For example, if SP 1 is texted at 4:30 PM with a 1-hour buffer, SP 2 is contacted at 8 AM the next business day.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use queries **V33**, **V34**, and **V35** to export response buffers at the company, portfolio, and property level. A blank value means the 60-minute default.
</Tip>
