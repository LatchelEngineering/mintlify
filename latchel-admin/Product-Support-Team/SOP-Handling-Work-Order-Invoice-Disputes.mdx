<Note>
  **Objective:** handle every work order (WO) invoice dispute consistently, from intake to refund or denial, in line with the [Latchel Guarantee and Refund Request Policy](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Latchel-Guarantee-and-Refund-Request-Policy).

  **Who owns what:** PS owns intake, the Deep Dive (DD), the decision write-up and all customer communication. **Accounts Payable (Samantha Terreros)** works with the service provider (SP) and processes refunds. **Will Gordon / Amrit Parajuli** decide when Latchel covers a cost. The **CSM** only steps in for churn-risk pushback.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Retired:** the Invoice Dispute Typeform, `#invoice-dispute-form`, `#ps-dd-requests`, Salesforce cases and templates, and refunds through `#accounts-payable`. Disputes are worked in **Wavelength (WL)**, DDs go through `#ps-dd-sddi`, and refunds go through `#work-order-payments`.
</Warning>

## How disputes reach PS

- **PM or Owner emails** [success@latchel.com](mailto:success@latchel.com) (the policy asks them to include the WO number or URL and the disputed invoice).
- **Ops escalates** through the **PS Escalation** workflow in `#ps-escalation` (Ops only escalates disputes, not general payment questions).
- **Accounts Payable** submits an **Invoice Dispute DD** in `#ps-dd-sddi` when a PM disputes an invoice with them.

All of these land in WL. Follow [SOP: Handling New Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-New-Ticket-in-Product-Support) to assign yourself, start the timer and link the Customer Profile. For Ops escalations, also follow [SOP: Handling Ops Escalations](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Ops-Escalations).

## Step 1: Flag the dispute to the Payments team

Do this as soon as the dispute is received, **whether or not the invoice has been paid**. Check the WO's invoice to see if it has been paid, then create a task on the WO:

1. On the WO, click **Action → Create Task**.
2. Add a subject line and set **Assign a Team** to **Payment Issues and Disputes**.
3. In the task, include the **WL ticket URL** and the message below.

| Invoice status | What the task says |
| --- | --- |
| **Not paid yet** | The invoice is under dispute. **Hold the charge** until the dispute findings are final. |
| **Already paid** | FYI: the invoice is under dispute and is being reviewed. |

Add an internal note on the WL ticket that the task was created.

## Step 2: Classify the ticket

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | **PM Inbound** · **Owner Inbound** (NOMAD and RentRedi owners) · **Latchel Ops Escalation** (from `#ps-escalation`) |
| **Topic** | **Invoicing & Payments → Invoice Dispute** |
| **Root Cause** | Only once the DD shows something went wrong (e.g., SP Performance, Latchel Associate Action, Account Configuration). Use **Undetermined – DD Requested** until then. |
| **Tags** | **DD Requested** when you open a DD. **Reimbursement or Credit Issued** if a refund or credit is approved. **CSM Handoff** / **Churn Risk** if it escalates. |

See [Job Aid: Ticket Category, Attributes, and Tags](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Interaction-Tags-Explained).

## Step 3: Check eligibility against the policy

Always check the policy **before** opening a DD. Record both timestamps on the ticket as an internal note:

- The date and time the **PM first reported the dispute**
- The date and time the **invoice was submitted**

<Warning>
  **Find the PM's first report, not when the ticket reached PS.** A dispute often lands in WL hours or days after the PM first raised it. Check every channel the PM used: call transcripts, webchat, SMS and emails, plus WO notes and activity. Use the **earliest** time the PM disputed the charge. Getting this wrong can put an eligible dispute outside the 72-hour window.
</Warning>

| **Reported within 72 hours of the invoice being submitted** | **Reported within 45 days after the invoice date** | **Reported more than 45 days after the invoice date** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligible for a **full refund** | Eligible for a **partial refund**: up to **50%** or a maximum of **\$300 per invoice** | **Not eligible.** Reply to the PM explaining the policy window. No DD needed. |

**Refunds are granted when** (per the policy):

- Latchel cancels a job without notifying the SP
- Latchel assigns an incorrect trade for a job
- Latchel fails to follow explicit PM instructions, resulting in monetary loss
- Pre-approval requirements configured in Latchel are not met
- Latchel authorizes a budget beyond the set limit without PM approval

**Refunds are not granted for:**

- PM errors using the Latchel software
- Inaccurate or misleading access instructions from the PM
- SP fees for work outside standard business hours
- Effective resolutions within the agreed budget or parameters
- A voided workmanship guarantee or parts warranty
- Appointment or service fees for confirmed appointments the resident missed (when no Permission to Enter was given)

<Note>
  **LVN guarantee and warranty:** LVN providers give a 30-day workmanship guarantee and a 1-year parts warranty. Both are voided if a **different provider** worked on the same issue (even one sourced through Latchel), and don't apply to end-of-life equipment when replacement was denied, or to a deeper root cause that was identified but not addressed. Preferred Service Providers aren't covered. Recalls go to [team@latchel.com](mailto:team@latchel.com).
</Note>

## Step 4: Review before you dive

- **Check the account's recent tickets** for related disputes and any **prior commitments** (credits or exceptions already promised). Link related tickets.
- **Check what's already on the WO** before asking the SP for more detail. If the invoice was approved, the estimate went to the PM and the line items are there, you usually have what you need.

## Step 5: Submit the DD and acknowledge the PM

**1. Submit the DD.** In `#ps-dd-sddi`, click **Invoice Dispute DD** (not the standard DD Request) and fill out the form:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **PM Company Name** | The PM or owner company |
| **Who is the CSM?** | From HubSpot (Company Owner) |
| **WL Link** | The WL ticket for this dispute |
| **WO Link** | The disputed work order |
| **Disputed amount (\$)** | The dollar amount (attach the invoice in the thread) |
| **Date/time the PM first reported the dispute** | The earliest time from Step 3, after checking transcripts, webchat, SMS and email |
| **Date/time the invoice was submitted** | From the WO invoice |
| **Refund window** | Within 72 hours / Within 45 days / Over 45 days (from Step 3) |
| **Invoice Paid?** | Paid or not paid (from Step 1) |
| **Payments task created on the WO?** | Hold requested or FYI (from Step 1) |
| **Prior commitments or related tickets?** | From Step 4 |
| **What is the PM disputing and why?** | The charge and the PM's reason, in your own words |
| **What initial review have you done?** | What you've already checked on the WO |
| **What question should the DD answer?** | The specific question (e.g., "Was the \$250 trip charge approved within budget?") |

**2. Link it.** Paste the Slack link into the WL ticket as an internal note and add the **DD Requested** tag.

**3. Acknowledge the PM.** Reply with the **Submit DD** template in WL. Update the WO status line in the template before sending. It tells the PM the review may take **up to seven business days**.

**4. Set the ticket to Escalated.** If it returns to Needs Response before the DD is done, move it back to Escalated and send the PM a short status update.

The DD itself follows [Job Aid: Deep Dive (DD) Process](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Deep-Dive-DD-Process). Post the conclusion on the WL ticket as an internal note with the DD ticket link and the WO link.

## Step 6: Workshop the conclusion

Before anything goes to the customer, submit your draft in `#ps-team-chat` using the **DD Conclusion Workshop** workflow. Write it with the [ACE framework](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/DD-Customer-Conclusion-Email-Framework-ACE) and always reference the Guarantee and Refund Request Policy.

The outcome is one of:

- **Approved:** the dispute is valid and a full or partial refund or credit is owed.
- **Denied:** the invoice is valid and stands.

## Step 7: If the dispute is approved (refund)

1. Submit the **Invoice Dispute** workflow in `#work-order-payments` with the DD link, the WL ticket link, the dispute amount, both timestamps, the refund window (72 hours or 45 days) and whether the workmanship guarantee was voided. Put the DD findings in **Describe the issue**.
2. **If Latchel is covering the cost** (a Latchel error or a one-time exception), tag **@Will Gordon** and **@Amrit Parajuli** for approval before you tell the PM.
3. **Accounts Payable (Samantha Terreros)** acknowledges the post, works with the SP and processes the refund. She confirms in the thread. AP doesn't contact the PM.
4. Tell the PM using ACE: the refund amount, the method, and the timeline (refunds can take up to 10 business days to show).
5. Add the **Reimbursement or Credit Issued** tag and close the ticket as **Closed** once the PM has been told and AP has confirmed.

<Tip>
  For a follow-up on a refund that's already in progress, reply in the existing `#work-order-payments` thread and tag @Samantha. Don't submit a new workflow.
</Tip>

## Step 8: If the dispute is denied (invoice stands)

1. Tell the PM using ACE: explain clearly why the invoice is valid and should be paid, based on the DD and the policy.
2. Update the **Payment Issues and Disputes** task from Step 1: the dispute was denied and the invoice stands, so the hold can be released.
2. Don't offer credits or exceptions outside the policy to end the conversation.
3. Close the ticket as **Closed** once the PM has the decision, unless they push back.

## NOMAD owners

Investigate every NOMAD owner dispute with the steps above first. Then follow [SOP: Handling Nomad Cases](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Nomad-Cases):

| Outcome | Who hears first |
| --- | --- |
| **Approved** | Tell the owner directly and CC Nomad ([support@nomadlease.com](mailto:support@nomadlease.com)). No escalation form needed. |
| **Denied** (invoice valid, payment still owed) | **Nomad first.** Send the full supporting package with the **NOMAD SUPPORT ESCALATION FORM** in WL: invoice, job photos, provider breakdown, budget approval history, DD findings and the policy. State that the invoice is valid and Latchel won't provide compensation. Wait for Nomad to align before contacting the owner. Follow up in `#latchel-nomad` if needed. |

## Handling PM pushback on a denied dispute

**PS handles the pushback.** Stand firm. Restate the reasoning calmly and point to the policy.

| **Situation** | **What to do** |
| --- | --- |
| **The PM pushes back** (even once) | Keep handling the ticket. Add the PM as an **FYI heads-up** on the weekly **PS \<> CS Sync** agenda so the CSM is aware. |
| **The PM keeps pushing back with extreme frustration**, putting the relationship at risk (**churn risk**) | [Escalate to the CSM](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Escalating-Tickets-to-the-CSM) using the workflow in `#csm-escalations`, with the category **PM Disagrees with Denied Invoice Dispute**. Add the **CSM Handoff** and **Churn Risk** tags. |

<Info>
  Not sure if it's reached churn risk? Ask the PSM first in `#psm-escalate` using the **PS Ticket Escalate** workflow.
</Info>
