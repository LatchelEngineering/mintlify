## **Purpose**

<Note>
  **Purpose of this document**

  This page shares the standard process and expectations for answering and completing customer tickets in **Wavelength (WL)**, formerly called Assembly. Ticket numbers still start with **ASM-**.

  **Who should read this document?**

  PS associates and CSMs.
</Note>
