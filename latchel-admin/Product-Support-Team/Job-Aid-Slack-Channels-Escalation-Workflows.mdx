<Note>
  #### **Purpose**

  When escalating issues or seeking answers from other departments, it's essential to use the **correct Slack channels and workflows**. Doing so ensures visibility, proper tracking, and faster resolution by the appropriate team.
</Note>

<Note>
  #### Who Should Use This

  - Product Support (PS)
  - Customer Success Managers (CSMs)
  - Any team member who needs to escalate a ticket or ask cross-functional questions.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **Always use the workflow when a channel has one.** Workflows capture the details the other team needs (company, ticket link, WO link, summary), so they don't have to come back and ask. After you submit, copy the Slack link and paste it into the Wavelength ticket as an internal note.
</Tip>

### Billing & Payments

| **Channel** | **Use it for** | **Workflow** | **Common PS examples** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `#accounts-receivable` | Monthly subscription invoices, PM billing POCs, payment methods, disputes, failed payments, internal errors and unit adjustments. Used to notify Accounting of a PM's current Active Unit count. | **Subscription Invoicing** — Billing disputes, change requests, general questions, past due invoices, failed payments, internal error catch, active unit adjustment alerts. | Credits for unit-count disputes, removing a payment method, microdeposit issues, requesting a copy of a contract or invoice, refunding a credit after cancellation. |
| `#work-order-payments` | **Work order invoice disputes** and WO payment questions. | **Invoice Dispute** — Escalate a WO invoice dispute or refund to Finance with the DD link, dispute amount and invoice date. **Payment Inquiry** — General WO payment questions. | Refunds after a Deep Dive conclusion, WO invoice disputes. |
| `#accounts-payable` | Charges and payouts on the **Service Provider/vendor side**. | _No workflow._ Include the company, WO link, ticket link and amount in your post. | A card charged without authorization, a vendor unable to save payment details, 1099 requests. |

### Product & Engineering

| **Channel** | **Use it for** | **Workflow** | **Common PS examples** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `#bug-report` | Low-urgency system issues or app misbehavior impacting customers or internal processes. | **Bug Report** — Submit through the [in-app bug report form](https://app.latchel.com/admin/bug-reports). It posts to the channel automatically. | Integration sync failures, recurring WOs stuck in Diagnosing, warranty-hold WOs auto-releasing, the resident portal showing the wrong WOs. |
| `#tech-high-sev-escalations` | **Business-critical** technical issues impacting multiple customers or Latchel operations. See [Job Aid: Tech High Sev Framework](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Tech-High-Sev-Framework). | **High Severity Tech Escalation** — Alert on-call engineers for immediate response. | Widespread outages or failures affecting many customers at once. |
| `#feature-requests` | Feature requests or PM pain points that could inform product enhancements. | **Feature Request** — Submit through the in-app form. It posts to the channel automatically. See [How to Handle: Feature Requests](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Handle-Feature-Requests). | Audit trails (on-call rotation, critical notes), assigning a vendor while a WO is Pending, invoice controls. Also used to log PM requests that deviate from the standard Pre-Dispatch process. |
| `#integration-health` | Integration sync problems and PMS connection issues. | _No workflow._ | Yardi, Rent Manager or Propertyware sync failures, PMS credential issues. |
| `#product` | General product feedback, questions and news. Use it to confirm product behavior or share PM feedback. | _No workflow._ | Confirming how a feature is supposed to work. |
| `#harmonia-feedback` | Feedback on how a work order was processed by **Intake Settings (Harmonia)**. | **Intake feedback on the WO** — Don't post directly. Submit from the work order and it populates in this channel (see below). | A PM emails us that a WO was classified or routed incorrectly by their intake settings. |

<Info>
  **How to submit intake feedback:** If a PM sends intake feedback directly by email, open the work order. In the **Intake Details** section, click the **thumbs up** or **thumbs down** based on the PM's feedback, then copy and paste the PM's **exact** feedback. Once submitted, it populates in `#harmonia-feedback`.
</Info>

<Warning>
  **Pre-Dispatch Instructions and Critical Notes are not feature requests.** Submit them through the **PD and CN Request** workflow in `#pd_cn_actionset`. Only log them in `#feature-requests` when the PM's request deviates from our standard process (see [SOP: Pre-Dispatch Instructions & Critical Notes](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Pre-Dispatch-Instructions-Critical-Notes)).
</Warning>

### Operations & Data

| **Channel** | **Use it for** | **Workflow** | **Common PS examples** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `#pd_cn_actionset` | Pre-Dispatch Instructions, Critical Note approvals and Action Set requests. See [How to: Request PD/CN Approval](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Request-Pre-Dispatch-Instruction-and-or-Critical-Note-Approval). | **PD and CN Request** — Non-standard PD/CN requests. **Action Set Request** — PM-specific automations. | Auto-assigning or suppressing a vendor for a portfolio, skipping resident notifications, notifying the PM when a vendor rejects a WO, after-hours emergency instructions. |
| `#ops_assistance_hub` | When we need Ops to take action on a work order, or carry out an update or instruction **in real time**. | _No workflow._ Include the WO link, what you need and how urgent it is. | Contacting a vendor for an estimate, photos or charges; creating an emergency WO; a courtesy call to a PM. |

| `#data-support-requests` | Data Support requests. | **Data Support Request** form. | Property and resident sync audits, removing critical notes in bulk, reactivating integrations, unit-count updates. |
| `#data-reporting-help-requests` | Custom reporting or engineering guidance on automations. | _No workflow._ | When an on-call rotation was changed, owner records for a portfolio. |
