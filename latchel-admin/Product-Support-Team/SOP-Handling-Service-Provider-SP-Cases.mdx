<Note>
  This SOP covers tickets from or about service providers (SPs): payments, skills, training, opting out, preferred status, joining the LCN, feature requests and email problems.
</Note>

### Who handles what

- **Ops agents** handle most day-to-day communication with SPs, using help guides and scripts for common scenarios. See the [Help Center Reference Guide](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Help-Center-Reference-Guide) and [PS Team vs Ops Agents](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/product-support-team-vs-ops-agent).
- **Ops escalates to PS** when the SP is very frustrated or the request needs PS (payments, account changes, opt-outs). See [SOP: Handling Ops Escalations](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Ops-Escalations).
- **LCN and LVN escalations** have their own SOPs: [SOP: How to Handle LCN SPs Escalations](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-How-to-Handle-LCN-SPs-Escalations) and [SOP: How to Handle LVN Escalations](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-How-to-Handle-LVN-Escalations).

### Start here: what type of SP is it?

Most SP tickets are handled differently depending on the SP's type **on that work order**. Open the work order and check whether the SP is:

| **Type** | **What it means** |
| --- | --- |
| **Preferred** | A vendor the PM added and prioritizes themselves. The PM owns the relationship, so most changes go through the PM. |
| **LCN** (Latchel Contractor Network) | Like a yellow pages: anyone who signs up and wants jobs. Latchel doesn't manage licensing or vet these vendors. |
| **LVN** (Latchel Vendor Network) | Latchel's national vendor partners. Their work is backed by the workmanship guarantee in our refund policy. Escalations go to `#lvn-war-room`. |

## Payment issues

For all payment tickets:

1. Open the work order.
2. Check the SP type on that work order (Preferred, LCN or LVN).
3. Follow the scenario below.

<Warning>
  **Don't share payment status with the SP yourself** if it's unpaid or pending. Send it to Finance through the **Payment Inquiry** workflow in `#work-order-payments` and include the work order link.
</Warning>

### Scenario 1: Payment is delayed

| **Preferred SPs** | **LCN \| LVN SPs** |
| --- | --- |
| Open the PM's company profile and go to the **Payments** tab. In the **Payment Settings** box, check **Preferred Service Provider Payments**. <ul><li>**If ON:** Submit the **Payment Inquiry** workflow in `#work-order-payments` with the work order link and ask Finance to process the payment.</li><li>**If OFF:** Latchel doesn't pay this SP. Email the PM to let them know their preferred SP is asking for payment on the work order.</li></ul> | Submit the **Payment Inquiry** workflow in `#work-order-payments` with the work order link and ask Finance to process the payment. |

### Scenario 2: No payment because the invoice went over the approved budget

| **Preferred SPs** | **LCN \| LVN SPs** |
| --- | --- |
| Check **Preferred Service Provider Payments** on the PM's **Payments** tab. <ul><li>**If ON:** Submit the **Payment Inquiry** workflow in `#work-order-payments` for payment **up to the approved budget**. Finance handles any dispute over the balance.</li><li>**If OFF:** Email the PM that the SP is asking for payment. Because it's their preferred SP, any negotiation over going past the budget without approval is between the PM and the SP.</li></ul> | **LCN:** Submit the **Payment Inquiry** workflow in `#work-order-payments` for payment **up to the approved budget**. Finance handles any dispute over the balance. <br /><br />**LVN:** Document the details, a high-level timeline, the approved budget and the invoice amount. Post it in `#lvn-war-room` and tag `@lvn-escalations`. |

### Scenario 3: Setting up direct deposit

Any SP can set up direct deposit themselves. Email them the help guide: [How can a Service Provider Add Their Bank Account Information](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wpGAA/How-can-a-Service-Provider-Add-Their-Bank-Account-Information).

## Updating assigned skills

<Note>
  An SP's skills decide which work orders they're offered. They should match the trades the SP **can** do.
</Note>

| **Preferred SPs** | **LCN \| LVN SPs** |
| --- | --- |
| <ol><li>Confirm whether the SP wants a skill **added** or **removed**.</li><li>Open the SP's profile and review the **Skills** box on the **Details** tab.</li><li>Let the PM know the update is needed and send them [How to Edit Service Provider Skills](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Edit-Service-Provider-Skills).</li><li>If the PM can't make the update, PS can make it for them.</li></ol> | Ops agents handle this. Reassign the ticket to the **Operations Team Email** teamspace in Wavelength. |

## Training requests

| **Preferred SPs** | **LCN \| LVN SPs** |
| --- | --- |
| <ol><li>Let the PM know their SP needs help using the platform, and share resources like [Service Provider Help Guides](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wpGAA/service-provider-help-guides) and [Latchel Assets](https://latchel.com/latchel-assets/).</li><li>If the PM pushes back, send the message below (adjust it to the conversation).</li><li>If the PM still pushes back, ask for their OK to share the information with the SP directly. Then send the SP the relevant help guides and assets.</li></ol> | <ol><li>Send the SP the relevant [Service Provider Help Guides](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wpGAA/service-provider-help-guides).</li><li>If the help guides don't answer their question:<ul><li>Impersonate the SP in Latchel to see what they see.</li><li>Send a Loom or a detailed email with screenshots walking them through the steps.</li><li>Request a new help guide in `#help-guide-requests` so future SPs have one.</li></ul></li><li>Close the loop once the SP confirms they're all set.</li></ol> |

</Accordion>

## Opting out of Latchel completely

| **Preferred SPs** | **LCN \| LVN SPs** |
| --- | --- |
| <ol><li>Let the PM know their SP wants to opt out, and wait for their direction.</li><li>**If the PM approves:** ask the SP for their feedback, then disable the SP account. Share the feedback in `#product` and close the loop with the SP.</li><li>**If the PM doesn't approve:** ask the PM to work it out with their SP.</li></ol> | <ol><li>Email the SP to ask why they want to opt out.</li><li>If they don't reply, follow up using [SOP: Managing Unresponsive Property Managers](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Managing-Unresponsive-Property-Managers-in-Case-Management).</li><li>Share any feedback you get in `#product`.</li><li>Close the loop with the SP, then disable the SP account.</li></ol> |

## Opting out of a specific PM company

| **Preferred SPs** | **LCN \| LVN SPs** |
| --- | --- |
| Follow the same steps as opting out of Latchel completely (above). | <ol><li>Ask the SP why they no longer want work orders from that PM.</li><li>If they don't reply, follow up using [SOP: Managing Unresponsive Property Managers](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Managing-Unresponsive-Property-Managers-in-Case-Management).</li><li>Show them how to reject work orders from that PM: [How to Reject a Work Order](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wpGAA/How-to-Reject-a-Work-Order-Service-Provider).</li><li>Close the loop with the SP.</li></ol> |

## Request to be preferred for a specific PM company

1. **Check the SP's current preferred status.** If they're already a preferred SP for that PM, let them know, and you're done.
2. **Ask the PM** whether they want the SP added to their preferred service providers.
3. **If the PM approves:**
   1. Open the SP's profile in Latchel and go to the **Preferred PMs** tab.
   2. Click **\+ Add PM**, search for the PM company, select it and click **Submit**.
   3. Let the PM and the SP know it's done.
4. **If the PM declines:** thank the PM and confirm no changes will be made. Then let the SP know the PM declined the request for now.

## Request to join the LCN

When an SP company asks to join our network and start accepting work orders, **Ops can handle it.** Reassign the ticket to the **Operations Team Email** teamspace in Wavelength.

If you're handling it yourself:

1. Search Latchel for the SP's email address.
2. If there's no record of the SP, send the **SP wants to join LCN** template in Wavelength. It shares the sign-up link: [latchel.com/vendor-sign-up](https://latchel.com/vendor-sign-up/).
3. If the SP already has a Latchel account, send them [How to Opt Into the Latchel Contractor Network](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Opt-Into-the-Latchel-Contractor-Network).

## Feature requests

1. Ask the SP for the details: what they'd like to see, and the problem it would solve for them.
2. Submit the request with the **Feature Request** form in the app. It posts to `#feature-requests` automatically. See [How to Handle: Feature Requests](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Handle-Feature-Requests).
3. Let the SP know it's been submitted, then close the ticket.
