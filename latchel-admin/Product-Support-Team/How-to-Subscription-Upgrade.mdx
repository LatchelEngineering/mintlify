<Note>
  **Purpose:** the steps PS takes when a PM asks to upgrade from **After-Hours Emergency to 24/7 Emergency** on their whole account or on specific portfolios.

  **Who owns it:** PS owns this upgrade, both temporary (e.g., 24/7 for one month) and ongoing. **Always notify the CSM.**
</Note>

<Warning>
  **After-Hours → 24/7 is the only upgrade PS can make.** Any other subscription change (adding Autopilot, On-Call or other add-ons, changing plans, downgrades, cancellations) goes to the CSM. Don't change the subscription. Escalate using [SOP: Escalating Tickets to the CSM](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Escalating-Tickets-to-the-CSM).
</Warning>

## Step 1: Confirm the request

Confirm with the PM:

- **What they want:** the account is on After-Hours Emergency today and they want 24/7 Emergency.
- **Where:** the whole account, or specific portfolios.
- **When:** the start date, and whether it's **temporary** (with an end date) or **ongoing**.

<Warning>
  **Don't quote a price.** Rates depend on each customer's contract. Tell the PM their rate will be confirmed by billing or their CSM.
</Warning>

Classify the WL ticket: **Topic: Subscription & Billing → Plan Change (Upgrade)**. Add the **Config Change Made** tag once the change is done.

## Step 2: Create the product package in Latchel

1. Go to the **PM Account** page in Latchel.
2. Open the **Product Packages** tab and click **New Package**. Name it after the subscription type (e.g., "24/7 Emergency").
3. Click the new package in the list to add products.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2025-10-16 at 3.06.57 PM.png](/images/PST-11.png)
</Frame>

4. For **Base Product**, select **24/7 Emergency** and set the **start date** the PM confirmed (today, unless they asked for a later date).

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2025-10-16 at 3.09.21 PM.png](/images/PST-12.png)
</Frame>

## Step 3: Make the package the active subscription

1. Go to the **PM Account** page (whole account) or the **Portfolio** page (specific portfolios).
2. Open the **Subscriptions** tab and click **New Subscription**.
3. Select the product package you just created.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2025-10-16 at 3.11.29 PM.png](/images/PST-13.png)
</Frame>

## Step 4: Notify billing and the CSM

1. In `#accounts-receivable`, submit the **Subscription Invoicing Request** workflow with the category **Change Request (CS)**. See [SOP: Handling Subscription Billing Inquiries](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Subscription-Billing-Inquiries) for the fields.
2. In **What do you need help with?**, include the new subscription, whether it's account-wide or which portfolios, the start date, and the end date if it's temporary.
3. Select the PM's **CSM** in the workflow (the Company Owner in HubSpot) and tag them in the thread so they're aware.
4. Paste the Slack link into the WL ticket as an internal note. If AR doesn't respond, tag **@Donita** in the thread.

## Step 5: Confirm with the PM

Let the PM know the change is done and when it takes effect, and that billing or their CSM will confirm the rate. Close the ticket as **Closed**.
