## **What's the difference between Product Support and Ops Agents?**

**Product Support (PS)** manages issues involving product knowledge, platform behavior, and helping property managers (PMs) update workflows to meet their goals. PS handles Ops escalations, Deep Dives, invoice disputes, feature requests, account configuration and service issues, focusing on functional product problems and business outcomes. PS also handles initial responses, troubleshooting and gathering details on customer issues before escalating, so the Customer Success Manager (CSM) receives complete information.

**Ops Agents** handle straightforward requests and basic account updates. Any request beyond a basic Tier 1 issue is escalated to PS.

---

Below is a summary of the roles of Product Support and Ops Agents, and **how you can tell the two apart**:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Product Support">
    - Technical problem solving and troubleshooting.
    - Answering customer questions about the product.
    - Fulfilling specific customer requests.
    - Investigating isolated interactions such as work orders, calls, dispatch attempts and configuration issues.
    - Subscription questions and system functionality.
    - Account configurations, including Intake Settings, Pre-Dispatch Instructions, Critical Notes, Emergency Escalation Contacts, Contact Rotations and Action Sets.
    - Focuses on immediate issues (short-term view).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Ops Agents">
    - Assists customers in updating contact information.
    - Basic settings and account updates listed below.
    - Assists SPs in making updates to their profiles.
    - Handles resident benefits questions directly with residents.
    - Escalates more complex product questions and account updates to PS.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## **How Ops Escalates to PS**

Ops escalates to PS through the workflows in the [**#ps-escalation**](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/C08475CCBGR) Slack channel. Each submission creates a ticket for PS in Wavelength.

| **Workflow** | **When Ops uses it** |
| --- | --- |
| **PS Escalation** | Anything outside of the Ops Agent requests listed below, or a question Ops can't answer. |
| **Benefits Response Review** | Ops needs help answering a resident's benefits question. Ops still owns the communication with the resident; PS sends the answer back to Ops. |
| **Failed Escalation Report** | Reporting a failed escalation to PS. |

For how PS handles these, see [SOP: Handling Ops Escalations](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Ops-Escalations).

    Before sending any login information or password reset links, the associate must strictly verify the requester's identity to protect account security and prevent unauthorized access. Follow these steps:

    1. Check if the Property Manager contacting us has an active user profile associated with the account.
       1. If the requester does not have an active profile, do not proceed.
    2. Ask the requester to confirm the email address where they expect to receive the login information or password reset link.
       1. Cross-check this email address against the one listed on the user's profile in the system.
       2. If the email addresses do not match, do not send any login information. Advise the requester that, for security reasons, login details can only be sent to the email address on file.

    Only proceed with sending the login information once both points have been confirmed.
