<Note>
  **Who this is for:** PS associates who get a ticket that isn't a support request, like a sales lead, a pricing question or an unrelated inbound email. PS doesn't sell or quote pricing. Your job is to send it to the right place quickly and close the ticket.
</Note>

## Quick reference

| **Who's asking** | **What they want** | **Where it goes** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Prospect** (not a Latchel customer yet) | A demo, pricing, or general info about Latchel | Post in `#growth-team` and send them the demo sign-up link |
| **Current customer** | Pricing, adding a product, changing their subscription | Their **CSM** |
| **Anyone else** | Partnership pitches, vendors selling to Latchel, job applicants, anything else that isn't a support request | Post in `#ask-answer` |

<Tip>
  **Not sure if they're a customer?** Search Latchel for their email address or company name (check **Disabled** in the search too). If there's an active PM company, they're a current customer.
</Tip>

## Prospects asking for a demo or pricing

1. **Check where they're located.** Latchel serves property managers in the **US and Puerto Rico** only for now. If they're outside those areas, still share it in `#growth-team` so Growth can decide how to respond.
2. **Share it in `#growth-team`.** Include:
   - Their name, company and contact info
   - Where they manage properties and roughly how many units, if they said
   - What they asked for, plus the Wavelength ticket link
3. **Reply to the prospect** with the demo sign-up link so they can book a time: [latchel.com/demo-request](https://latchel.com/demo-request/). Thank them for their interest. Don't quote pricing.
4. **Close the ticket.** See [SOP: Closing Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Closing-Cases-in-Product-Support).

## Current customers asking about pricing or new products

Pricing, adding a product, and subscription changes belong to the customer's CSM.

1. Follow [SOP: Escalating Tickets to the CSM](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Escalating-Tickets-to-the-CSM).
2. Don't quote pricing or promise anything about the change.

<Note>
  **Exception:** a **temporary or rolling coverage upgrade** (for example, 24/7 emergency coverage instead of after-hours) is handled by PS. See [How to: Subscription Upgrade](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Subscription-Upgrade) and [PS vs CSMs](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Product-Support-Team-vs-Customer-Success-Managers).
</Note>

## Everything else

For partnership pitches, vendors trying to sell to Latchel, job applicants, and other inbound that isn't a support request:

1. Post it in `#ask-answer` with a short summary and the Wavelength ticket link, so the right person can pick it up.
2. Close the ticket.

<Warning>
  **Service providers asking to join our network aren't sales leads.** They go to Ops. See [SOP: Handling Service Provider (SP) Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Service-Provider-SP-Cases#request-to-join-the-lcn).
</Warning>
