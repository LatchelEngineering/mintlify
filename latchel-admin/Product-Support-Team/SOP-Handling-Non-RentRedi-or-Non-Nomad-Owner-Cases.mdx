<Note>
  **Purpose:** How to handle tickets from property owners whose properties are managed by a PM company. The owner is the PM's client, so PS sends the owner's question to the PM to answer.

  **RentRedi or Nomad owner?** Those are the only two partnerships where we support owners directly. Use [SOP: Handling RentRedi Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-RentRedi-Cases) or [SOP: Handling Nomad Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Nomad-Cases) instead.
</Note>

## Step 1: Find the owner's profile

1. Search Latchel for the owner by **email address**.
2. Check **Disabled** in the search so deactivated accounts show up too.

## Step 2: Find the owner's PM company

1. Open the **Properties** or **Portfolio** tab on the owner's profile.
2. Click any property or portfolio listed.
3. Scroll to the **Details** tab.
4. In the **Property Manager** section, note the **PM Company**.

## Step 3: Find a PM contact email

1. Find the PM company's **Primary User**.
2. Check the **User Groups** section for the **Default User Group**.
3. Pick the best contact, usually the Primary User or the Default User Group. See [How to: Locate the "Default Notification User Group" contact(s)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Locate-the-Default-Notification-User-Group-contacts).

## Step 4: Send the owner's question to the PM

1. Email the PM contact from Wavelength. Include the owner's name, the property, and the owner's question, and ask the PM to follow up with their owner.
2. Close the ticket. See [SOP: Closing Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Closing-Cases-in-Product-Support).
