<Note>
  This job aid lists the values used to classify deep dive (DD) and SDDI tickets in the **PS Deep Dive** team space in Wavelength (WL). Consistent classification is what lets us spot trends across complaints and report them in the PS \<> CS Sync. For the full workflow, see [Job Aid: Deep Dive (DD) Process](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Deep-Dive-DD-Process).
</Note>

## DD vs. SDDI

| Type | Where it comes from | How much documentation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **DD (Deep Dive)** | Submitted by PS, Ops, CSMs, or Accounts Payable through the **DD Request** or **Invoice Dispute DD** workflow in `#ps-dd-sddi`. Covers escalated issues, complex scenarios, and process breakdowns that need a root cause. | Full timeline, child issue tickets, internal conclusion, and an ACE customer email reviewed in the DD Conclusion Workshop |
| **SDDI (Survey & Review Deep Dive Investigation)** | Created automatically from poor feedback (a rating of 3 or below) on **CSAT surveys**, **Google Reviews**, and **work order resident reviews**. Surfaces problems nobody escalated. | A 3–5 bullet summary tied to the feedback. See [Job Aid: SDDI Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-SDDI-Tickets). |

## Where each field goes in WL

Classify the DD ticket in this order. Each field has its own section below.

| Step | Field in the DD ticket | What it captures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Labels** → DD or SDDI | The type of dive |
| **2** | **Complaint Category** (click **\+** and search) | What the customer says went wrong |
| **3** | **Child issue tickets** (**Create → Escalation via Assembly**) | Each Latchel gap found, routed to the team that fixes it |
| **4** | **Customer Attributes** → **Root Cause Issue** (Attribute) and **Root Cause Details** (Value) | Why it happened, one pair per root cause |

<Warning>
  **Don't mix up the DD ticket and the original PS ticket.** The PS ticket that triggered the dive uses the standard PS fields in [Job Aid: Ticket Category, Attributes, and Tags](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Interaction-Tags-Explained): Root Cause **Undetermined – DD Requested** and the **DD Requested** tag while the dive is open. Once the DD is concluded, update the PS ticket's Root Cause to match what the dive found (see the [crosswalk](#crosswalk-to-ps-ticket-root-causes) below).
</Warning>

## Complaint Category

The **Complaint Category** is the main grouping for reporting and trend analysis.

- It describes the **issue as the customer experienced it**, based on the complaint they raised, not what the investigation found.
- It's set on the **parent (DD) ticket** and carries over to any child tickets.
- Pick the category that best matches the complaint. If the customer raised two separate problems, add both.

| **Complaint Category** | **Definition** | **Example(s)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Poor Scheduling Experience** | Scheduling or rescheduling issues delayed or disrupted the work order. | The vendor rescheduled multiple times, pushing the repair back over a week. |
| **Took Too Long to Complete** | The customer was unhappy with delays or the overall time to complete the work order. | An emergency took a week to resolve because the vendor accepted the job four days after it was assigned. |
| **Double Dispatch** | Two service providers were sent for the same work order. | A preferred vendor accepted the job, but the PM also sent another vendor not logged on the WO. |
| **Disclosed PM Info** | Sensitive PM information was shared with the resident or service provider. | The PM's phone number was given to a vendor, who then called the PM directly for payment. |
| **Too Long to Call Back** | The customer waited too long for a promised or expected callback. | More than 20 minutes passed between an emergency being reported and a troubleshooting call. |
| **No Job Submitted** | A maintenance issue was reported, but no work order was created. | A resident called in, or submitted through the portal, and no WO was generated. |
| **Latchel Team Unreachable** | The customer couldn't reach Latchel or didn't get a response. | A tenant repeatedly called and only heard hold music. SMS messages went unanswered. |
| **Service Given Beyond Subscription** | Latchel acted outside the PM's contracted service scope. | Latchel handled a non-emergency WO for a PM on an Emergency Only plan. |
| **Lack of Escalation** | An issue went unnoticed or unresolved because an escalation was missed or late. | The PM wasn't contacted for a PD/EH issue. A resident update wasn't escalated, delaying action. |
| **Poor Communication** | Critical details were missed, or unclear messaging led to confusion or the wrong action. | The resident wasn't told the WO was forwarded to the PM, or that the appointment was rescheduled. |
| **WO Landed on the Wrong State** | Automation put the work order in the wrong status or severity. | The WO was forwarded to the PM or held when the intake settings said otherwise. |
| **WO Wrongly Submitted** | The WO didn't match how the issue was reported or the actual problem. | A tenant used the plumbing flow to report a lawn issue, so the wrong skill and vendor were assigned. |
| **General Inquiry** | The customer wanted clarification about the work order or its timeline. | "Who created the WO?" or "How was the severity decided?" |
| **Issue Not Fixed** | The reported issue is still unresolved after service. | The sink kept leaking after the tech visit. |
| **Dissatisfaction with the SP** | The complaint is about the SP's work quality, professionalism, or conduct. | The resident said the SP was rude and left trash behind. |
| **Dissatisfaction with the PM** | The complaint is about the PM's handling or responsiveness. | The resident said their PM wasn't responding. |
| **Dissatisfaction with the Work Coordination** | The customer was unhappy with the overall coordination of the work order. | The PM complained about slow SP assignment and no updates for them or the resident. |
| **Incorrect Dispatch** | The wrong type of service provider was assigned. | A roofer was sent for a plumbing leak. |
| **System Malfunction** | A technical or system issue happened during coordination. | A resident couldn't submit a WO because of a portal error. A vendor couldn't upload an estimate. |
| **Other Ongoing Issues** | A separate issue was found during or after the service. | A tile was damaged after the SP fixed the toilet. |
| **Unhappy with Troubleshooting** | The customer was unhappy with the troubleshooting experience. | The troubleshooting call repeated questions the resident had already answered online. |
| **Disregarded PM Preferences** | The PM's instructions or preferences weren't followed. | Pre-dispatch notes were ignored, or the network was used instead of the PM's preferred vendor. |
| **Invoice Dispute** | The customer disputes an invoice on the WO. | The SP's invoice went over the approved budget. |

<Tip>
  **Invoice Dispute** dives come in through the **Invoice Dispute DD** workflow, not the standard DD Request. Eligibility, refunds, and outcomes follow [SOP: Handling Work Order Invoice Disputes](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Work-Order-Invoice-Disputes).
</Tip>

## Child Issue Tickets

Child issue tickets record the **underlying Latchel gaps** the investigation found, so the right team can act on them. They don't repeat the customer's complaint. They document the cause: system errors, associate or vendor actions, process gaps, or product improvements.

- Create one child ticket **for each distinct Latchel gap**. A single DD can have several.
- Causes outside Latchel (PM, owner, or resident actions, account setup) don't need a child ticket. Record them in a **Reported Issue Found** note instead.
- Create them from the DD ticket with **Create → Escalation via Assembly** (the menu still uses the old name) and the matching template.

| **Child Ticket Title** | **Use it when** | **Template → where it goes** | **Example(s)** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **LVN SP Feedback – SP Company Name** | A **Latchel Vendor Network SP's** actions, behavior, or non-compliance caused the complaint. | **LVN SP Feedback** → PS Deep Dive team space, plus the **LVN Vendor Feedback** workflow in `#vendor-feedback` | - The SP went ahead without budget increase approval.<br />- The SP tried to collect payment from the resident for PM-responsible work. |
| **Associate Feedback – Associate Name** | An **associate didn't follow an SOP, job aid, or guideline**, or their communication contributed to the issue. Also use it when a Shift Coach or support agent gave wrong or unclear instructions. | **Latchel Agent Feedback** → Feedback Loop team space, plus the **Agent Feedback** workflow in `#agent-feedback` | - Completed the WO without the tenant's confirmation.<br />- Posted in the emergency Slack channel instead of creating a task.<br />- Was dismissive or didn't listen to the customer. |
| **Ops Request (SOP)** | An **SOP or job aid is missing or outdated**, causing confusion or inconsistent handling. | **SOP Request/Update** → Service Ops Request team space, plus an "Ops SOP Needed/Updated" request in `#svc-ops-request` | - The SOP has outdated steps.<br />- The SOP is missing information, causing errors or delays. |
| **Ops Request (Help Guide)** | A **Help Guide needs to be created or updated** so users can follow the process. | See [How to: Report Issues with the Latchel Help Center](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Report-Issues-with-the-Latchel-Help-Center) | - No Help Guide exists for the process.<br />- The Help Guide is missing steps. |
| **Product (Feature Request)** | A **new feature or improvement** would prevent the complaint. | **Feature Request** → PS Deep Dive team space, plus the [Bug Reports form](https://app.latchel.com/admin/bug-reports) (**I wish it did X**) or `#feature-requests` | - The PM wants to customize tenant SMS notifications.<br />- A resident wants Spanish-language notifications. |
| **Product (Bug)** | A **system defect** disrupted the normal process. | **Bug Report** → PS Deep Dive team space, plus the [Bug Reports form](https://app.latchel.com/admin/bug-reports) (**Something is broken**), which posts to `#bug-report` | - The WO landed on "Waiting for PM" with no trigger.<br />- The system didn't send the expected PM notification. |

## Root Causes

**Root causes** are the specific factors that led to the complaint. Tag them in **Customer Attributes**: the **Issue Found** is the Attribute (**Root Cause Issue**), and the **root cause** is the Value (**Root Cause Details**).

A good root cause is:

- **Accurate:** clearly supported by the investigation.
- **Actionable:** points to something that can be fixed or improved.
- **Linked to the solution:** connects what went wrong to how we prevent it.

### Multiple root causes

Complex complaints often have **more than one root cause**. Add a Root Cause Issue/Details pair for **each** one, and create a child ticket for each Latchel gap. This keeps every factor traceable, sends follow-up to the right team (coaching, SOP update, product), and keeps trend reporting accurate.

## Crosswalk to PS ticket root causes

When the DD is concluded, update the **original PS ticket** from **Undetermined – DD Requested** to the PS Root Cause that matches what the dive found. Use this as a guide and pick every value that applies.

| **DD Issue Found** | **Closest PS ticket Root Cause** |
| --- | --- |
| LCN / LVN Issue · Preferred SP Issue | **SP Performance** |
| Owner Issue (RentRedi / NOMAD) | **Owner Action or Delay** (or **Account Configuration** for a severity settings issue) |
| Resident Issue | **Resident Action or Delay** |
| PM Issue | **PM Action or Delay** · **Knowledge Gap** (lack of familiarity) · **Account Configuration** (settings or profile data) |
| Latchel Agent Issue · Call Center Issue | **Latchel Associate Action** |
| Ops Request (SOP / Help Guide) | Usually paired with **Latchel Associate Action** or **Knowledge Gap**. Add the **SOP Gap** or **Help Guide Gap** tag. |
| Product Issue (bug) | **Platform Defect** (add the **Bug Filed** tag) |
| Product Issue (feature request) | Add the **Feature Request Filed** tag. Pick the Root Cause from what failed. |
| Automation behaved as configured but gave the wrong outcome | **Automation or Workflow Behaviour** |
| Data Entry Error | **Latchel Associate Action**, or **Account Configuration** if the error was in the PM's own data |

<Warning>
  Can't find a value that fits? Ask your direct manager in `#ps-team-chat` before picking the closest option, so the list stays accurate.
</Warning>
