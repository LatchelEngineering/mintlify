<Note>
  **Purpose of this document**

  This document serves as a comprehensive guide for handling property managers' requests for more information on their accounts. It includes clarifying questions, best practices, and response templates that product support (PS) can use to communicate desired workflows and potential caveats with property managers (PMs).
</Note>

## Important Note

<Warning>
  These templates are just a starting point. Always personalize them based on the situation and the conversation you've had with the PM.
</Warning>

## Process Overview

- **Lead with Questions**: Always ask clarifying questions and present options, not just solutions.
- **Customer Effort**: Minimize the effort for the customer. Your goal is to paint a clear picture for them and make their next steps as easy as possible.
- **Be the Expert**: Don't just take the PM's word for it. You are the Latchel expert—use your knowledge to share best practices and highlight any concerns or caveats that may arise.

Before responding, thoroughly review:

- The PM's case
- The PM account (active product package, portfolio setup, [pre-dispatch and critical notes](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Pre-Dispatch-Instructions-Critical-Notes), intake settings, owner setup, etc.)

<Tip>
  **Additional Resource:** [Help Center Reference Guide](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Help-Center-Reference-Guide)
</Tip>

## Scenarios and Suggested Responses

### Work Order Handling Concerns:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="PM wants to approve every single work order (WO) request.">
    As a best practice, we recommend leaving emergency WOs as emergencies and moving only non-emergencies to "Needs Pre-Approval." This avoids delays, ensuring emergency situations do not worsen. WOs in Needs Pre Approval trigger an email notification to your team, not a call.

    **Emergency Process:**

    - We always call your emergency contacts for issues related to [property damage or extreme habitability](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Emergency-Escalations-Property-Damaging-and-Extreme-Habitability-Situations). Refer to Latchel's [emergency outbound communication process](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Emergency-Outbound-Communication-Process).
    - For non-emergencies, we troubleshoot with the resident and confirm the severity before dispatching. If you'd prefer us to call a specific number before dispatching, let us know who we should contact. If the person is unreachable, should we:
      - A) Forward the WO to your team?
      - B) Proceed with dispatch?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The PM would like the Photo of Appliance Make/Model/Serial Number as a requirement when submitting a work order.">
    **Process**: The lack of requirement for pictures is intentional. There are accessibility laws/regulations (Fair Housing Act and more local stuff) that make us uneasy about requiring photos for a maintenance request (for example, for someone who has a vision disability). It's kind of a gray area but that is the reason.

    **Proposed Explanation**: Let the PM know the BRAND for appliances is REQUIRED; However, the model and serial number/pics are optional. There is typically not even a way to guarantee that a serial number is present. They are usually on a sticker that is often removed during installation or is worn and faded. So mandatory isn't even really an option. Same with model - It's usually not necessarily present on the appliance itself. It's on the manual/email receipt, things like that, that usually get thrown away.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The PM Doesn't Understand Soft vs. Hard Budget Limits">
    I hope you're doing well! We received your question about budget limits, and I wanted to clarify how they work.

    - **Soft Budget Limits**: The maximum amount shared with the service provider. Latchel auto-approves anything between the soft and hard limits without PM approval.
    - **Hard Budget Limits**: If the estimate is above this amount, we seek approval before proceeding. This amount is always hidden from the service provider.
    - Here's [a guide](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Customize-Work-Order-Budget-Limits) with more details on the budget limit process.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="PM Asking Why They Had to Approve a WO (No Intake Rule Matched)">
    **Before responding:** open the WO's **Intake** tab and check the outcome. If it says no rules matched, the rule set's default outcome was applied.

    **Suggested response:**

    This work order came to your team for approval because the resident's request didn't match any of the rules in your intake settings. When no rule matches, your default intake setting applies, and on your account that default sends the work order to your team for approval.

    To handle requests like this automatically in the future, you can:

    - Add a rule for this type of issue to your intake settings, or
    - Change your default intake setting.

    Here's how: [Managing Intake Settings](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Managing-Intake-Settings). For an overview, see [Introduction to Intake Settings](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Introduction-to-Intake-Settings).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Emergency WO Forwarded to PM Without Calling Their Escalation Contact">
    **Before responding, check:**

    - **Intake settings:** was the WO actually an emergency? Check the outcome on the WO's **Intake** tab.
    - **PD or EH:** was it property damaging (PD) or an extreme habitability (EH) issue? By default, we only call escalation contacts for PD/EH emergencies.
    - **Pre-dispatch instructions:** do they ask us to call the PM for **all** emergencies?

    Resources to share:

    - [Emergency Escalations: Property Damaging and Extreme Habitability Situations](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Emergency-Escalations-Property-Damaging-and-Extreme-Habitability-Situations)
    - [Emergency Outbound Communication Process](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Emergency-Outbound-Communication-Process)
    - [Emergency Process Quick Guide](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Emergency-Process-Quick-Guide)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Vendor Did Not Answer the First Call—Can They Later Accept the Job?">
    If a vendor misses our first contact attempt, the WO is still available to them as long as another vendor hasn't already accepted the job. Once notified, multiple service providers can accept open the job based on the prioritization you've set.

    **Process resources:**

    - [We notify each preferred service provider in the order you delegate.](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Prioritize-Edit-Service-Providers-with-their-skills)
    - Each service provider is [given a specific window of time (anywhere from 1 hour to 72 hours) to accept the job](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Customize-How-Long-Latchel-Waits-Before-Contacting-Your-Next-Service-Provider) before the next service provider gets notified.
    - Please see [this 6-minute video on the WO lifecycle](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Overview-of-Work-Order-Life-Cycle) for more details!
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

This allows residents who leave a 4 or 5-star review in Latchel to share their positive feedback on your company's social media page. To enable this, take a look at this Helpguide: [Configuring Portal Settings](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Configuring-Portal-Settings) and scroll to section Resident Review URL.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Missing Contact Information for Emergency Escalation">
    **What to explain:** we call the emergency escalation contacts (EECs) during emergencies: for property damaging (PD) or extreme habitability (EH) emergencies, or for all emergencies if the PM has a pre-dispatch instruction asking for that. That's why it's vital that every EEC has a phone number.

    The EEC should be a **contact rotation**. To fix a missing number, add a phone number to the user listed in the contact rotation.

    See [Emergency Escalation Settings Overview](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Emergency-Escalation-Settings-Overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Automation WO Emails Sent to Wrong User">
    <Warning>
      **What happened prior to this step?**

      - Thoroughly check the WO
        - Check the EEC emails,
        - the Assigned PM,
        - if the property is assigned to a portfolio,
        - the User Team,
        - and [SendGrid](/latchel-admin/Customer-Success-Team/job-aid-send-grid-training-unblocking-bounced-emails).
      - If the user is an owner, check the critical notes (CN), the owner profile and the owner communication settings.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="PM Requests a Specific Vendor for a Problem Category (Property Specific Rule)">
    <Warning>
      **What happened prior to this step?**

      Check if the inheritance toggle is on or off at the property level. If it's turned on:

      - [Copy Company or Portfolio Service Provider Prioritization](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Copy-Company-or-Portfolio-Service-Provider-Prioritization) to the specific property so that for all other WOs (outside of that particular issues), the desired service providers are notified accordingly.
      - Once copied, navigate to the problem category section and edit it so that the requested service provider is the [only](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Remove-a-Preferred-Service-Provider-Priority-List) service provider listed under that category.
      - Lastly, we will turn the inheritance OFF for that property.
    </Warning>

    We noticed the [portfolio inheritance](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Configuration-Inheritance-Property-Portfolio-Company-Level) setting is turned ON for this property, which may cause delays in assigning the desired vendor.

    For context, since this is turned ON, if [Vendor Name] does not accept within the [response buffer timeframe](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Customize-How-Long-Latchel-Waits-Before-Contacting-Your-Next-Service-Provider), the work order will be directed first to the portfolio's service providers. If they do not accept the work order, it will then be directed to the company's service providers. This setup poses a risk of a different service provider accepting the work order, potentially leading to delays or complications.

    To ensure that only \[Vendor Name\] is assigned to HVAC WOs, follow these steps:

    - [Copy Company or Portfolio Service Provider Prioritization](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Copy-Company-or-Portfolio-Service-Provider-Prioritization) to this specific property so that for all other WOs (outside of HVAC issues), your desired service providers are notified accordingly.
    - Navigate to the HVAC section and [list \[Vendor Name\] as the only provider](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Remove-a-Preferred-Service-Provider-Priority-List).
    - Turn OFF inheritance for this property.

    Here's a [quick video guide](https://www.loom.com/share/ad03a511427c49c096077f48424b6e0f?sid=7119e7b8-d292-44ea-bf8e-fe207d39cb9b) to walk you through these steps.

  <Accordion title="Pre-Dispatch Emergency Instructions Lacks Clarity -- How should we proceed if PM is unresponsive?">
    We're following up on the recent escalation regarding the Pre-Dispatch Instruction for emergency approvals. The current note: [insert\]

    It appears that the current instruction lacks specific details, and we want to ensure it fully aligns with your preferences to prevent any misunderstandings. Based on your feedback, we understand that you prefer to be contacted first before proceeding with a dispatch.

    To clarify and refine this instruction, could you let us know how you'd like us to proceed in cases where we are unable to reach any of your emergency contacts, or unable to reach the resident for troubleshooting? Specifically, would you prefer we continue with the dispatch, or should the work order be forwarded to your team for handling?

    As a best practice, we recommend proceeding with dispatch to prevent any potential delays that could worsen the emergency situation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pre-Dispatch Instructions Requires Clarity Before Modifying Any Accounts">
    The current instruction states: "Do not dispatch without approval from the PM."

    We need your confirmation on the following:

    - _For contacting for approval, should we be calling the emergency escalation contact list? Or a different POC(s)?_
    - _If we are not able to reach a contact(s), how should we proceed with the WO? Continue with dispatch? Or forward the WO to PM?_
    - _Is this approval required for all emergencies? Or only those deemed property damaging and/or an extreme habitability risk?_

    The key to gaining clarity on PM instructions before modifying any account details lies in asking probing questions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Differences Between Escalation Contacts and Pre-Dispatch Instructions">
    Our standard process is:

    - Latchel will call [escalation contacts](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Emergency-Escalation-Settings-Overview) 1x (with a voicemail).
    - Latchel will only call the designated escalation contacts.
    - Latchel will only call these escalation contacts for situations considered [Property Damaging or Extreme Habitability](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Emergency-Escalations-Property-Damaging-and-Extreme-Habitability-Situations) at all hours of the day.
      - Meaning, if the situation does not fall under our property damaging/extreme habitability situations, no call will be made to the escalation contacts. The work order will Forward to PM and notify the assigned [User Group](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Create-User-Groups) of that property via email for next steps.

    Pre-Dispatch Instructions are essentially instructions to be carried for [emergency WOs only](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Send-Latchel-Pre-Dispatch-Instructions) that are outside of our normal standard process listed above. You can find approved pre-dispatch instructions here: [SOP: Pre-Dispatch Instructions & Critical Notes](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Pre-Dispatch-Instructions-Critical-Notes).

    Now as far as settings flowing from company, portfolio to property level, please see this: [Configuration Inheritance (Property, Portfolio, & Company Level)](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Configuration-Inheritance-Property-Portfolio-Company-Level)

    - When a WO is created, the Latchel system first looks at the property level settings.
    - If nothing specific is configured at the property level setting, the system will move to the portfolio level settings.
    - If nothing specific is configured at the portfolio level setting, the system will move to the company level settings.
    - Essentially meaning, the property is inheriting the settings that have been default set at the company level.

    Another way to think about it is,

    - When a WO is created, the Latchel system first looks at the property level settings.
    - If there is a specific setting configured at the property level, this essentially overrides the next levels of settings. The WO would follow what is placed at the property level.
    - The same logic applies to portfolio settings!
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Service Provider Related Concerns:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Differences Between LCN and LVN">
    We wanted to clarify the difference between LCN (Latchel Contractor Network) and LVN (Latchel Vendor Network).

    LVN providers exclusively use Latchel's direct deposit payout technology, and all technicians in the LVN are guaranteed to be vetted, licensed, bonded, and insured. They also offer free recall services for any issues, ensuring higher service quality and accountability. Additionally, LVN providers operate through a central dispatcher who coordinates all work orders, ensuring the best technician is assigned for each job.

    In contrast, LCN do not guarantee vetted technicians or free recall services. The LCN operational model is more varied and flexible, without a central dispatcher managing the process. Our Latchel associates find LCN service providers through search tools like google, angie's list, yelp, etc.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="VIP Service Providers">
    General Information on how to turn on VIP for Service Providers

    Until the vendor accepts and schedules the appointment, the exact property address will not be released. We did this to avoid vendors seeing the address, not actually accepting the WO and not being assigned, and then coordinating directly with the resident outside of Latchel and leading to a double dispatch situation as Latchel would continue to find a service provider.

    To understand this process live, I highly recommend watching [this quick video](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Overview-of-Work-Order-Life-Cycle)!

    With VIP turned on, the resident info and property address are shared to the service provider as soon as they accept the work order. They can then schedule the appointment at a later date but the system does send them reminders to schedule the appointment. Depending on the severity of the WO, if no appointment is scheduled, a task and notification will be sent to your user group to make you all aware of the situation so that you can reach out and schedule the appointment or reassign the WO to a different vendor.

    [Here is more info on how VIP vendor accept WOs](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wpGAA/How-can-a-VIP-Service-Provider-Accept-or-Schedule-a-Work-Order)!
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
