## Purpose

<Note>
  **Purpose of this document**

  The steps to take when submitting a Pre-Dispatch Instruction and/or Critical Note for approval from the Product Support and Service Operations teams.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Who should read this document?**

  CSMs, Service Ops and Product Support.
</Note>

### Step-by-Step Guide

<Note>
  **What happened prior to this step?**

  Review [SOP: Pre-Dispatch Instructions and Critical Notes](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Pre-Dispatch-Instructions-Critical-Notes) to confirm the request is appropriate for a Pre-Dispatch Instruction or Critical Note.
</Note>

#### Process Overview

All requested Pre-Dispatch Instructions and/or Critical Notes that fall outside of the scenarios listed in the [SOP: Pre-Dispatch Instructions and Critical Notes](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Pre-Dispatch-Instructions-Critical-Notes) require approval from Product Support and Service Operations.

<Warning>
  **Before you submit, check the following:**

  - **Can it be configured instead?** Most requests can be handled through an existing setting (Intake Settings, blacklisting, Permission to Enter, Emergency Escalation Contacts, or an Action Set). Critical Notes are a last resort.
  - **Is it for emergencies only?** Pre-Dispatch Instructions only surface before dispatching an **emergency** work order. They never apply to non-emergencies.
  - **Does the standard process already cover it?** Encourage the PM to stick with our [Emergency Outbound Communication Process](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Emergency-Outbound-Communication-Process).
  - **Does it involve contacting people?** Use a [Contact Rotation](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/on-call-rotation-getting-started) URL built for that contact list, and state what to do if no one answers: **forward to the PM** or **continue with dispatch**.
  - **Is it a deviation from our standard process?** Also log it in **#feature-requests** (see [How to Handle: Feature Requests](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Handle-Feature-Requests)) so Product can track it.
</Warning>

- **Company Name** - The name of the property management company as it appears in the Latchel console.
- **Company URL Link (in Latchel)** - Copy the URL of the PM company page in Latchel (e.g., `https://app.latchel.com/admin/pm-company/12345`). The number at the end of the URL is the PM Company ID.

- **Current PD/CN** - Paste the Pre-Dispatch Instruction or Critical Note that is currently on the account. If there is none, enter **N/A**.
- **Level of Inheritance** - Whether the request is for the company account as a whole, a specific portfolio, or a specific property.
- **If Portfolio or Property Level, please share details** - Which portfolio(s) or property(ies) the request applies to.
- **Please submit the drafted PD/CN Request** - The Pre-Dispatch Instruction or Critical Note exactly as it would appear in Latchel.
- **PM Subscription** - The PM's active subscription. From the PM company page, go to the **Subscription** tab and find the green active subscription. You may also need to check the **Product Package** tab to understand the service level. - **Pre-Dispatch Instruction or Critical Note?** - Indicate which one you are requesting approval for.

#### Step 3 - Submit the Workflow

Submit the workflow. Watch the thread on your submission for any follow-up questions from Product Support or Service Operations.

#### Step 4 - Review and Approval

Product Support and Service Operations will review the request. Approval and details will be shared with the submitter in the thread within **4 hours** of submission.

<Tip>
  **Once approved:** If the update takes 15 minutes or less, the submitter can make it directly in Latchel. Anything longer should go to the Data Team.
</Tip>
