<Note>
  **When to use this:** An Autopilot PM asked Ops to take action on a work order for them (for example, call the resident until they respond, keep troubleshooting, or source another vendor), then got frustrated or ran into trouble with the self-service tools. Ops escalates it to PS, and PS explains how Autopilot works.

  - **Post-onboarding PMs:** PS owns this.
  - **PMs still in onboarding:** the CSM owns this. See [Product Support Team vs Customer Success Managers](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Product-Support-Team-vs-Customer-Success-Managers).
</Note>

## How Ops handles Autopilot PM requests

Ops talks with PMs mostly through SMS and webchat. When an Autopilot PM asks Ops to take an action or update a work order, Ops follows these rules:

| **Situation** | **What Ops does** |
| --- | --- |
| The PM reports an **emergency or urgent** issue | Takes action right away. Doesn't send a help guide. |
| The PM says they're **blocked** or have a limitation | Helps them directly and takes the action for them. Doesn't question the limitation or send a help guide. Documents the action and the reason. |
| The PM is **unsure how to do it** (no blocker, not an emergency) | Sends the relevant help guide and offers to walk them through it. If there's no help guide, takes the action for them and requests a new help article. |

If the PM is frustrated with this or has trouble with the self-service tools, Ops escalates to PS through the **PS Escalation** workflow in `#ps-escalation`, which creates a ticket in Wavelength.

## Step-by-step guide

<Tip>
  This is an Ops escalation, so follow [SOP: Handling Ops Escalations](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Ops-Escalations) for creating the PM email and closing the Ops ticket. The steps below cover what's specific to Autopilot.
</Tip>

1. **Review the escalation.** Read the Escalation Summary from Ops and the work order activity.
2. **Review the conversation.** Go to the PM user's Latchel profile and review the chat transcript or call recording to understand:
   - The context
   - The tone (frustration, confusion, etc.)
   - What the PM asked Ops to do, and which help guide Ops shared
3. **Flag any Ops concerns.** If Ops didn't handle the conversation well or didn't share the most relevant help guide, submit it through the **Agent Feedback** workflow in `#svc-ops-feedback`.
4. **Email the PM.** In Wavelength, create a new email with **Escalation via Email** and insert the **Autopilot Explanation** template.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2025-09-30 at 1.05.28 PM.png](/images/PST-2.png)
</Frame>

5. **Fill in the CONTEXT section** of the template with the details from the PM's conversation with Ops.
6. **Send the email.** Then update Ops and close the Ops escalation ticket per [SOP: Handling Ops Escalations](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Ops-Escalations).

<Warning>
  **If the PM pushes back and gets frustrated**, escalate the ticket to the CSM with the **New Escalation from PS** workflow in `#csm-escalations`. See [SOP: Escalating Tickets to the CSM](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Escalating-Tickets-to-the-CSM).
</Warning>
