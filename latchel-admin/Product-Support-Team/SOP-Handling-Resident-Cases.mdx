<Note>
  **Ops owns all resident conversations.** PS doesn't reply to residents directly. Residents are the PM's customers, and Ops handles their questions day to day, including work orders, dashboard access and benefits.
</Note>

## A resident ticket lands in the PS queue

1. Don't reply to the resident.
2. Reassign the ticket to the **Operations Team Email** teamspace in Wavelength. Ops will take it from there, including looping in the PM if needed.

## Ops escalates a resident question to PS

Ops may escalate when they don't know the answer, for example through the **Benefits Review** workflow for benefits questions.

1. Find the answer (check the SOP, FAQ or account settings that apply).
2. **Reply to Ops in the Slack thread** with the information they need. Ops passes it on to the resident.
3. If the answer depends on the PM (their settings, approvals or policies), say so in the thread so Ops can loop in the PM.
