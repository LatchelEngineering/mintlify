<Note>
  **PS doesn't reply to residents directly.** Residents are the PM's customers. Ops owns resident conversations about maintenance, and anything else goes to the PM.
</Note>

## Where does the resident's ticket go?

| **The resident's ticket is about...** | **Send it to** |
| --- | --- |
| **Maintenance** (a work order, scheduling, dashboard access, benefits, etc.) | **Ops.** Reassign the ticket to the **Operations Team Email** teamspace in Wavelength. |
| **Anything not maintenance related** (rent, lease, move-out, deposits, etc.) | **The PM.** Follow the steps below. |
| **A work order delay caused by the PM** (waiting on PM approval, PM never responded, PM-side hold) | **The PM.** Follow the steps below. |

## Sending a resident's question to the PM

### Step 1: Find the resident's profile

1. Search Latchel for the resident by **email address**.
2. Check **Disabled** in the search so deactivated accounts show up too.

### Step 2: Find the resident's PM company

1. On the resident's profile (**Details** tab), click the **Property** listed.
2. On the property page, scroll to the **Details** tab.
3. In the **Property Manager** section, note the **PM Company**.

### Step 3: Find a PM contact email

1. Find the PM company's **Primary User**.
2. Check the **User Groups** section for the **Default User Group**.
3. Pick the best contact, usually the Primary User or the Default User Group. See [How to: Locate the "Default Notification User Group" contact(s)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Locate-the-Default-Notification-User-Group-contacts).

### Step 4: Email the PM

1. Email the PM contact from Wavelength. Include the resident's name, the property, the resident's question and, for a delay, the work order link and what's waiting on the PM.
2. Close the ticket. See [SOP: Closing Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Closing-Cases-in-Product-Support).

## Ops escalates a resident question to PS

Ops may escalate when they don't know the answer, for example through the **Benefits Review** workflow for benefits questions.

1. Find the answer (check the SOP, FAQ or account settings that apply).
2. **Reply to Ops in the Slack thread** with the information they need. Ops passes it on to the resident.
3. If the answer depends on the PM (their settings, approvals or policies), say so in the thread.
