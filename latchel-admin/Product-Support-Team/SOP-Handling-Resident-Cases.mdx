<Note>
  **Purpose:** How to handle tickets from residents who belong to a PM company. Residents are the PM's customers, so most resident tickets go to the PM to answer. PS only replies to the resident directly in the approved scenarios below.
</Note>

## When PS can reply to the resident directly

| **Scenario** | **What to do** |
| --- | --- |
| **Resident-responsible work order** | Follow [How to: Handle Resident Responsible WOs](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Resident-Responsible-WOs). Resident-responsible requests are directed to Amenify. See [How To: Respond to Amenify Inquiries](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Benefits-Partnerships/How-To-Respond-to-Amenify-inquiries). |
| **Resident can't get to their dashboard** | If the resident profile is **active**, send them their [Public Dashboard URL](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-How-to-Locate-Public-Dashboard-URLs) after confirming their identity. |
| **Resident benefits question** | Follow the benefit's FAQ in [Latchel Resident Benefits](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Latchel-Resident-Benefits). |

<Warning>
  **Everything else goes to the PM.** If the resident's question isn't one of the scenarios above, follow the steps below to send it to their PM.
</Warning>

## Step 1: Find the resident's profile

1. Search Latchel for the resident by **email address**.
2. Check **Disabled** in the search so deactivated accounts show up too.

## Step 2: Find the resident's PM company

1. On the resident's profile (**Details** tab), click the **Property** listed.
2. On the property page, scroll to the **Details** tab.
3. In the **Property Manager** section, note the **PM Company**.

## Step 3: Find a PM contact email

1. Find the PM company's **Primary User**.
2. Check the **User Groups** section for the **Default User Group**.
3. Pick the best contact, usually the Primary User or the Default User Group. See [How to: Locate the "Default Notification User Group" contact(s)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Locate-the-Default-Notification-User-Group-contacts).

## Step 4: Send the resident's question to the PM

1. In Wavelength, use the **Resident Inquiry for PM** template to email the PM contact.
2. Close the ticket. See [SOP: Closing Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Closing-Cases-in-Product-Support).
