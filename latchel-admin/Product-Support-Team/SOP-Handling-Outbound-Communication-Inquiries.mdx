<Note>
  How to handle PM tickets about the calls, texts and emails Latchel sends: **requests** for a new communication, **complaints** about how often they hear from us, and **issues** where a communication didn't arrive.
</Note>

## Requests

_Examples: "Call me for X," "Text me when X happens."_

1. **Check whether existing settings can do it:**
   - **User groups and notification settings:** who gets which notifications. See [Communication & Notification Settings](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Communication-Notification-Settings-Overview).
   - **Emergency escalation contacts and contact rotations:** who we call for emergencies. These are admin only, so PS makes the changes. See [Account Settings](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/account-settings) and [Job Aid: On-Call URLs](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-On-Call-URLs-Intake-Outcome-vs-Contact-Rotation).
   - **Pre-dispatch instructions and critical notes:** see [SOP: Pre-Dispatch Instructions & Critical Notes](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Pre-Dispatch-Instructions-Critical-Notes).
   - **Action Sets:** for communications the standard settings don't cover. See [Job Aid: Top PM Requested Action Sets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Top-PM-Requested-Action-Sets).
2. **Nothing covers it?** Treat it as a feature request. See [How to Handle: Feature Requests](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Handle-Feature-Requests).

## Complaints

_Examples: "You contact us too often," "We don't hear enough."_

1. Email the PM with the **Outbound Comm Complaint** template in Wavelength to get the details: which communications, to whom, and what they'd prefer.
2. Based on their answer, follow the **Requests** steps above to adjust their settings.

## Issues and bugs

_Example: "I'm not getting notified when X happens."_

1. **Troubleshoot first:**
   - **Email:** [SOP: Handling Email Delivery Issues (SendGrid)](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Email-Delivery-Issues-SendGrid)
   - **Texts and calls:** [Job Aid: Using the Twilio Console (Logs & Phone Numbers)](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Searching-and-Analyzing-Messaging-Logs-in-Twilio)
   - Also confirm the user is in the right user group and has the notification turned on.
2. **Still not working?** Report it in `#bug-report` with the **Bug Report** workflow.
