<Note>
  **Objective:** This SOP covers how to manage new tickets in **Wavelength (WL)**, formerly called Assembly. It covers prioritizing the queue, classifying tickets, handling spam and non-actionable emails, and working tickets escalated by Latchel Ops.

  **Before you start:** review [Job Aid: Wavelength PS Automation Explained](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Assembly-Case-Automation-Explained) to understand how each ticket status affects the PS queue.
</Note>

### **Ticket Overview**

- A ticket is one unit of work for a PS associate. A ticket is created when:
  - someone emails [**success@latchel.com**](mailto:success@latchel.com), or
  - a Latchel team member submits the escalation form in [**#ps-escalation**](https://join.slack.com/share/enQtMTAzMDcxODM5NzMxMjQtODFjNWVkNGFlZTYxYzQyYzQwNDg5MTc2ZDJhZTU0Y2RkZDA4NTAwODBhNjBmZDgzMTA1ZjQ0MGNmNTIyOGE5NQ) (Ops escalations and Latchel Sync Requests).
- The sender automatically gets a confirmation that we received their message.
- New tickets land in the [**Product Support teamspace**](https://www.dashboard.askassembly.app/teams/0193894a-1308-78ee-8fdd-bd53cd8b90fb/issues) with the status **Needs Response**. This is your main working queue.
- Tickets aren't assigned automatically. Assign a ticket to yourself before you start working it.

---

### **Prioritizing the Queue**

At the start of your shift, work tickets in this order:

##### (1) Breaching (highest priority)

- Our first-response SLA is **8 business hours**. A ticket moves to **Breaching** at **6 business hours**, so you still have time to respond before the SLA is missed. Handle these **immediately**.

<Frame>
  ![Breaching tickets in the queue](/images/PST-33.png)
</Frame>

##### (2) Needs Response

This is your **main working queue** during your shift.

1. **Start with brand-new tickets** that haven't had a human response yet. Answer these first to meet the **8-business-hour first-response SLA**.
   - You will want to focus on the Created Date/Time. 
   - Tickets close to the SLA show the elapsed time and how close they are to breaching.

    className="mx-auto"
  />
</Frame>

2. **Then work oldest to newest** by date and time opened. 
   - For each ticket, decide whether it needs a response or follow-up, or can be closed.

<Check>
  **Keep an eye on the queue.** New tickets can arrive at any time. Go back to new tickets as they come in so nothing gets close to the SLA.
</Check>

##### (3) Escalated

- Escalated means PS is waiting on another Latchel department.
- If a ticket stays in **Escalated** for **3 days**, it moves back to **Needs Response** automatically with this internal note: _"PS Reminder for this Escalated Ticket! Check Slack URLs, Linked ASM Tickets, etc. and follow-up as needed!"_ Check the Slack thread, linked ASM tickets and any other follow-ups.
  - For Non-PS Escalations (product, bug, finance, etc.), tag the POC if there has not been a response. 
  - For DDs, you can move the ticket back to Escalated if the DD is not completed yet. 
  - For DS, you can tag DS to ask progress/status. 

<Info>
  For all escalated tickets, it is important to check in with the customer and let them know we are still working on their issue and will be sure to reach out as soon as we have the info needed. 
</Info>

##### (4) Open

- Work these by **last reply date and time, oldest first**. This queue is sorted with the most recent reply at the top, so start at the bottom.

### **Working a Ticket**

**Step 1.** Identify if it is an actionable ticket. <br />Review the ticket and take the right action to resolve it. There are 3 types: <br /><br />Actionable Tickets: 

- **Tickets from customers** come from a [non-@latchel.com](mailto:non-@latchel.com) email and are a normal typed message, without the Ops escalation form. Reply to the customer directly.
- CSMs and/or Leadership can also loop in PS by CC'ing [success@latchel.com](mailto:success@latchel.com) to take over the thread. 

Ops Escalations: 

- **Ops escalations** come in through the #ps-escalation form. Follow [SOP: Handling Ops Escalations](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Ops-Escalations)
  - Before you close an Ops escalation, send the **PS \<> Ops Follow-Along Escalation Thread** template with the link to the email thread with the PM, so Ops can follow along.

<Frame>
  ![Customer profile](/images/PST-37.png)
</Frame>

##### Spam/Not Actionable

- **Spam:** unsolicited messages such as junk mail, ads, or emails sent to harass or fish for a response.
- **Non-actionable:** automated replies (for example, out-of-office), system notifications, survey emails, or invalid escalations where no action is needed.

For both:

1. Set the Category to **Not Actionable**. No Attributes or Tags are needed.
2. Change the status to **Closed – No Workflow**. This doesn't send the CSAT survey.

<Frame>
  ![Close as Closed – No Workflow](/images/PST-39.png)
</Frame>

Ops Escalations are actionable tickets but follow a different process than tickets that are directly inbound to [success@latchel.com](mailto:success@latchel.com) - Follow [SOP: Handling Ops Escalations](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Ops-Escalations)<br /><br />For Inbound Actionable - Follow

##### Step 1. Assign yourself as the ticket owner, then start your timer.

<Frame>
  ![Assign yourself and start the timer](/images/PST-36.png)
</Frame>

##### Step 2. Link the customer and classify the ticket.

- Under **Customer Profile**, search for and select the company that goes with the sender's email.
- Classify the ticket with **Category**, **Attributes** and **Tags**. See [Job Aid: Ticket Category, Attributes, and Tags](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Interaction-Tags-Explained).
  - **Category** = how the ticket reached us. Pick one.
  - **Attributes** = what it's about (Topic), and why it happened (Root Cause) if something went wrong.
  - **Tags** = the outcome. Pick every tag that applies.

<Info>
  You will want to update and check these as you work on the ticket - When closing a ticket, there may be additions or adjustments needed. 
</Info>

##### Step 3. INVESTIGATE

Accuracy and quality over speed - We want are the product experts!! 

##### Step 4. RESPOND

### **Asking the PSM for Help (#psm-escalate)**

At any point in a ticket, you may need help deciding next steps. Use this process.

##### Step 1. Review the ticket and gather the details.

- Vet the request, ask the customer clarifying questions, and collect everything you need for full context.
- Think about the questions the PSM will ask you when you post in [#psm-escalate](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/C05L70GA77D).

##### Step 2. Fill out the PSM Ticket Escalate workflow in [#psm-escalate](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/C05L70GA77D).

<Frame>
  ![ASM Ticket Escalate workflow](/images/PST-40.png)
</Frame>

- **ASM URL:** the WL ticket link.
- **ASM Ticket Label:** the ticket's Attribute (topic).
- **If it's an integration issue, which PMS? (optional):** the PM's property management system.
- **Company Name:** the PM company or SP company you're working with.
- **PM Subscription:** the PM's active subscription, product package and attached products.
- **Company URL:** the company's link in the Latchel admin.
- **WO Link (if applicable):** the work order link, or "N/A".
- **Company Profile URL:** the profile link for the PM or SP the ticket is about.
- **Escalation Summary:** the customer's question or issue, with full context, details and any Slack links.
- **What research have you done? What did you find?**
  - Which settings did you check? Which SOPs did you review? Which help guides apply?
  - Paste the help guide and SOP links that are most relevant and OK to share with the customer. Start with the [Help Center Reference Guide](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Help-Center-Reference-Guide).
  - If there's no help guide for the situation, report it using [How to: Report Issues with the Latchel Help Center](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Report-Issues-with-the-Latchel-Help-Center).
  - If there's no SOP, request one in [**#svc-ops-requests**](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/C026G58ENE7) with the **Ops SOP Requires Update** workflow (teamspace: PS Mgmt).
- **What's your question?** What are you stuck on? How would **you** respond, and what do **you** think the answer is?

##### Step 4. Paste the Slack thread link into the ticket as an INTERNAL NOTE.

- If the ticket needs another Latchel department, follow [SOP: Latchel Department Escalation Process](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Latchel-Department-Escalation-Process-for-Product-Support-Cases).
