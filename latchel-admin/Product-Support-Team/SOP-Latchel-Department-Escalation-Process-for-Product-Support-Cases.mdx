<Note>
  **Objective**: This SOP outlines the process for Product Support (PS) associates to escalate customer questions or concerns to the appropriate Latchel department, ensuring efficient handling, tracking, and communication during escalations.
</Note>

### **1. Purpose of Escalation**

Customer inquiries may require escalation to other Latchel departments for additional clarity or next steps. Before escalating, PS must gather all relevant information so the other department can pick it up quickly and resolve it.

### **2. Pre-Escalation Preparation**

Before escalating any issue, make sure you have **everything the other department needs**:

- **Get all the details.** Ask the customer for any information you're missing about their inquiry or issue.
- **Do your research.** Cross-reference our internal SOPs and help guides first.
- **Know what you're asking.** Be clear on the question or request before you post it.

### **3. Escalating to a Latchel Department**

1. **Select the correct Slack channel and workflow.**
   - Refer to [Job Aid: Slack Channels & Escalation Workflows](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Slack-Channels-Escalation-Workflows) to make sure you're using the right channel.
2. **Fill out the workflow (or message) thoroughly.**

   - Fill out every field, and include the context and details the department needs: the Wavelength ticket link, work order link, company, and a clear summary of the issue.

<Warning>
  **Set the other department up for success.** Give them full context, but **don't be too wordy.** Stick to the details of the ticket so the other team doesn't waste time or go down the wrong path.
</Warning>

3. **Add the Slack link to the Wavelength ticket as an internal note.**
   - After submitting, click the three dots in the upper-right corner of the Slack message and select **Copy Link**.
   - Paste the link into the Wavelength ticket as an **internal note**.

### **4. Letting the Customer Know**

After escalating internally, let the customer know. Keep it simple, for example:

> _We've escalated your situation to our [XYZ] team, and they're looking into it. If we have any follow-up questions or need anything else from you, we'll let you know. If you notice anything else in the meantime, feel free to send it our way so we can share it with the team investigating._

Then move the ticket to **Escalated** in Wavelength.

### **5. Following Up on the Escalation**

1. **Monitor your Slack escalation post.**
   - If the department responds **before** the ticket moves back to Needs Response, take their answer and respond to the customer right away so we can close tickets faster.
2. **Check in when the ticket comes back.**
   - Every **3 days**, an Escalated ticket automatically moves back to **Needs Response**. This is your reminder to check the Slack escalation link, see where things stand and follow up if needed.
3. **Push things along.**
   - If you haven't received a response, you're empowered to tag the point of contact, the escalation department team or the department head in the channel to follow up.
4. **Document every follow-up.**
   - Add the Slack URL of each follow-up to the Wavelength ticket as an **internal note**.
5. **Keep the customer up to date.**
   - Even a quick check-in helps, for example: _"We're still working on it and will let you know as soon as we have more information."_
6. **Close the loop.**
   - Once the department responds, share the details, next steps or resolution with the customer, then work the ticket to close. See [SOP: Closing Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Closing-Cases-in-Product-Support).

### **6. Special Escalated Tickets**

Some escalations don't have a defined resolution timeline. Handle them this way:

| **Type** | **How to handle it** |
| --- | --- |
| **Data Team Bulk Updates** | There's no set timeline. **Leave the ticket open** until the update is done, then let the customer know it's complete. |
| **Feature Requests** | Use the **PM Feature Request** template in Wavelength as your starting point. It doesn't commit us to a timeline, since we may or may not build every request. It lets the PM know that while we can't promise immediate results, we take their suggestions seriously and will carefully consider them. See [How to Handle: Feature Requests](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Handle-Feature-Requests). |
| **Bug Reports: not impacting daily workflow, or a workaround exists** | Let the customer know our investigation team is on it and share the workaround. **Don't give a specific timeframe.** |
| **Bug Reports: impacting daily workflow** | **Leave the ticket open** until we have a resolution. Do not close these. |

---

#### **Key Reminders**:

- Always include the correct ticket details when escalating, and keep it concise.
- Add every Slack escalation and follow-up link to the ticket as an **internal note**.
- Keep communication with the customer clear and consistent throughout the process.
