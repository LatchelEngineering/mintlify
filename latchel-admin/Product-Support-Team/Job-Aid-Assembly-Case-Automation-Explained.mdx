<Note>
  This job aid explains the automations in **Wavelength (WL)**, formerly called Assembly: the templates it sends for you, and how each ticket status moves in the PS queue. "Case" and "ticket" mean the same thing here.
</Note>

## Template automation

### New tickets

As soon as a ticket is created (an email reaches [success@latchel.com](mailto:success@latchel.com) or a #ps-escalation form is submitted), the sender automatically receives:

<Tip>
  _Thank you for reaching out today! Your request has been received by Latchel. Our team will review your inquiry and respond to you within one business day._

  _If you are reaching out in regards to an urgent maintenance work order request, please contact our Operations Team directly via the Latchel WebChat Support or by texting (206) 717-8374._

  _Thank you!_

  _To add additional comments, reply to this email._
</Tip>

### Closed tickets

When a ticket moves to **Closed**, WL automatically sends, 24 hours later, the **"How did we do?"** CSAT survey. **Closed – No Workflow** does **not** send it. See [SOP: Closing Cases in Product Support](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Closing-Cases-in-Product-Support).

## Ticket status automation

| Status | What it means | What happens automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Breaching** | The ticket is getting close to the 8-business-hour first-response SLA. Handle it first. | Tickets move here automatically at 6 business hours. |
| **Needs Response** | Your main working queue: new tickets, tickets where the other party just replied, and tickets that moved back for a check-in (from Escalated or the unresponsive workflow). | A new email from the other party moves any ticket here. |
| **Escalated** | Escalated to a Latchel internal department (for example, Engineering, Accounts Receivable or Data Support). | After **3 days**, moves back to Needs Response with the note _"PS Reminder for this Escalated Ticket! Check Slack URLs, Linked ASM Tickets, etc. and follow-up as needed!"_ |
| **Open** | A holding cell: PS has responded and no action is needed right now. | Moves to Needs Response when the customer replies, or after **24 hours** with no reply. |
| **Closed** | Resolved, or the customer stayed unresponsive after the unresponsive follow-up template. | Sends the CSAT survey 24 hours later. A new customer email reopens it into Needs Response. |
| **Closed – No Workflow** | No customer action needed: spam, duplicates, the original #ps-escalation tickets. | No survey. A new customer email reopens it into Needs Response. |

These are the only statuses in the PS teamspace.
