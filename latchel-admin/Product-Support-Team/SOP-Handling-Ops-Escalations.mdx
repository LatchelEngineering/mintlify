<Note>
  Service Ops associates may need help from the Product Support team to resolve complex customer inquiries. Ops submits these through the **PS Escalation** workflow in the [**#ps-escalation**](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/C08475CCBGR) Slack channel, which creates a ticket in the **Product Support teamspace in Wavelength (WL)**. This SOP walks you through how to spot an Ops escalation and how to handle it.

  - To learn more about the Ops escalation path, refer to [How to: Escalate to Product Support](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/x/JQCJW).
</Note>

#### Workflows in #ps-escalation

Every submission in #ps-escalation creates a ticket in the Product Support teamspace. There are several workflows in the channel, and each one is handled differently:

| **Workflow** | **Who uses it** | **How PS handles it** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PS Escalation** | Ops | The main Ops escalation, and a large share of the PS queue. Follow the steps in this SOP. |
| **Benefits Response Review** | Ops | Ops needs help answering a resident's benefits question. See [Benefits Response Review](#benefits-response-review) below. |
| **Failed Escalation Report** | Ops | Follow [SOP: Sending Proactive Failed Escalation Emails](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Sending-Proactive-Failed-Escalation-Emails). |
| **Latchel Sync Request** | CSMs only | Ops agents should not use this workflow. See [SOP: Running AppFolio Sync on Behalf of Customers](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Running-AppFolio-Sync-on-Behalf-of-Customers). |

#### How to spot an escalation from Latchel Ops

The ticket comes from the **PS Escalation** workflow in #ps-escalation and includes these details from Ops:

- **Submitter** - the Ops associate who submitted the escalation.
- **Customer Type** - SP, PM, Owner or Resident.
- **Did you share a Help Guide?** - which help guide Ops already shared, if any.
- **Escalation Summary** - what Ops needs help with.
- **Is this related to a specific work order?** - the work order link, if applicable.
- **Name of the person that reached out** - with their Latchel profile URL.
- **Source of the Escalation** - Call, SMS, Email or Webchat.
- **What is their primary email address?**
- **Work Order on Hold Due to Pending PS Resolution?** - Yes or No.

#### Benefits Response Review

Ops should handle all communication with residents about their benefits. When Ops receives a resident benefits question they don't know how to answer, they submit the **Benefits Response Review** workflow. The goal is for Ops to build their own SOPs and eventually own this category without PS. See [How to Handle Home Assistant and Benefits Inquiries](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Home-Assistant-and-Benefits-Inquiries).

1. Find the answer, then **reply on the original ticket** with the details Ops needs to answer the resident (e.g., _"Ops, here is the answer…"_).
2. Close the ticket as **Closed – No Workflow** so Ops doesn't receive the CSAT survey.

<Warning>
  **Reply outwardly, not as an internal note.** An outward reply posts back to the Slack thread and notifies the submitter. An internal note only shows on the ticket and never reaches Slack.
</Warning>

<Info>
  If Ops has a follow-up question, they reply in the Slack thread and the ticket reopens automatically.
</Info>

### Step-by-Step Guide

#### Step 1. Thoroughly review the escalation

<Warning>
  **Do not take the Escalation Summary at face value.** Ops may copy and paste the customer's wording, or ask for a specific fix when there's a better solution than what they asked for. Before you reach out to anyone, make sure you understand what the escalation truly is.
</Warning>

Review everything that applies:

- The **Escalation Summary** from Ops.
- The **work order activity** (if a work order is shared).
- The **Intake Settings**.
- **Pre-Dispatch Instructions, Critical Notes and Emergency Escalation Contacts**.
- **Action Sets** on the account.
- Other **settings** related to the escalation, such as prioritized service providers, the subscription, owner settings and portfolios.
- **Transcripts, calls and conversations** with the person who reached out. Go to that person's Latchel profile and review the chat transcript.

#### Step 2. Confirm who the escalation is about

Open the Latchel profile URL from the escalation form. The user type appears at the top of the profile page. Don't rely only on the Customer Type Ops selected. Handle the ticket according to the SOP for that user type:

- **Property Manager:** continue to Step 3. Most Ops escalations are from PMs.
- **Service Provider:** refer to [Handling SP Cases](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/x/EICmvg).
- **Resident:** refer to [SOP: Handling Resident Cases](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Resident-Cases).
- **Owner:** refer to [SOP: Handling Non-RentRedi or Non-Nomad Owner Cases](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Non-RentRedi-or-Non-Nomad-Owner-Cases).
- **RentRedi Owner:** refer to [SOP: Handling RentRedi Cases](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-RentRedi-Cases).
- **Nomad Owner:** refer to [SOP: Handling Nomad Cases](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Nomad-Cases).

<Tip>
  Some Service Provider and Resident escalations don't need PS to reach out at all. If Ops just needs an answer, push it back to Ops on the original ticket with the information they need.
</Tip>

#### Step 3. Classify the Ops escalation ticket

Assign yourself to the ticket and classify it (see [Job Aid: Ticket Category, Attributes, and Tags](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Interaction-Tags-Explained)). Set the Category to **Latchel Ops Escalation**.

#### Step 4. Create a new email in Wavelength

In the ticket's thread feed, click **\+ Create**, then choose **Escalation via Email**.

  <img
    src="/images/PST-43.png"
    alt="+ Create, then Escalation via Email"

  />
</Frame>

<Note>
  - This option starts a **brand-new email thread**. It does not include any past correspondence.
  - A pop-up opens where you draft the email, and it can block your view of the ticket. Open the ticket in a **second window** so you can keep reviewing the Ops escalation details while you write.
</Note>

#### Step 5. Address the email

- **From:** change the sender to [**success@latchel.com**](mailto:success@latchel.com).
- **To:** the person who reached out, using the name and primary email address from the escalation form.
- **CC (when needed):** if urgent action is required, you need the PM's help, or there's an issue with an intake setting or work order, check the PM company account for a group inbox (for example, maintenance@, team@ or a shared team email). CC it for extra visibility.
- **Subject:** use a subject that makes sense for the escalation, for example _"123 Main St - Intake Settings Question"_.

#### Step 6. Compose and send the email

Insert the Wavelength template **"Clarify Ops Escalation w/ PM"** from the bottom of the email composition box, then fill it out with the relevant details.

<Warning>
  **This is a blind outreach.** It's a brand-new email thread, so the PM has no context from us yet. Always recap why we're reaching out.
</Warning>

- Open with a greeting, such as _"I hope you're doing well."_
- Recap the reason for the email, for example:
  - _"We received your question about work order XYZ. Here's why this happened and what we found."_
  - _"We received your request to update XYZ. Here's what we've done."_
- **If you need more clarity,** ask **open-ended questions**. Probe for details so we know the best path to take.

Add your signature, then click **Escalate** to send.

#### Step 7. Update Ops and close the Ops escalation ticket

Once sent, the new email thread is linked to the Ops escalation ticket.

1. Open the linked escalation and copy the link to the **PM-facing thread**.
2. Go back to the Ops escalation ticket and insert the **PS \<> Ops Follow-Along Escalation Thread** template.
3. Paste the PM-facing thread link into the template and send it as an outward reply. It posts back to the Slack thread, so if Ops has questions or the person reaches out again, they can use the link to see the context and where things stand.
4. Close the Ops escalation ticket as **Closed – No Workflow** so Ops doesn't receive the CSAT survey.

<Info>
  From here, the **PM-facing thread** is the real ticket where you communicate with the PM. For the PM, it's a brand-new email thread.
</Info>

#### Step 8. Work the PM thread to close

Based on how the case goes, follow either:

- Responsive PM - [SOP: Closing Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Closing-Cases-in-Product-Support)
- Unresponsive PM - [SOP: Managing Unresponsive Property Managers](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Managing-Unresponsive-Property-Managers-in-Case-Management)

<Warning>
  If the escalation form says **Yes** to "Work Order on Hold Due to Pending PS Resolution?", PS needs to:

  - Email [team@latchel.com](mailto:team@latchel.com) that the situation was resolved and how the WO should proceed.
