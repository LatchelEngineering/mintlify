## How the audit works

- **Every week**, a report pulls a **random sample of 3 closed Wavelength tickets per associate** from the most recent Monday–Sunday report period.
- Each ticket is scored on **13 items** in five categories using the [PS Quality Audit form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSegLUzj7BaRwtAEri7TAtyfJOvC3PU5dHHSlwMuQmOf06m7pg/viewform).
- Scores land in the audit workbook, which calculates each category and an overall %. Individual and team scores are shared with each associate directly, not on this page.
- Any **No** or **Partial** that can still be fixed gets a **follow-up** with a deadline.

<Warning>
  **Verify, don't skim.** A thread-only review isn't an audit. Open every work order, Slack thread, admin record and help guide the ticket mentions, and check each claim against the record **as it was when the reply went out**. If you can't open a source, mark that item unverified instead of guessing.
</Warning>

## Scoring

Each item is scored **Full**, **Partial**, **No** or **N-A** (not every item offers all four).

| **Score** | **Points** |
| --- | --- |
| Full | 1.0 |
| Partial | 0.5 |
| No | 0 |
| N-A | Left out of that category's average |

**Overall score = A × 35% + B × 20% + C × 15% + D × 20% + E × 10%**

Each category score is the average of its items (N-A items excluded). Don't flat-average all 13 items.

## The 13 items

### A. Answer & Solve (35%) · Tenets 3 & 4

| **Item** | **Full** | **Partial** | **No** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A1. Addressed every question or request in the message?** | All requests answered | Some addressed, at least one missed | Answered the wrong problem |
| **A2. Accurate info and action per SOP and product?** | Correct | Minor error or omission | Materially wrong |
| **A3. Resolved (or a clear plan with owner and timeframe), root cause addressed, not closed early?** | Resolved or clear plan | Surface fix only, or vague plan | Unresolved, or created a new issue |

<Tip>
  **DD conclusion emails:** judge A3 against the [ACE framework](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/DD-Customer-Conclusion-Email-Framework-ACE).
</Tip>

### B. Reduce Customer Effort (20%) · Tenet 2

| **Item** | **Full** | **Partial** | **No** | **N-A** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **B1. Clear, concise, no acronyms or jargon?** | Clear, acronyms spelled out | Wordy, or one unexplained acronym | Confusing | — |
| **B2. Reduced effort (shared a help guide, link or screenshot, or did the action)?** | Resource or action provided | — | Put avoidable work back on the customer | None needed |

### C. Treat as a Friend: Empathy & Tone (15%) · Tenet 1

| **Item** | **Full** | **Partial** | **No** | **N-A** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **C1. Empathetic, friendly, positive tone?** | Acknowledges the customer, positive framing | Polite but flat | Curt or dismissive | — |
| **C2. If we erred or the customer was frustrated, owned it?** | Genuine acknowledgment | — | Deflected or ignored | No error or frustration |

### D. Compliance & Escalation (20%) · SOPs + Tenet 5

| **Item** | **Full** | **Partial** | **No** | **N-A** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **D1. Correct ticket tag?** | Correct | — | Wrong or missing | — |
| **D2. 24-hour first-response SLA met?** | Met | — | Missed | — |
| **D3. If escalated: right channel, and link documented in the ticket?** | Right channel, link and clear details | Missing link or detail | Wrong channel or not documented | No escalation |
| **D4. PM company name present and correct in the Customer Profile?** | Present and correct | — | Missing or wrong | No profile applies |

### E. Proactivity & Follow-through (10%)

| **Item** | **Full** | **Partial** | **No** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **E1. Set expectations and next steps, and kept promises (or bias for action if the customer went quiet)?** | Set and followed through | Vague | Left hanging, or no action when it was needed |
| **E2. Surfaced product or process feedback (bug, feature request, doc gap) when warranted?** | Raised, or genuinely none needed | — | A clear gap was missed |

## Calibration: how hard to grade

- **Save "No" for substantive failures.** Wording, tone and formatting nitpicks go in the feedback notes, not the score.
- **A2 is where factual slips land.** A wrong fact or materially incomplete product info that the customer could act on is A2. An unlabeled timestamp or awkward wording is B1, not A2.
- **A1 Partial means a real question went unanswered,** not a side comment buried in a forwarded chain. A resident's request that's correctly sent to their PM counts as answered.
- **C2:** use N-A when Latchel didn't make an error and the customer wasn't visibly frustrated. Use Full when we erred and the associate owned it.
- **D3 on escalation replies:** when the ticket is itself the reply to an Ops escalation, D3 is usually N-A. Score it when the associate escalated onward (for example, to the CSM, a reseller, or `#pd_cn_actionset`) with the Slack link in an internal note.
- **Know the customer before you score.** Is it a PM client, a RentRedi or Nomad owner, a resident, or a service provider? Several SOPs (CSM handoffs, who we reply to directly) depend on it.
- **Check the SOP the associate was following,** and its neighbor. Similar-sounding processes work differently (for example, LCN blacklisting vs. the LVN being all or nothing).

## D2: the 24-hour first-response clock

- **Starts** when the ticket reaches PS: the Ops escalation is created, the customer's email arrives, or (for Latchel-initiated work) the internal Slack request is posted.
- **Business days only.** Skip weekends and office closures, keeping the time of day: Friday 3:00 PM → Monday 3:00 PM.
- **After-hours arrivals** start the clock at **8:00 AM CST** the next business day. Business hours are weekdays 8 AM–5 PM CST.
- **Counts:** the first written, human reply. The automatic acknowledgment and phone calls don't count.
- **Latchel-initiated tickets** (no inbound customer message) are D2 = Full.
- Work it out to the minute. Misses are often caused upstream (the ticket sat before it was assigned), so look for patterns across the team, not just individual misses.

## Notes, documentation check and follow-up

**What was great?** Call out specific things the associate did well, especially anything worth sharing with the team.

**What could be better?** For every No or Partial, explain what was missed and give suggested wording or the right step. Full scores can still get tips here.

**Documentation check (not scored):** Is the scenario covered by an SOP, Job Aid or Help Guide? Mark **Yes — covered**, **No — not documented**, or **Partially — outdated/unclear**. For a gap, note the topic, what's missing or wrong, the existing doc link and a suggested owner.

**Follow-up:** if any No or Partial can still be fixed, log a follow-up with a summary of the action needed and a deadline of **3 business days**. Follow-up types:

| **Type** | **Use when** |
| --- | --- |
| **Corrective Action** | The associate needs to redo a missed step (reply to an unanswered question, fix a setting, add a missing note) |
| **Bug** | The ticket revealed a product bug that wasn't reported |
| **Feature Request** | The customer's need should be logged as a feature request |
| **Follow-Up & Process Gap** | A process or documentation gap needs to be addressed |
| **Bias for Action** | The associate should have moved something forward instead of waiting |

<Warning>
  **The follow-up is an instruction the associate will act on.** Before submitting, double-check that the step is right for this customer type and that the problem is still live.
</Warning>
