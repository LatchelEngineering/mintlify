<Note>
  This SOP covers closing tickets in **Wavelength (WL)**, formerly called Assembly. 
</Note>

### **Before you close a ticket**

Make sure the ticket has:

- **Customer details:** the correct PM company is linked under Customer Profile.
- **Classification:** Category, Attributes and Tags are filled in. Accurate classification drives our reporting. See [Job Aid: Ticket Category, Attributes, and Tags](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Interaction-Tags-Explained).

  - Category is always required.
  - Attributes (Topic) is always required, except when the Category is **Not Actionable**.
  - Add a Root Cause only if something went wrong.
  - Add every outcome Tag that applies.

### **How to close a ticket**

Change the ticket status to one of these:

| Status | When to use it | CSAT survey sent? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Closed** | All actions are done and the request, issue or question is resolved. Also use this when the customer stopped responding, but only after you've sent the unresponsive follow-up templates in WL and got no reply. | **Yes** |
| **Closed – No Workflow** | No customer action is needed: spam, duplicates, and the original #ps-escalation tickets from Ops. | **No** |

<Warning>
  **Ops escalations:** before you close one, send the **PS \<> Ops Follow-Along Escalation Thread** template with the link to your email thread with the PM.
</Warning>

### **What happens after you close a ticket**

When a ticket in the **Product Support teamspace** moves to **Closed**, WL automatically emails the customer our **"How did we do?"** CSAT survey **24 hours later**. It has five faces to choose from, and responses go to the PS CSAT (Responses) sheet. This happens no matter who closed the ticket.

**Closed – No Workflow** never sends the survey.
