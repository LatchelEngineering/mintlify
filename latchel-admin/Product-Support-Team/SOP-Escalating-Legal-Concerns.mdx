<Danger>
  As soon as the client makes any of the following remarks, please escalate the issue right away. This will be the next step in handling the ticket. You do not need to communicate with the client, as the Product Support Manager (PSM) will take care of it.
</Danger>

### Escalation Scenarios against Latchel:

1. **Threatening Legal Action**: If the client threatens to pursue legal action.
2. **Filing a Police Report**: If the client mentions filing a police report.
3. **Reporting to the BBB**: If the client threatens to report Latchel to the Better Business Bureau (BBB).
4. **Other Legal Actions**: Any other scenarios that could result in legal action against the company.

#### Step-by-Step Guide:

1. Assign the Wavelength ticket to the PSM (Maddie) and move it to **Escalated**.
   - **Don't reply to the client.** The PSM handles all communication.
2. In `#psm-escalate`, fill out the **PSM Escalation** workflow. In the **Escalation Summary**:
   - Be thorough. Write it as if the reader can't open the ticket.
   - Include every communication with the client, and explain **why** you think they're making the threat.
   - Link any related Slack conversations (settings questions, Ops behavior, Finance, etc.).
3. Paste the Slack thread link into the ticket as an internal note.
4. The PSM will review the details and take over the next steps.
5. Depending on the severity of the situation, the ticket may be escalated to Will Gordon.

### **Escalation Scenarios against a Property Manager:**

- Resident: Instances where the resident reports they are going to sue their PM, report them to the Better Business Bureau (BBB), threatening legal action, etc.
- Service Provider: Instances where a service provider threatens legal action against a PM, putting a lien on a PM property, report them to the Better Business Bureau (BBB), etc.

#### Step-by-Step Guide:

1. **Access the resident/service provider profile in Latchel.**

- Search for the profile in Latchel by using their email address.
- Ensure that the “Disabled” option is checked when performing the search to include deactivated accounts.

2. **Once on the profile, identify the associated Property Management (PM) Company.**

- For Residents:
  - Once on the Resident Profile, you will automatically be on the Details tab.
  - Click on the Property listed below the Details tab.
  - On the Property page, scroll down to the Details tab.
  - Locate the Property Manager section to find the PM Company associated with the owner.
- For Service Providers:
  - Once on the SP Profile, you will automatically be on the Details tab.
  - Click on the “Preferred PMs tab”.
  - Find the PM Company in question and click on the name to go to the PM Company page.

3. **Find a PM Contact Email.**

- Identify the Primary User for the PM Company from the Property Manager information.
- Review the User Groups section for the Default User Group.
- Select the most appropriate email for reaching out to the PM, typically from the Primary User or Default User Group

4. **Contact the PM Regarding the Legal Escalation.**

- Email the PM contact from Wavelength. Include who raised the concern (resident or service provider), the property or work order, exactly what they said, and that the PM should follow up with them directly.
- Close the ticket. See [SOP: Closing Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Closing-Cases-in-Product-Support).
