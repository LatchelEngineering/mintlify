<Note>
  **Check the account setup before you change anything.** Many settings can be set at the company, portfolio or property level. Review how the PM's account is set up and read [Configuration Inheritance (Property, Portfolio & Company Level)](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Configuration-Inheritance-Property-Portfolio-Company-Level) so you update the right level.
</Note>

## Bulk update vs. small update

| **Type** | **What it means** | **Who does it** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Small update** | Takes the PM or PS **15 minutes or less** to complete. | The PM, using our help guides. PS steps in if the PM pushes back or the setting is admin only. |
| **Bulk update** | Takes the PM and/or PS **more than 15 minutes** to complete. | Data Support, through a request in `#data-support-requests`. |

To find the right help guide or process for a specific setting, see [Account Settings](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/account-settings).

## Small updates

1. **Vet the request.**
   - Ask clarifying questions until you understand exactly what the PM wants changed.
   - Confirm whether the update is for the whole account, a specific portfolio or a specific property.
   - Confirm whether it affects emergencies, non-emergencies or both.
2. **Send the PM the help guides.** Share the relevant help guides and steps so the PM can make the update themselves. Use [Account Settings](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/account-settings) to find the right guide.
3. **Make the update yourself if needed.** PS completes the update when:
   - The PM pushes back on doing it themselves, or
   - The setting is **admin only**. For example, PMs can no longer update their own **Emergency Escalation Contacts**.
4. **Let the PM know.** Once the update is done, confirm it with the PM, then close the ticket. See [SOP: Closing Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Closing-Cases-in-Product-Support).

## Bulk updates

<Note>
  **Recurring update reports:** Most PMs who send a recurring update report (weekly, monthly, etc.) already send it straight to [datateam@latchel.com](mailto:datateam@latchel.com). If one comes into PS as a ticket or email, **reassign the ticket to the Data Support teamspace** in Wavelength. You don't need to submit a Data Support Request.
</Note>

1. **Vet the request.**
   - Ask clarifying questions until you understand exactly what the PM wants changed.
   - Confirm whether the update is for the whole account, a specific portfolio or a specific property.
   - Confirm whether it affects emergencies, non-emergencies or both.
3. **Submit to Data Support.** Fill out the **Data Support Request** form in `#data-support-requests` with the details the PM sent. See [Job Aid: Slack Channels & Escalation Workflows](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Slack-Channels-Escalation-Workflows).
4. **Add the Slack link to the ticket.** Copy the link to your Slack submission and paste it into the Wavelength ticket as an **internal note**. See [SOP: Latchel Department Escalation Process](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Latchel-Department-Escalation-Process-for-Product-Support-Cases).
5. **Let the PM know and move the ticket to Escalated.** Tell the PM we've sent the request to our Data team and will let them know when it's done.
6. **Leave the ticket open until the update is done.** Bulk updates don't have a set timeline. Once Data Support confirms the update is complete, let the PM know, then close the ticket. See [SOP: Closing Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Closing-Cases-in-Product-Support).

<Warning>
  **If Data Support needs more details from the PM**, they'll ask in the Slack thread of your submission.

  1. Email the PM from the Wavelength ticket and ask for the missing details.
  2. If the PM doesn't respond, **no follow-up is needed**. Close the ticket.
  3. Close the loop with Data Support in the Slack thread. Let them know we never got the details from the PM.
  4. If the PM responds later, Data Support can reopen the ticket or open a new one, whichever they prefer.
</Warning>
