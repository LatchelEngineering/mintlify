## Purpose

</Note>

## Content

## General FAQs

**What is the Amenify benefit?**

Latchel has partnered with [Amenify](https://www.amenify.com/) to upgrade our existing Home Assistant services by offering professional cleaning (routine and move-out), food & grocery, chores and handyman services. Latchel residents gain access to $300 in Amenify Credits when they sign up ($50 at sign-up + $20 in monthly credits). Effective January 24, 2025, residents are directed to Amenify for any resident responsible work orders.

**How can I learn more about the different services Amenify offers residents?**

You’ll find more information about the services Amenify offers on their website at the following links:

1.  [https://www.amenify.com/cleaning-services](https://www.amenify.com/cleaning-services)
    
2.  [https://www.amenify.com/food-grocery-service](https://www.amenify.com/food-grocery-service)
    
3.  [https://www.amenify.com/handyman-services](https://www.amenify.com/handyman-services)
    
4.  [https://www.amenify.com/chores-services](https://www.amenify.com/chores-services)  
    
5.  [https://www.amenify.com/move-out-cleaning-services](https://www.amenify.com/move-out-cleaning-services)  
    

**Which Latchel residents are eligible for the Amenify benefit?**

Amenify is currently available to residents enrolled in Home Assistant by their Property Manager. To confirm if a PM is enrolled, see [https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2571730945/How+to+Handle+Home+Assistant+and+Benefits+Inquiries#How-can-I-check-if-a-PM-offers-Home-Assistant-to-their-residents?](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2571730945/How+to+Handle+Home+Assistant+and+Benefits+Inquiries#How-can-I-check-if-a-PM-offers-Home-Assistant-to-their-residents?).

**How do eligible residents access the Amenify benefit?**

Residents can click on the Amenify tile in their Benefits Hub to sign up.

**How do residents book services with Amenify?**

Residents can book, pay, follow up on appointments, and get support using the Amenify Resident App.

**Do residents need to provide Amenify with a credit card?**

Amenify requests a credit card from residents during sign-up. A credit card is required for residents to place their first order.

**Does Amenify contact residents? How?**

Yes, Amenify primarily reaches out to residents via email to remind them of their unused credits.

**How can residents get in touch with Amenify if they have questions about their account, credits, or services booked?**

Amenify’s dedicated Support team provides help & support 7 days a week. Residents should visit [https://www.amenify.com/contact-us](https://www.amenify.com/contact-us).

**How is Amenify different than Latchel's Home Assistant?**

They are complementary benefits! We’ve partnered with Amenify to upgrade our existing concierge services. Effective January 24, 2025, residents are directed to Amenify for any resident responsible work orders.

_For internal reference only: In the future, we may replace the Home Assistant diagnostic flow with Amenify. If/when we do, this will be communicated internally and to customers._

**Is there a new process for resident responsible work orders?**

Residents can continue to submit resident responsible work orders via Latchel. Effective January 24, 2025, residents receive an email and SMS upon submission directing them to Amenify as our trusted partner for all resident responsible work orders. These work orders are automatically cancelled in Latchel once the resident has been directed to Amenify. PMs no longer receive notifications about resident responsible work orders, but the job log includes a note that residents have been redirected to Amenify. For more information, see How to: Handle Resident Responsible WOs.  
  
**SMS residents receive upon RR WO submission:** Because this job is your responsibility, you will be responsible for payment. However, our trusted concierge partner Amenify handles all resident responsible requests and offers Latchel residents $300 in yearly credits! [Please visit this link to book your service through the Amenify app](https://m.amenify.com/sign-up?utm_source=email&utm_campaign=partner_latchel&utm_medium=marketing). This Latchel work order will be canceled. You can track the status of any Amenify requests via their app.

_For internal reference only: In the future, we may replace the Home Assistant diagnostic flow with Amenify. If/when we do, this will be communicated internally and to customers._

## PM FAQs

**How does Amenify benefit me as a Property Manager?**

This benefit allows you to:

-   Offer residents discounts on cleaning, grocery delivery, and other lifestyle services
    
-   Help keep your units clean, including offering move-out deep clean to residents to turn units faster
    
-   Offer an attractive added benefit for residents that increase opt-in rates for optional benefits users
    

**Can I submit a cleaning request on behalf of a resident?**

No, only residents can activate a service request by clicking the Amenify tile in their Benefits Hub.

**How can I get an updated resident flier and other assets that include the new Amenify benefit?**

Please visit [https://latchel.com/latchel-assets/](https://latchel.com/latchel-assets/) .

**I don't want to offer Amenify to my residents. How do I opt out?**

Visit your Benefit Hub, locate the Amenify tile, and toggle the “Active for Residents” switch off. See [this Help Article](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Modify-Custom-Resident-Benefit#how-to-modify-custom-resident-benefit) for more information. Please note, however, that residents will still be directed to Amenify for any resident responsible work orders they submit.

**Is Latchel selling our resident's information?**

Latchel does not sell resident information. Amenify is a vetted Latchel partner, and part of Amenify’s service is informing residents of their offers.

**I’m ok with Amenify being included in my hub, but I don’t want them to reach out to my residents. What can I do?**

Since Amenify only contacts residents that have the default Latchel Amenify tile active in their hub, you can disable our tile and create your own custom Amenify benefit. This will prevent your residents from being contacted by Amenify, but still allow residents to take advantage of their credits if interested. To do this:

1.  Visit your Benefit Hub, locate the Amenify tile, and toggle the “Active for Residents” switch off. See [this Help Article](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Modify-Custom-Resident-Benefit#how-to-modify-custom-resident-benefit) for more information. 
    
2.  Create a custom benefit, including [this Website link](https://m.amenify.com/sign-up?utm_source=email&utm_campaign=partner_latchel&utm_medium=marketing) and [this Image](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1UP6uDz-6kGz-yqIZMJJB9Ilf7tL5RdrG/view?usp=drive_link). See [this Help Article](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Create-New-Resident-Benefit#how-to-create-new-resident-benefit) for more information.
    

**Will I be notified before other new partnerships are enabled?**

Yes, Latchel sends an email announcement anytime a new benefit is added so you can decide if you'd prefer not to offer it to your residents.
