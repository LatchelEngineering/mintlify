# TLDR

Latchel is adding a new proprietary benefit to the Benefits hub that increases the renter experience by ensuring security deposit deductions are fair and independent. Our Industry experts will do an independent review of Resident’s security deposit charges to make sure they are lawful and market-rate which would normally cost a Resident $200 or more. This benefit is equally beneficial to property managers because it reduces disputes and friction around security deposit deductions. Only Benefits subscribers are eligible for this benefit.

# Press Release

Latchel launches a new proprietary benefit to Residents that leverages their maintenance experience and industry knowledge to provide Residents a free security deposit review that ensures a higher degree of financial security for renters. Similar independent reviews typically cost $200 (or more) and won’t even have access to all the maintenance information that Latchel has on file for the home. Residents with Latchel are moving out confidently knowing that their property manager treated them fairly and that all deductions are market-rate.

Property managers also experience huge benefits by offering Latchel’s security deposit review to renters. Managers typically spend hours handling deposit disputes and don’t have access to independent audits without paying hundreds of dollars. Even worse, if a deposit dispute ends up in a lawsuit, property managers spend hours of time and potentially **thousands of dollars** to ensure they don’t break landlord-renter laws. Latchel prevents this problem from happening by first presenting their review findings to property managers and providing independent, expert advice to ensure that property managers avoid legal issues.

Billy Crystoph, a renter at Monarch, explained how easy it was to get his deposit reviewed. “I knew I’d get hit with some charges because my cat peed on all the carpets, but the $1250 charge felt way out of line to me, so I submitted a deposit review to Latchel. In less than a week, Latchel experts had researched my deposit charges and came back with a detailed report showing me that normally I would have been charged even more than $1250. I felt a lot better about the charges knowing they were fair and that I wasn’t getting taken advantage of.”

Ross Gruble, an asset manager at Monarch, said, “Latchel does a fantastic job helping renters and the property manager solve these types of issues with their independent review. Normally my property managers would spend at least a couple hours going back and forth with the tenant. In this case, Latchel handled the review and we didn’t even have to get on a call to resolve the issue. I love when solving problems is this easy!”
