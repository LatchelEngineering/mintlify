# TLDR

Latchel is adding a new proprietary benefit to the Benefits hub that guarantees move-in maintenance completion in a 2 week time period after Property Manager approval of the work order submitted within the first 30 days after move-in. If Latchel fails to accomplish this timeline, the resident receives a $50 giftcard to Doordash. Property Managers will love this benefit because move-in maintenance is a critical part of their customer service experience. Only Benefits subscribers are eligible for this benefit.

# Press Release

Latchel launches a new proprietary benefit for Residents that promises all move-in maintenance requests made in the first 30 days after move-in are completed two weeks after approval. Renters are often stuck dealing with prolonged maintenance issues after move-in because turnover crews missed repairs. These can often take multiple weeks to wrap up. If Latchel doesn’t get these issues resolved, then renters get a $50 Doordash card.

Billy Chili, a resident at Splatoo Homes, described their move-in experience with Latchel, “I submitted 4 repair requests during my move-in week. There were a few small repair issues like one of the window latches not working properly and a slow drain in one of the sinks. Latchel scheduled the handyman and all my issues were fixed in just a couple days! It was awesome to get the repairs done so I could start settling in to my new home.”

Porky Pot, a manager at Splatoo Homes, said, “We usually get a bad online review if move-in maintenance isn’t done well. Latchel does a great job, and fortunately, when things don’t go right, they’ll send our tenants a Doordash giftcard. We’ve had a noticeable reduction of bad reviews on our Google page thanks to Latchel.”
