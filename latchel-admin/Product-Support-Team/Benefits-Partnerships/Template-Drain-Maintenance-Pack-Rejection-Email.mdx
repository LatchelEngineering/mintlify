## **Purpose**

The purpose of this document is to provide an email template to send to residents when one of their Drain Maintenance Pack Requests are denied.

## **Email Template:**

Subject: Drain Maintenance Pack Request

Hi \[**Resident First Name**\],

Thank you for submitting your Drain Maintenance Pack request to Latchel. We appreciate your efforts in completing the form.

Unfortunately, we  regret to inform you that we are unable to approve your request for the drain maintenance pack. **\[List reason we are unable to process this reimbursement**\]

If you have any questions regarding this decision, please do not hesitate to contact us. We understand this may be disappointing news for you and would be happy to discuss with you in more detail and provide further explanation that may be needed. 

Thank you for your understanding. 

Sincerely, 

\[**Your Name\]**

## **Email Example**

Subject: Drain Maintenance Pack Request

Hi Jace,

Thank you for submitting your Drain Maintenance Pack request to Latchel. We appreciate your efforts in completing the form.

Unfortunately, we  regret to inform you that we are unable to approve your request for reimbursement because you have already received a drain maintenance pack this quarter. There is a limit of one drain pack sent per calendar quarter.

If you have any questions regarding this decision, please do not hesitate to contact us. We understand this may be disappointing news for you and would be happy to discuss with you in more detail and provide further explanation that may be needed. 

Thank you for your understanding. 

Sincerely, 

Matt
