# Purpose of this Doc

The Purpose of this doc is to outline the sop for fulfillment of any drain pack request submitted by a resident in the Latchel system.

# Step-by-step Guide

1.  Resident fills out [Typeform](https://latchel.typeform.com/to/WoB5vi53?typeform-source=app.latchel.com) to request Drain Pack.
    
2.  Slack workflow automatically alerts Partnership team in the Partnerships/Perks channel and sync to [Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IFoIEIHjjWwyXAE6mmvmYle1OaRHKU9WN4Vn7ACTQ_c/edit#gid=0) for tracking.
    
3.  Partnership team will go into Latchel system to look up the property/resident
    
    1.  Partnership team verifies resident is active benny subscriber in Latchel system.
        
        1.  If resident is a benny subscriber proceed with steps below.
            
        2.  If resident is not a benny subscriber, send them [an email](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Benefits-Partnerships/Template-Drain-Maintenance-Pack-Rejection-Email) explaining that this perk is only for benefit subscribers.
            
            1.  Before doing this double check at the resident, property, and property management company level that there is no benefit subscription.
                
4.  Partnership team uses Latchel Amazon account to order a drain clearance product with drain pack Ramp card.
    
    1.  Select Drain Pack Option
        
        1.  [Drain Pack option 1](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09KRCL9SV/ref=sspa_dk_detail_1?pd_rd_i=B09KRCL9SV&pd_rd_w=o1qov&content-id=amzn1.sym.dd2c6db7-6626-466d-bf04-9570e69a7df0&pf_rd_p=dd2c6db7-6626-466d-bf04-9570e69a7df0&pf_rd_r=XWM87YN13SM0WYXYNS1C&pd_rd_wg=CNz6f&pd_rd_r=13ed7285-01b3-4b5c-8811-5f36640d20ca&s=hpc&sp_csd=d2lkZ2V0TmFtZT1zcF9kZXRhaWxfdGhlbWF0aWM&spLa=ZW5jcnlwdGVkUXVhbGlmaWVyPUExVDVTNU5EU1JOWEsmZW5jcnlwdGVkSWQ9QTAxMTk2ODBQSlJWTzc4V1RZUDYmZW5jcnlwdGVkQWRJZD1BMDQ1NTAyMDM5R01QVUJKRU1DWTAmd2lkZ2V0TmFtZT1zcF9kZXRhaWxfdGhlbWF0aWMmYWN0aW9uPWNsaWNrUmVkaXJlY3QmZG9Ob3RMb2dDbGljaz10cnVl&th=1)
            
        2.  [Drain Pack option 2](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09F9TDTSV/ref=sspa_dk_detail_2?psc=1&pd_rd_i=B09F9TDTSV&pd_rd_w=YBlfz&content-id=amzn1.sym.dd2c6db7-6626-466d-bf04-9570e69a7df0&pf_rd_p=dd2c6db7-6626-466d-bf04-9570e69a7df0&pf_rd_r=QN7JPQ4GSM5GTWSXBRQ1&pd_rd_wg=koKZw&pd_rd_r=67b819ea-6f83-4cb0-a383-e2067771e26a&s=hpc&sp_csd=d2lkZ2V0TmFtZT1zcF9kZXRhaWxfdGhlbWF0aWM&spLa=ZW5jcnlwdGVkUXVhbGlmaWVyPUEzUlU1T0ZYQ0hDNjdKJmVuY3J5cHRlZElkPUEwNDQ4NTk3Mk5CWDQ1VDlZOU1KUSZlbmNyeXB0ZWRBZElkPUEwODc4Mzk0MkczUkxVMVoyUkRTUyZ3aWRnZXROYW1lPXNwX2RldGFpbF90aGVtYXRpYyZhY3Rpb249Y2xpY2tSZWRpcmVjdCZkb05vdExvZ0NsaWNrPXRydWU=)
            
    2.  Add the residents shipping address and phone number to Amazon order
        
    3.  Make sure that the item marked as a gift
        
    4.  Add Gift Message on order ([See Appendix A)](#_6mxihb3mx5ge)
        
        1.  "Hi \[Resident\_First\_Name\], Please check your email for drain pack instructions and let us know if you have any questions.”
            
        2.  Change the “From” field to, “From: Latchel”
            
    5.  Make sure payment is the correct Ramp card
        
    6.  Complete the order and send the drain pack.
        
5.  Partnership team sends email using the [team@latchel.com](mailto:team@latchel.com) account to resident letting them know product is on it’s way
    
    1.  See [Appendix B](#_e5dhfhz2c3kp) for Email Copy.
        
    2.  Include pdf of the instructions on how to use the drain pack [(Canva Link)](http://canva.com/design/DAFdNafxDG0/u7vSLChmWgpOxGFQcyEm2w/view?utm_content=DAFdNafxDG0&utm_campaign=share_your_design&utm_medium=link&utm_source=shareyourdesignpanel)
        
        1.  Pdf located in [Free Drain Pack Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1FttaFzBdLI66Nwa_SOW11f3YD1fS_m0x/view?usp=share_link).
            
            1.  PDF titled “Drain Pack Directions”
                
            2.  Pdf might need to be downloaded to attach to an email.
                
6.  Partnership team texts resident from system
    
    1.  See [Appendix C](#_vq7vrcq5xfit) for text copy.
        
    2.  Only text residents during business hours between 9am-5pm, Monday through Friday, in their timezone
        
7.  Partnership team adds Critical Note stating when the delivery will happen for the Resident who requested the drain pack in the Latchel system.
    
    1.  Critical Note to say: “Drain Pack delivered \[insert Amazon delivery date\].
        
8.  Partnership team inputs check mark and date sent on the [Resident Benefits Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IFoIEIHjjWwyXAE6mmvmYle1OaRHKU9WN4Vn7ACTQ_c/edit#gid=0).
