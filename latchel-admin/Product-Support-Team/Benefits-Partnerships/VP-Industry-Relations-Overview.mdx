### **WHAT YOU'LL WORK ON**

The Industry Relations Team is an entrepreneurial group focused on growing the company. We are responsible for building and managing strong industry partnerships. and we play a vital role in facilitating revenue growth alongside our partners and sales team. We are also adept at working across departments in the company, such as Marketing, Business Development, and Product.

### **RESPONSIBILITIES**

The VP Industry Relations focuses their time on bringing in enterprise level accounts, ensuring relationships are built for successful land and expand of all customer portfolios on strategic accounts, creating channel partnerships to drive CMRR from new biz, and educating PM's on what Latchel is and how it works to drive more MQL's.

The VP Industry Relations develops, maintains, and expands relationships with named industry partners. This role holds a quarterly quota of their own and is also responsible for facilitating driving up team-wide quotas by building strong community relationships. 

**What You'll Do:** 

-   Develop, cultivate and own industry relationships with relevant influencers and partners to generate new business opportunities, accelerate existing sales opportunities and drive revenue for Latchel.
    
-   Act as an ambassador of Latchel, generating brand awareness and business opportunities among industry channels, and various networks within, to drive market growth
    
-   Develop strong relationships with key operational customers, specifically with the senior leadership of the customers at the Corporate and Local Market levels
    
-   Hold a quarterly quota of $75k from both account expansions and new sales of enterprise accounts (or partner channels), and support a great launch and expansion experience for them.
    
-   Create brand engagement through industry outreach, media channels, and partnerships. 
    

-   Drive and deliver Industry education, in person and virtually, through relationships with industry groups, influencers, publishing white papers, speaking at events, and other high visibility activities with strong ROI
    
-   Understand PM communities needs or challenges so that you can act as an internal advocate to help our products meet PMs’ needs
    

-   Plan and design programs, activities, and campaigns, to build a community of advocates and boost brand awareness.
    
-   Works cross functionally with marketing, sales, and customer success to execute and deliver results across Industry Relations initiatives.
    
-   Deliver timely and comprehensive communications to Latchel executives and leaders, ensuring they are well informed regarding developments that could impact the business and have an opportunity to influence outcomes.
    
-   Develop and implement effective advocacy strategies for key business priorities, ensuring that Latchel is among the leading voices in industry debates
