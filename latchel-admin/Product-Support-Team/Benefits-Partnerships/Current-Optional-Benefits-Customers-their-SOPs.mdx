Until Latchel onboards at minimum 5 optional benefits customers, the SOPs that allow Latchel to deliver optional benefits may vary slightly from customer to customer. This page describes the SOPs that Latchel team members will follow in order to deliver benefits to the first 5 optional benefits customers.

# Current Optional Benefits Customers

[https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/12yFy7Va33XeB31YuzCENme8ssTWjyuZAaTZRhmmDPJQ/edit#gid=1117237309](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/12yFy7Va33XeB31YuzCENme8ssTWjyuZAaTZRhmmDPJQ/edit#gid=1117237309)

### **DS Huber**

Effective March 1, 2024, DS Huber is implementing optional benefits. They expect:

-   To be billed $10.99 for ONLY active units that have a signed Latchel Lease Addendum that indicates the resident desires Latchel benefits
    
-   That ONLY residents associated with an Active Unit that has a signed Latchel Lease Addendum will have access to Latchel benefits, via a benefits only link 
    

To accomplish this:\* 

-   **Customer Profitability Team -** will, by the 5th of each month
    
    -   Download the customer’s GL report for the previous calendar month from Appfolio. Create a Jira ticket for the Data Support Team to complete by the 7th calendar day of the month.
        
        -   Note:  Each Jira ticket should request that all DS Huber residents and properties listed on the report attached to the ticket be marked as “Active” in Latchel. All other properties and residents (i.e., those not listed on the report attached to the Jira ticket) should be disabled. 
            
    -   Create a new monthly report in the [Monthly Scorecard](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1e9psvlXljLhCK_j_Dzhz1LmHRCYp7S23ns9kzJh19x0/edit#gid=1192480706) for the previous calendar month
        
        -   For instance, if a contract has a February 1, 2024 service start date, CPT would provide this information beginning March 1, covering the period of Feb 1 - 29. 
            
-   **Data Support Team** \- will complete the Jira tickets submitted by the Product Support team, per the schedule described [here](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/TST/pages/2772140035/Job+Aid+Task+Share+Jira+Improved+Process).
    
-   **Finance** - No change; bill based on Active Units in Latchel.
