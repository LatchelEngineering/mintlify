## **Objective**

The purpose of this document is to provide the process that needs to be taken when a new Partnership Tile is launched in Latchel’s Resident Benefit Hub

## **Process Overview**

1.  IR team posts in Partnership/Perks Slack channel about new benefit and go-live date.
    
    1.  There must be a _minimum_ of 72 hours from this post to the go-live date.
        
    2.  IR to tag any person/department affected by new benefit.
        
2.  CS Team to notify any current Property Managers who need to be informed before any new benefit becomes active.
    
    1.  CS to have list of PMs who have requested this.
        
3.  Sales team can update decks and talking points.
    
4.  Launch team to update decks and talking points
    
5.  Marketing to update current assets
    
6.  IR team will launch benefit at 5pm PT of go-live date
