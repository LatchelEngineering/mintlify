## Overview

The purpose of this page is to define the IR process for any partnership that requires change management.

We understand that not every partnership release is large enough to require the processes defined in this page. As a result, we’ve determined that the process should only be applied for any partnership where a group of Latchel stakeholders (Ops, PMs, CS, Sales, etc.) needs to change its behavior to enable a better customer experience. If you’re unsure on whether or not the Partnership requires the process defined here , please make sure to ask someone from the impacted stakeholder team on whether or not it’s necessary.

### Set IR Inputs

This section defines the inputs that the IR team will provide to the Main Stakeholder Team Champion(s) so that he/she can properly execute any partnership launch or change. IR will provide all this information within the \[Change Management\] IR Releases section in Confluence. Each partnership will have its own child page under that section Please go to this page for an example with a previous launch.
