## **Objective**

The purpose of this document is to provide the process that needs to be taken when a Benefit Tile is deprecated form Latchel’s Resident Benefit Hub.

____________________________________________________________________________

## **Departments Impacted**

-   Sales
    
-   Launch
    
-   CS
    
-   IR
    

____________________________________________________________________________

## **Process Overview**

1.  IR team posts in Partnerships/Perks Slack channel about benefit being deprecated.
    
    1.  There must be a _minimum_ of 72 hours from this post to the deprecation date.
        
    2.  IR to tag any person/department affected by the benefit deprecation.
        
2.  CS team to notify current Property Managers who need to be informed of this change.
    
3.  Sales team to update decks and talking points.
    
4.  Launch team to update decks and talking points.
    
5.  Marketing to update current assets.
    
6.  IR Team will deprecate benefit at 5pm PT of deprecation date specified in original post.
