# TLDR

Latchel is adding a new proprietary benefit to the Benefits hub where residents will receive drain unclogging snakes in the mail to help reduce plumbing issues. This is the first benefit that is a physical product inside of Latchel and will help reduce the number of maintenance requests. Property Managers will love this benefit because it reduces maintenance costs and provides physical value to the resident. Only Benefits subscribers are eligible for this benefit.

# Press Release

Latchel launches a new proprietary benefit to Residents to help them reduce maintenance issues from clogged drains. Residents and property managers alike know how valuable preventative maintenance is to make rental homes more livable, but never before have residents been given drain snakes to help prevent the one of the **most common** maintenance requests - clogged and slow drains.

With Latchel’s benefits, residents now get **free** drain unclog packs shipped directly to their homes to keep their drains running clean and reduce the need for plumbers and handyman services. This service has completely enhanced the convenience for residents while streamlining operations for property managers who don’t have to worry about coordinating maintenance visits for drain issues.

Stevie Snakes, a resident at Gogum Management, explained how convenient it is to get free drain clearance deliveries, “When I put in my drain pack order, I received drain clearance snakes to last me another 6 months **and** it arrived next day. Super fast delivery, totally free to me, and I was able to pull out loads of hair that had been causing my drain to slow drain.”

Cassy Crissy, a manager at Gogum Management, said, “We’ve gotten so many messages from residents thanking us for their free drain packs. It has been a real level up on service. We’ve also noticed a meaningful reduction in handyman Work Orders for drain clogs. We are so thankful to have Latchel’s Benefits for our renters.”
