## **Purpose**

The purpose of this document is to provide an email template to send to residents when one of their Security Deposit Review Requests are denied.

## **Email Template:**

Subject: Security Deposit Review Request

Hi \[**Resident First Name**\],

Thank you for submitting your Security Deposit Review request to Latchel. We appreciate your efforts in completing the form.

Unfortunately, we  regret to inform you that we are unable to approve your request for a security deposit review. **\[List reason we are unable to process this reimbursement**\]

If you have any questions regarding this decision, please do not hesitate to contact us. We understand this may be disappointing news for you and would be happy to discuss with you in more detail and provide further explanation that may be needed. 

Thank you for your understanding. 

Sincerely, 

\[**Your Name\]**

## **Email Example**

Subject: Security Deposit Review Request

Hi James,

Thank you for submitting your Security Deposit Review request to Latchel. We appreciate your efforts in completing the form.

Unfortunately, we  regret to inform you that we are unable to approve your request for review because your lease did not contain a benefit subscription. 

If you have any questions regarding this decision, please do not hesitate to contact us. We understand this may be disappointing news for you and would be happy to discuss with you in more detail and provide further explanation that may be needed. 

Thank you for your understanding. 

Sincerely, 

Matt
