## **Purpose**

The purpose of this document is to provide an email template to send to residents when one of their Move-In Guarantee requests are denied.

## **Email Template:**

Subject: Move-In Guarantee Request

Hi \[**Resident First Name**\],

Thank you for submitting your Move-in Guarantee request to Latchel. We appreciate your efforts in completing the form.

Unfortunately, we  regret to inform you that we are unable to approve your request. **\[List reason we are unable to process this reimbursement**\]

If you have any questions regarding this decision, please do not hesitate to contact us. We understand this may be disappointing news for you and would be happy to discuss with you in more detail and provide further explanation that may be needed. 

Thank you for your understanding. 

Sincerely, 

\[**Your Name\]**

## **Email Example**

Subject: Move-In Guarantee Request

Hi Jesse,

Thank you for submitting your Move-In Guarantee request to Latchel. We appreciate your efforts in completing the form.

Unfortunately, we  regret to inform you that we are unable to approve your request because the maintenance request was not approved by the property manager.

If you have any questions regarding this decision, please do not hesitate to contact us. We understand this may be disappointing news for you and would be happy to discuss with you in more detail and provide further explanation that may be needed. 

Thank you for your understanding. 

Sincerely, 

Matt
