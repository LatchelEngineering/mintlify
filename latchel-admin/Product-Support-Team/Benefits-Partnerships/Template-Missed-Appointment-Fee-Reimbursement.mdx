## **Purpose**

The purpose of this document is to provide an email template to send to residents when one of their missed appoint fee reimbursements are denied.

## **Email Template:**

Subject: Missed Appointment Fee Reimbursement Request

Hi \[**Resident First Name**\],

Thank you for submitting your Missed Appointment Fee Reimbursement request to Latchel. We appreciate your efforts in completing the form.

Unfortunately, we  regret to inform you that we are unable to approve your request for reimbursement. **\[List reason we are unable to process this reimbursement**\]

If you have any questions regarding this decision, please do not hesitate to contact us. We understand this may be disappointing news for you and would be happy to discuss with you in more detail and provide further explanation that may be needed. 

Thank you for your understanding. 

Sincerely, 

\[**Your Name\]**

## **Email Example**

Subject: Missed Appointment Fee Reimbursement Request

Hi Jonah,

Thank you for submitting your Missed Appointment Fee Reimbursement request to Latchel. We appreciate your efforts in completing the form.

Unfortunately, we  regret to inform you that we are unable to approve your request for reimbursement because there was no Latchel Work Order Number submitted with the request.

If you have any questions regarding this decision, please do not hesitate to contact us. We understand this may be disappointing news for you and would be happy to discuss with you in more detail and provide further explanation that may be needed. 

Thank you for your understanding. 

Sincerely, 

Matt
