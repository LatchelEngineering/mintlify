<Note>
  **Purpose**: A Deep Dive (DD) is a **detailed investigation by the Product Support (PS) team** to resolve complex or unresolved issues raised by property managers (PMs), residents, owners, or service providers (SPs). This SOP covers **when and how to submit** a DD request so issues are escalated efficiently and accurately. For the full DD process, see [Product Support Deep Dive Management](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Product-Support-Deep-Dive-Management).
</Note>

### **1. When to Request a Deep Dive**

Submit a DD request **only after**:

- You've **investigated the issue** using the resources available to you (e.g., work order history, PM settings, call recordings, message threads).
- You've spent approximately **10–15 minutes** trying to resolve it and it's clearly going to take longer.

<Note>
  If the issue can be resolved within that time, do **not** submit a DD. DDs are reserved for **complex issues that need deeper analysis**.
</Note>

### **2. Common Reasons for a Deep Dive Request**

- A customer is **unhappy with how a work order was handled**.
- A PM, resident, or vendor reports **service delays or availability issues**.
- A PM asks **why Latchel did something** (e.g., severity, vendor assignment, who was or wasn't called).
- An agent acted **outside of Latchel's policies**, and the cause is unclear (even without a complaint).
- A PM or owner **disputes a work order invoice** (use the Invoice Dispute DD workflow).

<Note>
  Low work order review scores, CSAT, and Google Reviews go through **SDDI** tickets instead, which are created automatically. See [Job Aid: SDDI Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-SDDI-Tickets).
</Note>

### **3. Common Deep Dive Requesters**

| Role | Typical Scenario |
| --- | --- |
| Product Support Associates (PS) | Complaints received through Wavelength (WL) tickets. |
| Customer Success Managers (CSMs) | Escalations during PM calls, meetings, or a WL ticket. |
| Accounts Payable | A PM disputes an invoice with them (submitted as an **Invoice Dispute DD**). |
| Service Operations Support / Coach | Unusual system behavior or unclear agent actions. |

### **4. Pick the Right Workflow**

Both workflows are in the `#ps-dd-sddi` Slack channel.

| Workflow | Use it for | Don't use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **DD Request** | Standard dives: the "why" questions (why wasn't this treated as an emergency, why was this SP assigned, why wasn't the on-call contact called, etc.). | Invoice disputes |
| **Invoice Dispute DD** | Any disputed work order invoice. This is the **only** way to submit an invoice dispute dive. | Anything that isn't an invoice dispute |

### **5. Prepare Before You Submit**

Have these ready:

- **One clear question** the DD should answer. If the customer's complaint is vague, ask them to clarify first.
- **What you've already checked** and what you found, with dates and times.
- **What the customer expected** to happen. The gap between that and what actually happened is what the DD explains.
- **Any prior commitments** already made to the customer (credits, exceptions, callbacks) and links to related tickets.

<Tip>
  The form is a handoff, not the dive itself. You don't need to review settings, logs, or transcripts in depth. The specialist does that. Just share what you've found and what needs answering.
</Tip>

### **Step-by-Step: Standard DD Request**

#### **1. Open the workflow**

1. Go to `#ps-dd-sddi` in Slack.
2. Select the **DD Request** workflow.

<Frame>
  ![image-20250929-214633.png](/images/PST-18.png)
</Frame>

#### **2. Complete the form**

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **PM Company Name** | PM or SP name as listed in Latchel |
| **Who is the CSM?** | The assigned CSM for the company (HubSpot Company Owner) |
| **WL Link** | The Wavelength ticket for the complaint, or "N/A" |
| **WO Link** | The work order link, or "N/A" |
| **Who raised it?** | Who the answer is ultimately for (PM, owner, resident, SP, CSM, Ops, etc.) |
| **What outcome is being questioned?** | The outcome the customer wants explained (e.g., severity, vendor assignment, who was or wasn't called) |
| **What have you found so far in your research?** | What you checked and what you found. Include dates and times |
| **What did the customer expect to happen?** | What the customer thought should have happened |
| **Prior commitments or related tickets?** | Anything already promised to the customer, and links to related tickets |
| **What question should the DD answer?** | One clear "why" question, e.g., "Why was this classified as Normal instead of Emergency?" or "Why wasn't the on-call contact called?" |

#### **3. Submit**

1. Click **Submit**.
2. The request posts in `#ps-dd-sddi` and **automatically creates a ticket** in the **PS Deep Dive Tickets** teamspace in WL.
3. If you're working a WL ticket, paste the Slack link into it as an internal note and add the **DD Requested** tag.

### **Step-by-Step: Invoice Dispute DD**

<Warning>
  Before submitting, complete the first steps of [SOP: Handling Work Order Invoice Disputes](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Work-Order-Invoice-Disputes): flag the dispute to Payments with a WO task, check the refund window against the policy, and check what's already on the WO. Disputes reported **more than 45 days** after the invoice date aren't eligible and don't need a DD.
</Warning>

1. In `#ps-dd-sddi`, select **Invoice Dispute DD** (not DD Request).
2. Fill out the form. In addition to the company, CSM, WL, and WO links, it asks for:
   - The **disputed amount** (attach the invoice in the thread)
   - The **date/time the PM first reported the dispute** (the earliest time across calls, webchat, SMS, and email, not when the ticket reached PS)
   - The **date/time the invoice was submitted**
   - The **refund window** (within 72 hours / within 45 days / over 45 days)
   - Whether the **invoice is paid**, and whether you **created a Payments task** on the WO
   - **Prior commitments or related tickets**
   - **What the PM is disputing and why**, **what initial review you've done**, and **the question the DD should answer**
3. Submit, then paste the Slack link into the WL ticket as an internal note and add the **DD Requested** tag.
4. Acknowledge the PM with the **Submit DD** template in WL (review can take up to seven business days).

---

### **After Submission**

- A PS specialist picks up the DD, investigates, and posts updates and findings on the WL ticket or in the same Slack thread where you submitted it.
- DDs have a **3-day completion SLA**.
- **Stay engaged.** Answer follow-up questions quickly and watch the thread. DDs go fastest when requesters give clear context and stay involved.
- **Before anything goes back to the customer**, the conclusion is reviewed through the **DD Conclusion Workshop** workflow in `#ps-team-chat` and written with the [ACE framework](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/DD-Customer-Conclusion-Email-Framework-ACE). Don't share findings with the customer before that review.
