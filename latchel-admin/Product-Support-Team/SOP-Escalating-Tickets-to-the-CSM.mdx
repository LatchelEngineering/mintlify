<Danger>
  ##### Overview

  Before escalating a ticket to the CSM, make sure you fully understand it:

  - Read the full ticket history, including any Ops escalation summary or transcript.
  - Reach out to the customer to gather any missing details or ask clarifying questions.

  Only escalate if the ticket meets the CSM criteria in [Product Support Team vs Customer Success Managers](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Product-Support-Team-vs-Customer-Success-Managers). PS handles most issues directly.
</Danger>

Once you've reviewed the Wavelength ticket and confirmed it belongs with the CSM, follow these steps:

#### Step 1: Find the Assigned CSM for the PM

1. Navigate to Hubspot.
2. Search for the PM Company and find the Company Owner → CSM.

#### **Step 2: Communicate with the Property Manager (PM)**

1. Use the **Escalate to CSM** email template as a **starting** point. Adjust it as needed based on the situation.

> _Hi [PM’s Name],_
>
> _Thank you for reaching out to us regarding [issue/inquiry]. To make sure you’re fully supported, I’ve looped in your Customer Success Manager, [CSM’s Name]. They’ll connect with you directly to provide next steps and discuss this further. In the meantime, please don’t hesitate to share any additional details with us if needed._

**Reminders:**

- Ensure the PM's Company is assigned to the interaction in the Customer Profile section.

  ![Screenshot 2025-09-30 at 8.25.21 AM.png](/images/PST-4.png)
</Frame>

#### **Step 3: Submit the Escalation via Slack Workflow**

1. Navigate to Slack. Use the #csm-escalations channel and initiate the PS Escalate to CSM workflow.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2025-09-30 at 8.42.50 AM.png](/images/PST-5.png)
</Frame>

2. Fill Out the Workflow Prompts:

- **Escalation Category:**
  - PM Disagrees with Denied Invoice Dispute
  - PM Requesting Training
  - PM Requesting Call/Meeting with CSM
  - Cancellation Request
  - PM Growing Frustrated
  - Email Thread Growing Complex → Call Recommended
  - AI Messaging / AI Voice Calls (account-level turn-off request or complaint)
  - Harmonia Upgrade
  - Other
- **Priority:**
  - URGENT → Needs to be dealt with ASAP, delay may result in Churn.
  - MEDIUM → Still a higher priority but can be dealt with the next day, the PM will likely not churn out.
  - NORMAL → Not urgent, training request, account upgrade.
- **Issue Summary:**
  - Avoid copying and pasting the PM’s message verbatim.
  - Provide a detailed summary of the issue, including:
    - Context of the conversation.
    - Troubleshooting steps already performed.
    - Actions taken thus far.
    - Any related Slack links or Wavelength notes.
  - For AI Messaging / AI Voice Calls escalations, also include the details in [AI Messaging and AI Voice Call Escalations](#ai-messaging-and-ai-voice-call-escalations) below.
- **Next Steps:**
  - Clearly explain the reason for escalation.
  - Provide suggestions/resolution and a call to action (e.g., Does the PM appear frustrated? Did they ask for a refund? Should the CSM reset expectations?).

3. **Submit the Workflow:**

- Once all prompts are completed, click **Submit**.

#### **AI Messaging and AI Voice Call Escalations**

The level of the request decides who handles it.

| Request | Who handles it |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Account-level:** turn off AI messaging for the whole company, or turn off automated vendor calls for the whole account (the account's AI subscription) | **CSM.** Escalate using this SOP |
| **Individual-level:** for example, turn off AI calling for one service provider | **PS handles it directly.** Don't escalate. For how PMs and vendors opt a service provider out of automated calls, see the FAQs "Can PMs opt vendors out of automated calls?" and "Can vendors opt themselves out?" in [Service Ops Job-Aid: AI Voice for Vendors](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Service-Ops-Job-Aid-AI-Voice-for-Vendors) |
| **Complaints about AI messaging or AI voice calls, and unclear AI escalations** | **CSM**, unless the fix is an individual-level change PS can make |

Do not post AI escalations in #ai-messaging-beta. Escalate with the workflow in Step 3 and let the CSM decide what goes to Product.

The CSM should be able to act without asking you a follow-up question. In the Issue Summary, cover every row below. Write "not provided" instead of leaving one blank.

| Include | Details |
| :-- | :-- |
| Who and which account | PM name, role and company |
| The ask | Turn off AI messaging for the whole account, turn off automated vendor calls for the whole account, both, or a complaint with no ask |
| Their reason | The PM's own words, quoted. If none was given, say so and say whether you asked |
| A real example | One work order or Conversations link showing what the customer saw. If none was given, say so |
| Who was affected | Residents, service providers, or the PM's own staff |
| What you've told the customer | Exactly what PS has said so far, including anything about who handles messages once AI is off |
| Account context | Autopilot or not, emergency coverage, and how the PM uses Latchel for work orders |
| Urgency | Whether the PM is threatening to cancel or a live emergency is affected |
| PS recommendation | Your own call on the best next step and why |

**Rules for the customer reply**

- If the PM gave no reason or example, ask once in your reply, then escalate either way.
- Don't promise an account-level turn-off, a timeline or a fix. Tell the PM their Customer Success Manager will follow up.

**When the ticket doesn't answer a row, ask Minion first**

Post in `#ask-the-minion` and mention Latchel Minion. Minion reads Latchel's database, work order notes and conversation history. Treat its answer as research, check it against the work order, and say in the escalation what you verified.

- Give it a work order number, the vendor or resident name, and roughly when it happened.
- Minion can't open Wavelength links, so paste the customer's email text into the thread.
- Ask one question per thread, for example: "Is AI messaging on for [company], and are automated vendor calls on?" or "The PM says [quote]. Find a work order or conversation from [date range] that shows this."
- If Minion finds nothing, write "Minion found no example" in the escalation.
- If its answer points to a defect, mention Minion with "bug" and a short description to file it, and tell the CSM you did.

#### **Step 4: Update the Wavelength Ticket**

1. **Copy Workflow URL:**

- After submitting the Slack workflow, copy the Slack URL and paste it into the Wavelength ticket as an Internal Note.

2. **Update the ticket attributes:**

- **Status:** Set to “Needs Response.”
- **Owner:** Assign the case to the CSM.
- **Team Owner:** Reassign from Product Support to Customer Success.
- **Label**: [Job Aid: Ticket Category, Attributes, and Tags](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Interaction-Tags-Explained)
