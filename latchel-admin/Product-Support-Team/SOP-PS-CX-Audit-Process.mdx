<Note>
**Purpose:** The end-to-end weekly **PS Quality Audit**: how tickets are picked, scored and shared, and what associates do with the results.

**Who should read this:** PS associates and anyone who audits PS tickets.

**How tickets are scored** (the 13 items, weights and calibration) is covered in [PS CX Audit Guidelines](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/PS-CX-Audit-Guidelines).
</Note>

## The weekly cycle at a glance

| **Step** | **Who** | **When** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Pull a random sample of tickets | Report (automatic) | Start of the week, for the previous Monday–Sunday |
| 2. Verify and score each ticket | Claude runs the audit, the PS Manager reviews | During the week |
| 3. Approve and submit scores | PS Manager | During the week |
| 4. Share results with each associate | PS Manager | After submission |
| 5. Complete follow-ups | Associate | Within **3 business days** |

## Step 1: Tickets are pulled

A report pulls a **random sample of 3 closed Wavelength tickets per associate** from the most recent Monday–Sunday report period into the audit tracker. Every ticket in the sample is audited.

## Step 2: Verify and score

The audit is run with Claude, using the same steps for every ticket:

1. **Read the full ticket**, including every earlier message and any quoted email chain, plus the parent Ops escalation if there is one.
2. **Open every source the ticket mentions:** each work order (Details and Activity, including the SMS and Calls filters), Slack threads, Latchel admin records, help guides and the SOP or Job Aid for the scenario.
3. **Check every claim against the record as it stood when the reply went out**, not against what it looks like today. Work orders often change after the reply.
4. **Score the 13 items** following the [PS CX Audit Guidelines](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/PS-CX-Audit-Guidelines).
5. **Write the notes:** what was great, what could be better (with suggested wording or the right step), and the documentation check.
6. **Draft a follow-up** for any No or Partial that can still be fixed (see Step 5).

<Warning>
  **A thread-only review isn't an audit.** If a source can't be opened, the item is marked unverified and flagged for the PS Manager, rather than scored from the thread alone.
</Warning>

## Step 3: Approve and submit

1. The **PS Manager reviews every score, note and follow-up** before anything is submitted, and corrects any judgment calls. AI does the legwork, but a person makes the final call.
2. Approved scores are submitted on the [PS Quality Audit form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSegLUzj7BaRwtAEri7TAtyfJOvC3PU5dHHSlwMuQmOf06m7pg/viewform) and land in the quality workbook, which calculates each category and the overall %.
3. Each ticket is marked **Audit Completed** in the tracker.

## Step 4: Results are shared

Each associate has their **own audit sheet** with their scores, notes and follow-ups. When the week's audit is done, the PS Manager sends each associate a **Slack DM** with an overview and a link to their sheet.

<Tip>
  **Patterns matter more than single misses.** When the same issue shows up across several tickets or associates (for example, tickets sitting before they're assigned), it's handled as a team or process fix, not as individual coaching items.
</Tip>

## Step 5: Complete follow-ups

If your audit includes a follow-up, complete it within **3 business days** (weekends and office closures don't count).

| **Follow-up type** | **What you do** |
| --- | --- |
| **Corrective Action** | Redo the missed step: answer the unanswered question, fix the setting, add the missing note or tag. |
| **Bug** | Report the bug in `#bug-report` with the Bug Report workflow and add the link to the ticket. |
| **Feature Request** | Submit it with the Feature Request form in `#feature-requests`. See [How to Handle: Feature Requests](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Handle-Feature-Requests). |
| **Follow-Up & Process Gap** | Close the loop with the customer or team, and flag the gap to the PS Manager. |
| **Bias for Action** | Take the action you should have taken, then let the customer know. See [SOP: Managing Unresponsive PMs](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Managing-Unresponsive-Property-Managers-in-Case-Management). |

When you're done, **log it on your follow-up in the quality workbook**: what you did and any links (ticket, Slack thread, bug or feature request).

<Note>
  **Have a question about a score?** Bring it to the PS Manager.
</Note>
