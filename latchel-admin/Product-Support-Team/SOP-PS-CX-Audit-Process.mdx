<Note>
**Purpose:** The end-to-end weekly **PS Quality Audit**: how tickets are picked, scored and shared, and what associates do with the results.

**Who should read this:** PS associates and anyone who audits PS tickets.

**How tickets are scored** (the 13 items, weights and calibration) is covered in [PS CX Audit Guidelines](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/PS-CX-Audit-Guidelines).
</Note>

## The weekly cycle at a glance

| **Step** | **Who** | **When** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Pull a random sample of tickets | Report (automatic) | Start of the week, for the previous Monday–Sunday |
| 2. Verify and score each ticket | Auditor | During the week |
| 3. Review and submit scores | Auditor (PS Manager approves) | During the week |
| 4. Share results with each associate | PS Manager | After submission |
| 5. Complete follow-ups | Associate | Within **3 business days** |
| 6. Dispute a score (optional) | Associate | After reviewing results |

## Step 1: Tickets are pulled

A report pulls a **random sample of 3 closed Wavelength tickets per associate** from the most recent Monday–Sunday report period into the audit tracker. Every ticket in the sample is audited.

## Step 2: Verify and score

For each ticket, the auditor:

1. **Reads the full ticket**, including every earlier message and any quoted email chain, plus the parent Ops escalation if there is one.
2. **Opens every source the ticket mentions:** each work order (Details and Activity, including the SMS and Calls filters), Slack threads, Latchel admin records, help guides and the SOP or Job Aid for the scenario.
3. **Checks every claim against the record as it stood when the reply went out**, not against what it looks like today. Work orders often change after the reply.
4. **Scores the 13 items** on the [PS Quality Audit form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSegLUzj7BaRwtAEri7TAtyfJOvC3PU5dHHSlwMuQmOf06m7pg/viewform), following the [PS CX Audit Guidelines](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/PS-CX-Audit-Guidelines).
5. **Fills in the notes:** what was great, what could be better (with suggested wording or the right step), and the documentation check.
6. **Logs a follow-up** for any No or Partial that can still be fixed (see Step 5).

<Warning>
  **A thread-only review isn't an audit.** If a source can't be opened, the auditor marks that item unverified and says so, rather than scoring it from the thread alone.
</Warning>

## Step 3: Review and submit

1. The auditor reviews scores with the PS Manager before submitting, especially anything that depends on a judgment call or an unverified source.
2. Scores are submitted through the form and land in the audit workbook, which calculates each category and the overall %.
3. The auditor marks each ticket **Audit Completed** in the tracker.

## Step 4: Results are shared

Each associate gets their own results: scores, what went well, what to improve, and any follow-ups with deadlines. Individual and team scores are shared directly, not on a shared page.

<Tip>
  **Patterns matter more than single misses.** When the same issue shows up across several tickets or associates (for example, tickets sitting before they're assigned), it's handled as a team or process fix, not as individual coaching items.
</Tip>

## Step 5: Complete follow-ups

If your audit includes a follow-up, complete it within **3 business days** (weekends and office closures don't count).

| **Follow-up type** | **What you do** |
| --- | --- |
| **Corrective Action** | Redo the missed step: answer the unanswered question, fix the setting, add the missing note or tag. |
| **Bug** | Report the bug in `#bug-report` with the Bug Report workflow and add the link to the ticket. |
| **Feature Request** | Submit it with the Feature Request form in `#feature-requests`. See [How to Handle: Feature Requests](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Handle-Feature-Requests). |
| **Follow-Up & Process Gap** | Close the loop with the customer or team, and flag the gap to the PS Manager. |
| **Bias for Action** | Take the action you should have taken, then let the customer know. See [SOP: Managing Unresponsive PMs](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Managing-Unresponsive-Property-Managers-in-Case-Management). |

When you're done, reply to the PS Manager with what you did and any links.

## Step 6: Dispute a score (optional)

If you think a score is wrong, you can ask for a reassessment with the [PS CX Audit Reassessment Dispute form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeT6dIOGvyP6JfgJRWcAjsKiMjYp7lRr_4D4MQ5eR8iJz3OrQ/viewform). Include the ticket link, the item you're disputing, and why (point to the SOP, work order or record that supports you).

The PS Manager reviews every dispute:

  - **Valid:** the score is corrected, and the auditor gets the feedback so future audits are scored the same way.
  - **Not valid:** you get an explanation of why the score stands.
