<Note>
  This SOP outlines how Product Support (PS) handles a work order complaint from a Property Manager (PM): investigating what happened, responding with the **ACE framework**, standing firm in our decisions, and knowing when to escalate to the Customer Success Manager (CSM).
</Note>

### Step-by-step Guide

#### Step 1: Gather Details

- **Clarify:** Ask **open-ended** questions to get the full picture of the complaint and what outcome the PM expected.
- **Request documentation:** Ask for any supporting files, such as recordings, screenshots or photos.

<Warning>
  **Don't take the complaint at face value.** Understand what actually happened before you respond. The PM's description is where the investigation starts, not the conclusion.
</Warning>

#### Step 2: Review the Situation

Review everything that applies to the work order:

- **Work order activity:** delays, dispatch attempts, SP communication, approvals and status changes.
- **Account settings:** Intake Settings, Pre-Dispatch Instructions, Critical Notes, Emergency Escalation Contacts and Contact Rotations, budget limits and prioritized service providers.
- **Automations:** Action Sets on the account.
- **Conversations:** chat transcripts, calls and emails with the resident, SP and PM.
- **Slack:** search for the work order link. There may already be a thread about the situation.

#### Step 3: Decide How to Investigate

| **If…** | **Then…** |
| --- | --- |
| The investigation will take **under 10 minutes** | Investigate it yourself. Document your findings and resolution in the ticket as an internal note. |
| The investigation will take **over 10 minutes**, or needs input from other teams | Submit a Deep Dive. See [How to Submit a Deep Dive Request](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Submit-a-Deep-Dive-Request). |
| The complaint is about **charges on a WO invoice** | Follow [SOP: Handling Work Order Invoice Disputes](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Work-Order-Invoice-Disputes). Always check eligibility against the [Latchel Guarantee and Refund Request Policy](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Latchel-Guarantee-and-Refund-Request-Policy) first. |

#### Step 4: Respond Using the ACE Framework

Whether the findings come from a Deep Dive or your own investigation, write your response using the [**ACE framework**](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/DD-Customer-Conclusion-Email-Framework-ACE). The investigation is your _input_, not your output. The PM needs to feel heard, understand what went wrong and know what happens next.

| **Part** | **What it means** | **Example** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A — Acknowledge the impact** | 1–2 sentences naming what the PM experienced. Don't open with "we reviewed" or a WO number. | _"I understand the resident waited three days for a vendor on an emergency."_ |
| **C — Clarify the cause** | One paragraph max, in plain English. Own it where Latchel is at fault. If you need a timeline, use short bullets with timestamps. | _"Your intake settings classify this issue as non-emergency, so it followed the standard scheduling timeline."_ |
| **E — Execute the resolution** | The concrete action, who owns it and when. Add one sentence on prevention, and point to the setting or tool that gets the PM the outcome they want. End with a statement, not "please let us know how you'd like to proceed." | _"We've updated your intake settings so this issue is treated as an emergency going forward. Here's how to review them: [help guide]."_ |

<Check>
  **Before you send,** run through the [ACE pre-send checklist](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/DD-Customer-Conclusion-Email-Framework-ACE#pre-send-checklist).
</Check>

**If the findings come from a Deep Dive:**

1. The DD conclusion is added to the Wavelength ticket as an internal note.
2. Draft your ACE response and share it in `#ps-team-chat` using the **DD Conclusion Workshop** workflow for review.
3. Once the PS Manager approves it, send it to the PM and work the ticket to close.

#### Step 5: Stand Firm in Our Decisions

Our conclusions are based on the investigation and our policies. Be empathetic, but **don't change a decision just because the PM pushes back.**

- **Own what's ours.** If Latchel made a mistake, say so clearly and state what we're doing about it.
- **Don't accept fault that isn't ours.** If the outcome came from the PM's own settings, instructions or a policy they agreed to, explain that plainly and point them to the setting or tool that gets them the outcome they want.
- **Don't reopen a dispute decision.** Invoice dispute outcomes follow the [Latchel Guarantee and Refund Request Policy](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Latchel-Guarantee-and-Refund-Request-Policy). Don't offer extra credits, one-off exceptions or a different outcome outside the policy to end a conversation.
- **Restate the reasoning once, then escalate.** If the PM still disagrees after you've clearly explained the decision, don't keep going back and forth. Escalate to the CSM (see Step 6).

<Warning>
  **Avoid over-apologizing.** Phrases like "we apologize for any confusion" without a clear resolution read as vague and invite more pushback. Acknowledge the impact, explain the cause and state the outcome.
</Warning>

#### Step 6: Know When to Escalate to the CSM

PS resolves most complaints directly. Escalate to the CSM when the conversation moves beyond day-to-day support into a relationship-level issue:

| **Escalate to the CSM when…** | **Workflow category** |
| --- | --- |
| The PM **continuously pushes back** on a denied invoice dispute and the frustration puts the relationship at risk (**churn risk**) | PM Disagrees with Denied Invoice Dispute |
| The PM is **extremely frustrated** after repeated explanations and the relationship is at risk | PM Growing Frustrated |
| The email thread is going back and forth and a **call would resolve it faster** | Email Thread Growing Complex → Call Recommended |
| The PM asks for a **call or meeting** with their CSM | PM Requesting Call/Meeting with CSM |
| The PM mentions **canceling, downgrading** or leaving Latchel | Cancellation Request |
| The PM needs **training** on how to use Latchel to meet their goals | PM Requesting Training |

Escalate with the **New Escalation from PS** workflow in `#csm-escalations`. Include what happened, what you found, what you've already told the PM and your recommended next step. See [SOP: Escalating Tickets to the CSM](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Escalating-Tickets-to-the-CSM) and [Product Support Team vs Customer Success Managers](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Product-Support-Team-vs-Customer-Success-Managers).

<Info>
  Not sure if it's time to escalate? Ask the PSM first in `#psm-escalate` using the **PS Ticket Escalate** workflow.
</Info>

#### Step 7: Identify Gaps and Create Feedback

If the complaint uncovered a gap, make sure it gets fixed:

| **Gap** | **Where it goes** |
| --- | --- |
| Ops didn't follow an SOP, or an SOP is unclear | `#svc-ops-requests` (**Ops SOP Requires Update**) or `#svc-ops-feedback` (**Agent Feedback**) |
| The system didn't behave as expected | `#bug-report` through the [in-app bug report form](https://app.latchel.com/admin/bug-reports) |
| The PM wants something the product can't do | `#feature-requests`. See [How to Handle: Feature Requests](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Handle-Feature-Requests). |
| An intake setting routed the WO incorrectly | Thumbs up/down on the WO's **Intake Details** (posts to `#harmonia-feedback`) |

For Deep Dive gaps and feedback, follow [Job Aid: Deep Dive (DD) Process](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Deep-Dive-DD-Process).
