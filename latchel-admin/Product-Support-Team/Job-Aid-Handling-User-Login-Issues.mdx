<Note>
  **Objective:** Help service providers, property managers and residents who can't log in, by confirming their account and email and getting them a way back in.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Verify identity first.** Before sending any login email or link, confirm you're talking to the account owner. Ask for their address, phone number or another detail on file. Never send a login link to an email address that doesn't match the profile.
</Warning>

## Step 1: Confirm the user is active

1. Search for the user in Latchel by **email address**. Check **Disabled** in the search so deactivated accounts show up too.
2. Open their profile and confirm the account is **active**.

   - If it's disabled, find out why before you do anything else. For a resident, the PM decides whether to reactivate. See [SOP: Handling Resident Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Resident-Cases).

## Step 2: Confirm the email address

1. Check that the email on the profile is spelled correctly and matches the address the user is trying to log in with.
2. If it's wrong, update it (or, for a PM's resident or user, ask the PM to), then continue.

## Step 3: Get them back in, by user type

| **User type** | **What to do** |
| --- | --- |
| **Service provider** | Open the SP's profile, click the **three dots** and select **Send Account Email**. This sends them a login email. SP help guide: [Resetting Your Latchel Password (Service Provider)](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wpGAA/Resetting-Your-Latchel-Password-Service-Provider). |
| **Property manager** | Open the PM user's profile, click the **three dots** and select **Send Account Email**. PM help guide: [How to Change/Reset the Password of a Latchel User's Login](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Change-Reset-the-Password-of-a-Latchel-User-s-Login). |
| **Resident** | Residents use their **Resident Public Dashboard URL** instead of a password. Copy it from the resident's profile (**Details** tab, **Miscellaneous** box) and email it to them. See [Job Aid: How to Locate Public Dashboard URLs](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-How-to-Locate-Public-Dashboard-URLs). Only send it if the resident profile is **active**. |

## Step 4: Confirm and follow up

1. Ask the user to confirm they got the email or link and could log in.
2. **Didn't get the email?** Follow [SOP: Handling Email Delivery Issues (SendGrid)](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Email-Delivery-Issues-SendGrid).
3. Once they're in, close the ticket. See [SOP: Closing Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Closing-Cases-in-Product-Support).
