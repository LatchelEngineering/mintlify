<Note>
  **Objective:** This FAQ explains how Product Support uses the Latchel Minion in [#ask-the-minion](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/C0C9GE19KFH) to gather details about what happened on a ticket, and what to do with what it finds.

  **Rule of thumb:** If the question is "what does the system do?", ask the Minion. If it's "what should we do?", ask a person.
</Note>

### **When to use the Minion**

| If you need to know... | Go to |
| :-- | :-- |
| What happened on a work order, invoice, property, vendor or company | **Minion** |
| How the system behaves in a situation (dispatch, billing, messaging, integrations) | **Minion** |
| Counts, trends or an export of records | **Minion** |
| Whether something is a bug or working as designed | **Minion** |
| What the SOP or process says to do | **Mintlify Assistant** or the Help Center. The Minion has no access to SOPs |
| A policy call (should we take the job, what is the customer owed, how to handle an exception) | **Ops, your PSM, or the CSM** |
| Slow pages or timeouts | **An engineer** |

### **The basics**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the Minion?">
    A read-only assistant for Latchel's systems. It looks things up and explains them in plain terms. It can't change anything.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can it access?">
    - Latchel's code: the main app, the integrations service and the package they share. It is read-only and refreshed every few minutes.
    - A read-only copy of the production database. It runs a few seconds behind live and covers work orders, invoices, properties, vendors, tenants, companies and more.

    It can't see server logs, monitoring dashboards, SOPs or internal docs, Slack history outside its thread, email, Wavelength tickets, or the property management platforms themselves.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can it answer or investigate?">
    - **What happened on a specific record.** For example, when a vendor was assigned or removed, why a work order is in its current status, or what was billed.
    - **How the product works.** What the system does in a given situation, such as how dispatch, billing or messaging behaves, or which fields and options exist.
    - **Integrations.** How Latchel syncs with RentManager, Rentvine, Propertyware, Buildium, Yardi and Leo, and what data moves between them.
    - **Counts and trends.** For example, how many work orders a company opened last month.
    - **Reports and exports.** It can pull a query result and attach it to the thread as a CSV file.
    - **Whether something is a bug.** It can say whether something looks like it's working as designed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can't it do?">
    - **Make changes of any kind.** It can't edit records, reassign vendors, send messages, fix data or deploy code.
    - **Diagnose performance problems** like slow pages or timeouts. Ask an engineer.
    - **Make business policy calls.** Whether to take a job, how to handle an exception, or what a customer or vendor is owed. Ask Ops or the CSM.
    - **Share credentials or security details.**
    - **Troubleshoot code or write fixes.** It can tell you what happened and whether it looks like a bug. Engineering does the rest.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can it tell me how a process works?">
    Yes, if it's how the *system* runs it. It can tell you what happens to a work order when a tenant cancels, when a vendor is notified, how a job moves through its statuses, or when an invoice is sent. That comes from the code and the data.

    It can't tell you how a *person* should handle a situation, who to escalate to, or what steps to follow. It has no access to SOPs or team workflows. Use the Mintlify Assistant or the Help Center for those.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### **Asking well**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get a good answer?">
    - Be specific. A work order number, invoice number, or company or property name helps a lot.
    - Mention the Minion in your message, and ask one question per thread.
    - It can't open Wavelength links. Paste the customer's email text into the thread.
    - Say roughly when it happened.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are some questions that work well?">
    - "Why was the vendor removed from work order 123456 yesterday?"
    - "What's the history of status changes on work order 123456?"
    - "Did invoice 7890 get sent to the PM, and what was on it?"
    - "When a tenant cancels a work order, does the vendor get notified?"
    - "Does a work order from Rentvine sync back when it's completed?"
    - "How many emergency work orders did [company] have in October?"
    - "Can you export all open work orders for [company] older than 30 days as a CSV?"
    - "Is AI messaging on for [company], and are automated vendor calls on?"
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if it can't find anything, or the lookup times out?">
    A lookup can time out. Try again with a narrower question or a specific work order number. If it still finds nothing, write "Minion found no example" in your ticket note or escalation. That is useful to the CSM and better than leaving it blank.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I rely on its answer?">
    Treat it as research, like the Mintlify Assistant. Check what it returns against the work order and the account, and use your own judgment. If you escalate or ask the PSM for help, say what you verified yourself. Don't send its answer to a customer as the answer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I keep the answer?">
    Paste the Minion thread link into the ticket as an internal note.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### **What to do with what it finds**

| If the Minion's answer shows... | Do this |
| :-- | :-- |
| **A bug** | Mention the Minion with "bug" and a short description. The Minion files the bug report. |
| **Ops agent feedback** | The Minion doesn't file this. Use the **Agent Feedback** workflow in `#svc-ops-feedback`. See [Job Aid: Slack Channels & Escalation Workflows](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Slack-Channels-Escalation-Workflows). |
| **A feature request** | The Minion doesn't file this. Use the **Feature Request** workflow in `#feature-requests`. See [How to Handle: Feature Requests](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Handle-Feature-Requests). |
| **A situation that needs the CSM** (for example, an account-level request to turn off AI) | Escalate with [SOP: Escalating Tickets to the CSM](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Escalating-Tickets-to-the-CSM). |
