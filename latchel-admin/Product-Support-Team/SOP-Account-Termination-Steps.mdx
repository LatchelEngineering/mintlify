### Step-by-Step Guide:

Once account is confirmed that they are terminating and we have the termination date:

**Step 1. PS or CS receives cancellation request from the PM.**

- If request goes to PS, [PS should escalate the ticket to the CSM](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Escalating-Tickets-to-the-CSM).

**Step 2.** CSM meets with PM to discuss cancellation and next steps.

**_ONCE CANCELLATION IS CONFIRMED:_**

**Step 3.** **CSM** creates a calendar invite on the PS Team Calendar for **one week before** the PM's Date of Cancellation and invites the PS team ([abegail@latchel.com](mailto:abegail@latchel.com), [cza@latchel.com](mailto:cza@latchel.com), [sarah@latchel.com](mailto:sarah@latchel.com)). The invite must include:

- PM Latchel URL

**Step 4.** On that date, PS sends the PM a courtesy reminder email confirming their cancellation and disable date. Use the **Cancellation Courtesy Reminder** template in Wavelength.

**Step 5.** **CSM** creates a calendar invite on the PS Team Calendar for the PM's **Date of Cancellation** at close of business and invites the PS team ([abegail@latchel.com](mailto:abegail@latchel.com), [cza@latchel.com](mailto:cza@latchel.com), [sarah@latchel.com](mailto:sarah@latchel.com)). The invite must include:

- PM HubSpot URL
- PM Latchel URL
- Primary Termination Reason
- Wavelength Desire to Churn ticket URL

Additionally, CSM creates a second calendar invite under PS Team Calendar, dated 3 weeks after the Date of Cancellation, for the Twilio phone number release. Invite must include:

- PM Account Name
- PM Latchel URL
- Original Date of Cancellation
- Latchel-Assigned Phone Number
- Latchel Communication Phone Number

If the Date of Cancellation changes after this invite is created, CSM updates the ticket, recreates both invites with the new date, and recalculates this reminder to new date + 3 weeks.

**Step 6:** On the Date of Cancelation, PS turns OFF all communications to ALL users in Latchel account. **SKIP THIS STEP IF TERMINATED FOR NON-PAYMENT.**

1. Turn OFF invoice notifications via the Maint. Communication Settings.
2. Delete Emergency Escalation Contacts and Failed Email Contacts.
3. Go to each Users profile and toggle OFF all the Contact Settings:

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2025-01-30 at 1.59.37 PM.png](/images/PST-7.png)
</Frame>

**Step 7.** **PS** terminates account in Latchel.

1. Disable all properties via the Properties tab using the Bulk Update tool.
2. Cancel any open active WOs.
3. Click the 3 dots in the top right corner of the account home page, then select **Terminate Service**.
4. Link the Wavelength Desire to Churn ticket in the termination reason field and save.
5. Spot check the PS Team Calendar to confirm a Twilio Release reminder invite exists, dated 3 weeks from the Date of Cancellation. If it's missing, notify CSM to create it before moving on.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2025-09-30 at 3.20.03 PM.png](/images/PST-8.png)
</Frame>

**Step 8.** Complete the **Account Terminated** workflow in [#service-changes](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/CKA2W1KJA) using the information in the calendar invite. Once submitted, tag the CSM in the thread for confirmation.

**Step 9.** PS emails the PM to confirm the cancellation is complete. Use the **Cancellation Confirmation** template in Wavelength.

**Step 10.** CSM updates the Opportunity in HubSpot:

- Account is terminated in any pipeline stage BEFORE Live:
  - **Stage: Churned  > Substage: Churned During Onboarding**
- Account is terminated in stage Live:
  - **Stage: Churned > Substage: Churned Post-Launch**

**Step 11.** On the date noted in the Twilio Release reminder invite (3 weeks after the Date of Cancellation), release all Twilio phone numbers associated with the customer.

Before releasing, confirm no recent call/text activity on the numbers (check Twilio Messages/Calls Log). If activity is found in the last few days, delay release and flag to CSM.

<Note>
  Find phone numbers associated with the PM by going to the PM Account page in Latchel. In Maint. Communication Settings, you will want use the **Latchel-Assigned Phone Number** and the **Latchel Communication Phone Number**.
</Note>

1. After logging into Twilio, navigate to Phone Numbers > Manage > Active Numbers.
2. Copy/Paste the PM phone number in the search bar and select **Filter**.

<Frame>
  ![image-20240524-141908.png](/images/PST-9.png)
</Frame>

3. Select the phone number from the filtered results, and then navigate to the **Properties** tab. From the **Properties** tab, select the option in the bottom right corner, **Release Phone Number**.

<Frame>
  ![image-20240524-142159.png](/images/PST-10.png)
</Frame>

**Step 12.** Post a confirmation reply in the original [#service-changes](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/CKA2W1KJA) thread (from Step 8) noting that the Twilio phone numbers have been released, so the termination record is fully closed out in one place.
