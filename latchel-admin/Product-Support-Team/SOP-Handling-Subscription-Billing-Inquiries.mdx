<Note>
  **Purpose:** how Product Support handles questions about a PM's **monthly subscription invoice** and escalates them to Accounts Receivable (AR).

  **Who owns what:** PS owns the ticket and all customer communication, unless AR says they'll handle the customer directly. **AR (Donita)** answers billing questions and makes invoice changes in Rillet. **Amrit Parajuli** approves refunds and credits over \$250. The **CSM** approves credits up to \$250 and handles contract questions and terminations.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Subscription invoices vs. work order invoices.** This SOP is only for the monthly subscription invoice. Disputes about charges on a **work order** invoice follow [SOP: Handling Work Order Invoice Disputes](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Work-Order-Invoice-Disputes).
</Warning>

## Know the basics before you escalate

Many questions can be answered without AR:

- **Invoices** go out on the **10th** (the official invoice date is the 1st), based on active units at midnight UTC on the last day of the prior month. A reminder goes out on the **21st**. Most customers are **Net 5**. AutoPay runs on the **14th or 15th**.
- **There's no billing portal.** PMs can't see subscription invoices in the console. For invoice copies or payment history, they can email [**billing@latchel.com**](mailto:billing@latchel.com).
- **Payment methods:** a payment method saved in the console only applies to **work orders**, not the subscription. To change the subscription payment method, AR sends a secure link (see [How to Update Your Subscription Payment Method](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-update-your-Subscription-Payment-Method)).
- **Card fee:** credit cards carry a **3.5%** processing fee, so bank accounts are encouraged.
- **Checks:** Latchel, Inc., P.O. Box 200846, Dallas, TX 75320-0846.
- **Terminations or cancellations** go to the CSM through [SOP: Escalating Tickets to the CSM](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Escalating-Tickets-to-the-CSM), not to AR. Pausing an account isn't supported.

## Step 1: Understand the request

Before escalating, make sure you know exactly what the PM is asking. If they say "this invoice is wrong," ask what looks wrong (the unit count, the rate, a line item they don't recognize) before you send it to AR.

## Step 2: Classify the ticket

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | **PM Inbound** (or **Owner Inbound** / **Latchel Ops Escalation** if that's how it arrived) |
| **Topic** | **Subscription & Billing →** **Subscription Invoice Query**, **Unit Count & Billable Inventory**, **Plan Change (Upgrade)** / **(Downgrade)**, **Partner Programme Billing** or **Adoption & Pricing Question** |
| **Tags** | **Reimbursement or Credit Issued** if a credit or refund is approved. **CSM Handoff** / **Churn Risk** if it escalates. **Feature Request Filed** if you log one. |

See [Job Aid: Ticket Category, Attributes, and Tags](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Interaction-Tags-Explained).

## Step 3: Submit the Subscription Invoicing Request

In [#accounts-receivable](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/C05N2RRD14P), open **Workflows** and start **Subscription Invoicing Request**.

<Frame>
  ![image](/images/PST-98.png)
</Frame>

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Property Manager Name** | The PM company name |
| **Assembly Link/ Hubspot Deal Link** | Paste the **WL ticket link** (WL was formerly Assembly). |
| **CSM** | The PM's CSM (the **Company Owner** in HubSpot). If there isn't one, select yourself. |
| **Priority** | See the table below |
| **Category** | See the table below |
| **What do you need help with?** | A clear summary of the request in your own words and what you've already checked. Upload any invoices or screenshots the PM shared in the thread. |

### Priority

| Priority | When |
| --- | --- |
| **URGENT** | Any **Billing Dispute (CS)** · The customer is clearly upset |
| **Medium** | Default |
| **Low** | Nobody needs to follow up with the customer |

### Category

Options ending in **(AR)** (**Failed Payment (AR)**, **Internal Error Catch (AR)** and **Issue Not Resolved (AR)**) are for AR only. Don't select them.

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Billing Dispute (CS)** | Questioning the unit count or rate billed · Charged twice for the same invoice · On AutoPay but says they were never billed · Asking for a credit or refund |
| **Change Request (CS)** | Change the payment method · Turn AutoPay on or off · Update the billing POC · Plan upgrade (tag the CSM) |
| **General Question (CS)** | Confirm the payment method or billing POC on file · Resend an invoice · Receipts or payment history · Trouble paying or opening an invoice link · What rate they're billed · Whether AutoPay is on · Why the invoice is higher than expected |
| **Past Due Invoice(s)** | Whether any invoices are past due · Request a payment plan |

After you submit, paste the Slack link into the WL ticket as an internal note and set the ticket to **Escalated**.

## Step 4: Credits and refunds

- **Latchel prefers a credit over a refund.** Refunds are for exceptional cases (internal errors, or charges that should never have happened) and for terminated customers with no future invoices.
- **Approvals:** credits up to **\$250** need the **CSM**. Credits over \$250, card-fee credits and all refunds need **Amrit Parajuli**. AR requests the approval in the thread.
- **Timing:** approved **before the 8th** → applied to the current month's invoice. **On or after the 8th** → applied to next month's invoice. If the current invoice is already paid, the credit carries to the next invoice.

See [How to Handle Monthly Subscription Invoice Adjustments](/latchel-admin/accounts-receivable/How-to-Handle-Monthly-Subscription-Invoice-Adjustments).

## Step 5: Close the loop

1. **AR replies in the thread.** If there's no response, tag **@Donita** in the same thread to follow up. Don't submit a new workflow.
2. **You tell the PM** what AR found or did, confirm in the thread that you've sent it, and close the ticket as **Closed**.
3. **If AR says they'll handle the customer directly,** let the PM know AR will reach out, note it on the WL ticket, and close the ticket.
4. **If the CSM asks to take it** (for example, the billing question turns into a contract question), follow [SOP: Escalating Tickets to the CSM](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Escalating-Tickets-to-the-CSM).

<Warning>
  **AutoPay and payment links are unique to each customer.** Only send a customer the link AR created for them. Never forward one customer's link to another.
</Warning>
