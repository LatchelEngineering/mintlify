<Note>
  **What the LVN is:** The Latchel Vendor Network (LVN) is Latchel's group of national vendor partners. They're large companies with a central dispatcher who assigns their own technicians (often subcontractors). In return for being offered real work orders, LVN vendors agree to:

  - use Latchel technology for every work order
  - get paid only through Latchel's direct deposit payouts
  - guarantee every technician is vetted, licensed, bonded and insured
  - offer free recalls. Their work is backed by at least a **30-day workmanship guarantee** in the [Latchel Guarantee and Refund Request Policy](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Latchel-Guarantee-and-Refund-Request-Policy).

  **How to spot one:** an LVN service provider has a **Latchel Vendor Network** tag next to their name on the work order. For the current list of LVN companies and how the LVN differs from the LCN, see [Latchel Vendor Network FAQs](/latchel-admin/Product-Engineering/latchel-vendor-network-faqs).
</Note>

<Info>
  **The LVN is all or nothing.** A PM either has it on or off. We can't block one LVN company while keeping the others.
</Info>

Ops handles most day-to-day LVN work (unresponsive vendors, tech changes, cancellations). See [Handling Work Orders Assigned to LVN Service Providers](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Handling-Work-Orders-Assigned-to-LVN-Service-Providers). PS gets these when a PM is unhappy, wants the LVN off, or has a complaint about an LVN vendor.

## A PM wants an LVN company removed from a job

<Warning>
  **Don't reassign a job to a different LVN company if the original LVN company has already been to the property.**
</Warning>

1. **Offer a different technician from the same LVN company first.** Send the **Remove LVN from WO** template in Wavelength. It explains that the LVN company assigns individual technicians, and that we can ask them to send a different technician and make sure the original one isn't sent to the PM's property again. This keeps the warranty and workmanship guarantee in place.
2. **If the PM is OK with a new technician,** reach out to the LVN company. Ask them to reassign the work order to a different technician and to stop dispatching the original technician to this PM's properties.
3. **If the PM wants a different company** (for example, switching from Puls to Keepe, or using their own vendor), check whether the original LVN company has **visited the property** or **been paid**:
   - **Yes:** confirm the PM agrees to pay the original invoice. There's likely a dispatch or service fee owed. Let them know the original provider's warranty may no longer apply.
   - **No:** cancel the original work order and create a new one. Ask the PM which SP to assign, assign them, and [lock in the SP](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Enable-Service-Provider-Locked-In-on-a-Work-Order-New-Experience).

<Warning>
  **Locking in the SP is required.** The new work order needs an SP assigned and locked in so automation never takes over and reassigns it to the original LVN company.
</Warning>

## A PM never wants to work with an LVN company again

1. **Explain that the LVN is all or nothing.** Send the **Blacklist LVN Company** template in Wavelength. It explains that we can't remove one LVN company, and that the PM can either turn the LVN off completely or keep it on.
2. **If the PM wants it off:**
   1. Open the PM company in Latchel.
   2. On the **Account Details** tab, find the **Vendor Assignment Automation** box.
   3. Toggle **Use Vendor Provider Networks** off.
   4. Let the PM know it's done.
3. **If the PM wants to keep it on,** make sure the setting is on. No other changes are needed.

<Tip>
  Before they turn it off, it may help to suggest prioritizing their preferred vendors instead. See [Prioritize preferred vendors](#prioritize-preferred-vendors) below.
</Tip>

## A PM has a complaint about an LVN company

1. **Get the full details** from the PM on the Wavelength ticket.
2. **Review the work order** against the PM's complaint.
3. **If you can't find the root cause,** or the investigation will take more than 10 minutes, submit a Deep Dive. See [How to: Submit a Deep Dive Request](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Submit-a-Deep-Dive-Request).
4. **Decide whether the complaint is valid** (something went wrong) or **invalid** (nothing went wrong, and expectations need to be reset).
5. **Share your conclusion** and next steps with the PM either way.

### If the complaint is valid

Use the options below as a guide and tailor them to the situation.

### If the complaint is invalid

Make sure the PM feels heard, even when the outcome isn't what they expected.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Explain the process">
    |  | **Next steps** |
    | --- | --- |
    | **When** | The PM is confused about how Latchel's service or vendor selection works. |
    | **Action** | Explain our standard process, such as how vendors are sourced, response times, and Latchel's role vs. the vendor's. Share relevant help guides from [help.latchel.com](https://help.latchel.com/home). |
    | **Example** | _"The vendor was dispatched according to our standard process, which prioritizes [timeliness/availability/vendor expertise]."_ |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Clarify how updates work">
    |  | **Next steps** |
    | --- | --- |
    | **When** | The PM is confused about how and when they get work order updates. |
    | **Action** | Explain how Latchel communicates with vendors, PMs and residents, including when automatic updates go out. |
    | **Example** | _"Latchel automatically sends you updates once a service is scheduled and completed. We know everyone has different preferences, so there are options to customize how often you get these updates. Let us know what works best for you!"_ |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reset timeline expectations">
    |  | **Next steps** |
    | --- | --- |
    | **When** | The PM's expectations about how fast work gets done need to be realigned. |
    | **Action** | Explain typical response times for emergency vs. non-emergency work orders. How a work order is classified comes from the PM's Intake Settings. See [Introduction to Intake Settings](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Introduction-to-Intake-Settings). |
    | **Example** | _"[Type] work orders generally take about X hours to schedule and complete, while emergencies are prioritized and handled within X hours."_ |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Explain when the LVN is used">
    |  | **Next steps** |
    | --- | --- |
    | **When** | The PM expects their preferred vendors to always be used, even when they're not available. |
    | **Action** | Explain that we contact their preferred vendors first. If none of them accept in time, we use the LVN so the job still gets done. Suggest extending the last preferred SP's response buffer time (see **Prioritize preferred vendors** above). |
    | **Example** | _"If your preferred vendors don't accept the work order within X hours, the system moves to the Latchel Vendor Network so the job still gets done on time."_ |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
