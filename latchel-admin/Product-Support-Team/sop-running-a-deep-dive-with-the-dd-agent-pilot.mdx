## Purpose and scope

The DD AI agent does the research and first drafts. A PS team member checks the facts, makes the calls, and sends the final customer email. Use this for every Standard DD and Invoice Dispute DD from `#ps-dd-sddi`. Ticket mechanics (timer, labels, tags) still follow the [Job Aid: Deep Dive (DD) Process](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Deep-Dive-DD-Process).

**Not covered:** Google Reviews dives (see [SOP: How to Deep Dive Google Reviews](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-How-to-Deep-Dive-Google-Reviews)) and SDDI tickets. We are building a separate SDDI agent, so keep following [Job Aid: SDDI Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-SDDI-Tickets) for those.

## Roles

Three people touch every dive (Cza, the PS agent on the PM ticket, and Maddie). Abi covers when Cza is out, and Will signs off only when there is a legal threat.

The DD Conclusion Workshop continues in the interim while we pilot, so we can keep improving the skill in Claude.

| Role | Who | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Runs the dive | Cza | Picks up the DD ticket, runs the agent with the kickoff prompt, answers the agent's questions, and creates the child tickets |
| Validates | Cza | Works the validator checklist, verifies facts against source, listens to call recordings, adds her own recommendation, and logs corrections in the DD Correction Log |
| Writes the final conclusion | PS agent assigned to the PM ticket | Turns the agent's ACE draft into the customer email in their own voice, and owns any commitment in it |
| Reviews before it goes out | Maddie | Reviews the conclusion in the DD Conclusion Workshop, and reviews the Correction Log every two weeks |
| Backup when Cza is out | Abi | Runs and validates the dive the same way |
| Legal sign-off | Will (Jen Lye in his absence) | Signs off on any dive where a vendor, owner, or PM has threatened legal action, before anything is sent |

## Output: what the agent gives us

The agent returns these outputs, in this order, for every dive. Cza reads them in step 3 of the [process steps](#process-steps).

| # | Output | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Customer conclusion (ACE) | Acknowledge, Clarify, Execute, written to the [ACE framework](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/DD-Customer-Conclusion-Email-Framework-ACE). Under ~150 words, ends on a statement. No timeline unless the dispute is about sequence (then 3–5 dated bullets). Includes the ACE self-score. |
| 2 | Internal conclusion (the WL internal note) | What happened, why, what's next, and a verdict that answers the DD question word for word. Leaves a slot for the validator's own recommendation. |
| 3 | Sourced timeline | Always included, internal only. Each line has a source. |
| 4 | Findings | Intake corrections first, then the settings at the time and the on/off-platform ledger for money dives. |
| 5 | Tagging suggestion | Complaint Category and Root Cause + Details from the [Category Matrix](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-PS-DD-and-SDDI-Category-Matrix). |
| 6 | Child ticket recommendations | Each gap is sorted into one of four types. (a) An SOP exists and wasn't followed → Agent Feedback. (b) An SOP exists but is unclear or outdated → SOP update. (c) No SOP → SOP request. (d) System behavior → Bug/Feature. LVN issues → LVN SP Feedback. Each comes pre-filled in the child ticket template from the [Job Aid](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Deep-Dive-DD-Process). |
| 7 | Agent feedback appendix | Transcript with key lines bolded, what was missed, what should have been said, and what the agent did well. |
| 8 | Validator checklist | What a human must still check: call recordings, photos, SendGrid/Twilio receipts, every fact and promise. Also what would flip the verdict. |
| 9 | Conditional | Cover note when a CSM or partner relays it. Escalation brief for every legal threat. |

## Process steps

1. **Pick up the ticket.** Start the timer, set yourself as Owner, add the DD or SDDI label. For invoice disputes, check it's within 45 days. (Ticket steps are in the [Job Aid: Deep Dive (DD) Process](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Deep-Dive-DD-Process).)
2. **Run the agent.** Open the Deep Dives project, paste the [kickoff prompt](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-DD-Agent-Kickoff-Prompt) and the DD request. Answer every question it asks.
3. **Check the output.** Work the checklist below. Fix anything wrong and log each fix in the DD Correction Log. To counter-check the dive or confirm how the system behaves, ask the Latchel Minion in `#ask-the-minion`.
4. **Create child tickets and tag.** Keep or drop each draft the agent wrote. Set Complaint Category and Root Cause.
5. **Hand off.** Post the findings as an internal note on the DD ticket, then give the customer draft to the PS agent on the PM ticket.
6. **Write and submit the email.** Edit the draft in your own voice and submit it in the DD Conclusion Workshop in `#ps-team-chat`.
7. **Approve (Maddie).** Review in the workshop.
8. **Send and close.** The PS agent sends the approved email. Cza tags the requester, sets the ticket to Escalated, stops the timer, and closes it.

## Validation

The agent's output is a draft, and the following needs to be validated by a team member:

- [ ] Dates, amounts, and who-did-what match the work order, messages, and invoice
- [ ] Call recordings listened to and photos viewed (the AI agent can't do these)
- [ ] SendGrid or Twilio receipts checked for any "I never got it" complaint
- [ ] Every promise in the draft has an owner and a date
- [ ] Your own recommendation is written in the findings

## DD Correction Log

Every fix Cza makes to the agent's output goes in the [DD Correction Log](https://claude.ai/artifact/DSeFBtEZu96HMS2pWYpxEz) before the dive closes. This is how the skill improves: Maddie reviews the log every two weeks and updates the `ps-deep-dive` skill from it.

Log one entry per correction, whether it is a wrong name or a flipped verdict. A dive with no corrections needs no entry.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Ticket | DD ticket or WO number, owner or vendor |
| Date of the dive | The day the dive was run |
| Section of the output | Which output the miss was in (ACE conclusion, timeline, child ticket drafts, and so on) |
| What kind of miss | Missing data, wrong fact, wrong amount, ACE miss, wrong or missing SOP gap, and so on |
| What the agent got wrong | Quote or describe what it said |
| What you changed it to | The corrected fact, wording, or verdict |
| Suggested skill change | A rule or check that would have prevented it (optional, but the most useful field) |
| Impact | Minor, or changed the verdict or the money |

Entries start as New. Maddie moves each one to In skill or Won't change after her review, and uses Sent to Maddie or Sent to Will when an entry needs a decision from them. Anyone with Contributor access can log; ask Jen if you only have view access.

## Conclusion, workshop, and sending

Nothing reaches a customer until Maddie has reviewed it in the DD Conclusion Workshop. The PS agent on the PM ticket writes the final email, starting from the agent's ACE draft, and owns every fact and promise in it.

Write it with the [ACE framework](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/DD-Customer-Conclusion-Email-Framework-ACE) (Acknowledge, Clarify, Execute). That page has the structure, length guidance, examples, and pre-send checklist, so they aren't repeated here.

Three rules come from past dives:

- Off-platform work or negotiations between a customer and a vendor they hired themselves are between them and that vendor. Latchel can't say what or whether something happened.
- Don't commit to a vendor's response deadline. Say "we will keep you updated."
- To a PM or owner, say a valid charge "will be paid," not "we'll pay it."

To review, submit the draft in `#ps-team-chat` using the DD Conclusion Workshop workflow, make Maddie's changes, then send. The internal findings stay internal; the customer email is a short distillation of them.

## Legal threats

If anyone mentions a lawsuit, legal action, or a lawyer, stop. Nothing is sent until Will signs off (Jen if Will is out). Post the agent's escalation brief and the draft in one thread, and keep admissions like "we should have" out of the customer email.

## Timing and metrics

A dive should finish inside the 3-day SLA and take no more than four hours of working time. Wavelength tracks both, so the timer has to run. See [Product Support Deep Dive Management](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Product-Support-Deep-Dive-Management) for the service levels.

| Measure | Target | How it's tracked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Completion time | 3 days from submission | Wavelength flags a ticket as Breaching if it sits in Needs Response near day 3 |
| Handling time | Within 4 hours of working time per dive | The Wavelength timer: start it at pick-up, pause it for breaks |
| Average age of all dives (WBR) | 7 days goal, 3 days stretch | WBR dive metrics |
| Average handling time of DD tickets (WBR) | 6 hours goal, 4 hours stretch | WBR dive metrics |

The pilot is working if handling time stays at or under four hours and the DD Correction Log shows fewer corrections per dive over time. Review both at the end of the pilot.

## Appendix: What the agent does and what people do

The agent owns the evidence work and the first drafts. People own verification, judgment, and anything that is sent. Nobody should rebuild by hand what the agent already produced.

| Work | Owner | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pull WO log, settings at the time, tasks and queue routing, webchat and text threads, call and SMS records | AI Agent | Wavelength and BigQuery |
| Build the sourced timeline and the on/off-platform ledger | AI Agent | Cza verifies the facts the conclusion relies on |
| Find what the SOP requires and compare it to what happened | AI Agent | Uses the Help Center (Mintlify), never Confluence or Rovo |
| Draft the internal conclusion, the ACE customer draft, and the ACE self-score | AI Agent | Drafts only; nothing is sent |
| Draft child tickets and the tagging suggestion | AI Agent | Agent feedback, SOP gaps, bugs, feature requests, LVN SP feedback |
| Listen to call recordings, view photos, check SendGrid and Twilio receipts | Cza | The agent cannot do these |
| Decide any verdict that rests on an unverified fact, and add the recommendation | Cza | Latchel policy and visible facts override AI output |
| Create child tickets and post in Slack | Cza | Cza decides which drafts to create or drop |
| Write the final customer email and own every promise in it | PS agent on the PM ticket | Starts from the agent's ACE draft |
| Review the conclusion before it goes out | Maddie | DD Conclusion Workshop |

**Stop doing by hand:** building timelines from scratch, writing the first ACE draft from scratch, filling child ticket templates from scratch, and rewriting the findings into a separate conclusion.
