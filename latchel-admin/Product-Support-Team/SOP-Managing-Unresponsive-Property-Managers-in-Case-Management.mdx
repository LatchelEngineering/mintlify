<Note>
  **Objective:** How to follow up when a PM doesn't reply, and when to close the ticket, so every PM gets a consistent follow-up and no ticket sits open forever.
</Note>

## How the ticket status moves

1. When you reply to the PM, the Wavelength ticket automatically moves to **Open**.
2. If the PM doesn't reply within **24 hours**, the ticket automatically moves back to **Needs Response**.
3. When it comes back to **Needs Response**, PS owns the follow-up.

## First reopen: send one follow-up

When the ticket reopens because the PM hasn't replied:

1. Send the PM a **short follow-up** with the **Unresponsive PM Follow-Up** template in Wavelength. Don't copy and paste your original message. A quick nudge is easier for the PM to act on.
2. If you've been waiting on another team (Finance, Data Support, Engineering), send the PM an **update on where things stand** instead.
3. The ticket moves back to **Open**.

<Warning>
  **No follow-up needed** for these ticket types. When they reopen, close the ticket:

  - [Feature requests](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Handle-Feature-Requests)
  - [Bulk account updates](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/How-to-Handle-PM-Account-Updates). Also let Data Support know in the Slack thread that we never got the details from the PM.
  - Low-impact bug reports
</Warning>

## If the PM replies

Answer their question or share next steps, then close the loop. See [SOP: Closing Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Closing-Cases-in-Product-Support).

## Second reopen: close the ticket

If the PM still hasn't replied and the ticket reopens again after another **24 hours**:

1. Close the ticket as unresponsive.
2. The PS CSAT survey goes out automatically **24 hours** after the ticket closes, the same as every closed PS ticket. See [Job Aid: Wavelength PS Automation Explained](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/Job-Aid-Assembly-Case-Automation-Explained).

<Tip>
  **Bias for action.** If the PM's silence is blocking something you can safely move forward yourself (for example, a setting they already asked for), do it and tell them what you did, rather than waiting on a reply.
</Tip>
