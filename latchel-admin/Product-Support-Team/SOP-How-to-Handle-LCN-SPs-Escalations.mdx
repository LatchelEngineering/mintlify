<Note>
  **What the LCN is:** The Latchel Contractor Network (LCN) is like a yellow pages. Anyone who signs up and wants jobs can join. Latchel doesn't manage licensing or vet these vendors. PMs can opt in to use the LCN as a backup when their preferred vendors aren't available:

  - **Emergency work orders:** Latchel can source a new service provider on the spot, or use one we've worked with before in the area, when no one in the PM's preferred network can take the job.
  - **Non-emergency work orders:** the PM gets access to LCN vendors when their preferred vendors aren't available.

  For the other SP types (Preferred and LVN), see [SOP: Handling Service Provider (SP) Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Service-Provider-SP-Cases).
</Note>

## PM requests

### A PM wants to blacklist an LCN company

Use this when a PM never wants a specific LCN company assigned to their work orders.

1. Open the **LCN company** in Latchel.
2. On the **Preferred PMs** tab, click **\+ Add PM**. Search for the PM company and add it.
3. Open the **PM company** in Latchel and go to the **Service Providers** tab.
4. Open **Service Provider Management** and search for the LCN company.
5. Click the **three dots** next to the LCN company and choose **Blacklist Service Provider**.
6. Confirm it worked: open **Blacklisted Service Providers Management** and check that the LCN company is listed.
7. Let the PM know it's done.

PM help guide: [How To Blacklist a Service Provider](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-To-Blacklist-a-Service-Provider)

### A PM wants to turn on the LCN

Send the PM [How to Opt Into the Latchel Contractor Network](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Opt-Into-the-Latchel-Contractor-Network).

<Tip>
  **Only want the LCN for certain problems?** PMs can limit it to specific work order problems. See [Setting Up Latchel Contracted Network (LCN) Preferences](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Setting-Up-Latchel-Contracted-Network-LCN-Preferences).
</Tip>

### A PM wants to turn off the LCN

PM help guide: [How to Opt Out of the Latchel Contractor Network](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Opt-Out-of-Latchel-Contractor-Network)

1. **Opt the PM out of the LCN** if it isn't already done.
2. **Delete all LCN payment methods** on the account.
3. **If a payment method won't delete,** it's still in use on a portfolio or property:
   1. Find where it's used with the queries below.
   2. Temporarily turn the LCN back on if you need to, so you can make edits.
   3. Remove the LCN payment method from each portfolio and property that uses it.
   4. Delete the payment method, then turn the LCN off again.

<Accordion title="Queries: find where an LCN payment method is used">
  Find the PM company's payment methods:

  ```sql lines
  SELECT * FROM payment_method
  WHERE relation_id = 37645 -- replace with the PM company ID
  AND relation_type = 'Latchel\Company'
  ```

  Find portfolios using them (swap `portfolio` for `property` to check properties):

  ```sql lines
  SELECT * FROM portfolio
  WHERE pm_company_id = 37645 -- replace with the PM company ID
  AND use_lcn_vendor_payments IN (
  ) -- list the payment method IDs you want to find
  ```
</Accordion>

## SP requests

### An SP wants to join the LCN

Ops can handle these. Reassign the ticket to the **Operations Team Email** teamspace in Wavelength.

If you're handling it yourself, see [SOP: Handling Service Provider (SP) Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Service-Provider-SP-Cases). Useful links:

- [A Guide in Joining Latchel Contractor Network](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wpGAA/A-Guide-in-Joining-Latchel-Contractor-Network)
- [Vendor sign-up page](https://latchel.com/vendor-sign-up/)

### An SP wants to be removed from a work order

| **Situation** | **What the SP does** |
| --- | --- |
| **Already assigned** to the work order | In their dashboard, open the work order and click **Provide Work Order Update** (top right), then choose **Remove Me From This Work Order**. See [A Service Provider's Guide in Updating a Work Order](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wpGAA/A-Service-Provider-s-Guide-in-Updating-a-Work-Order). |
| **Not assigned yet** | Reject the work order when it's offered. See [How to Reject a Work Order](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wpGAA/How-to-Reject-a-Work-Order-Service-Provider). |

### An SP doesn't want any more Latchel work orders

1. Ask the SP why, and share any feedback in `#product`.
2. Open the LCN company's page in Latchel.
3. Click the **three dots** (top right) and choose **Disable Account**.
4. Let the SP know it's done, then close the ticket.

For opting out of just one PM company, see [SOP: Handling Service Provider (SP) Tickets](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Service-Provider-SP-Cases).
