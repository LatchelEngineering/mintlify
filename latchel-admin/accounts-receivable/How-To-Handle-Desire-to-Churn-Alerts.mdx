## Overview

When a customer communicates a desire to cancel, it triggers a "Desire to Churn Alert" thread in #service-changes. CSMs then meet with the customer to try to prevent termination, and need from AR: the customer's contract terms (term remaining, early-termination fee), a draft final invoice, and the customer's current billing status (paid/open/past due).

## Actual Process

1. **Pull up the HubSpot deal** via the Deal Record link in the alert (select the record ending in "Live"), and check the Quotes/Attachments sections for a signed contract.
   - If no contract is found, no final invoice is required — skip to updating the thread.
   - If a contract is found, review the Purchase Terms / Payments to Latchel section to determine the termination fee formula (e.g., most recent monthly fee × remaining months, or initial units × rate × remaining months).
2. **Create a draft final invoice** in Rillet (Analyzed Checking account) reflecting the termination fee as separate line items per remaining contract month, apply tax if applicable, add a processing fee line if applicable, then screenshot it for the CSM — and delete the draft invoice afterward without posting it to the account.
3. **Update the Desire to Churn Alert thread**, tagging the CSM with the contract terms, the draft invoice screenshot, and the current billing status, using the standard templates for customers with or without a contract.
