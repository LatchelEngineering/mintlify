## **Purpose**

This SOP is a reference for Customer Success Managers (CSMs) and the Product Support (PS) team when handling common Accounts Receivable (AR) inquiries. It explains how to:

- Answer frequently asked billing questions
- Determine the correct AR request category
- Recognize when a request should be escalated to the AR team

**⏰ Reminder:** For anything not covered in this SOP, submit the inquiry through the appropriate Slack channel or workflow and coordinate with the AR team as needed.

## **Escalation Guidelines**

Submit a request to the AR team whenever:

- A billing record needs modifying
- Payment methods need updating
- Billing contacts need updating
- A customer disputes an invoice
- Unit count adjustments are requested
- Invoice history is needed and can't be obtained from existing records
- The request involves subscription billing and you're unsure how to proceed

**_When in doubt, submit the request in #accounts-receivable with all relevant customer information to minimize back-and-forth._**

## **Invoice copies and payment history**

Customers can get invoice PDFs from the original invoice email sent to their billing contacts. If they no longer have access:

- Direct them to [billing@latchel.com](mailto:billing@latchel.com), or
- Submit a request in #accounts-receivable.

**There is currently no customer-facing billing portal. This is a known Latchel limitation; a feature request has already been submitted, so you can advise the PM accordingly.**

## **Questions about an increased invoice**

Subscription invoices aren’t accessible in the Latchel console. To have an invoice reviewed, submit a request in the **#accounts-receivable** channel, and the AR team will look into it and get back to you with answers to your billing questions.

## **Account Termination Requests**

**Permanent termination**

- Terminations are handled by the assigned CSM, following the _Account Termination Steps_ SOP.
- Product Support should escalate via the Escalate to CSM workflow and tag the assigned CSM. _Note:_ no post in #accounts-receivable is necessary for termination requests.

**Future-dated termination**

Only the assigned CSM may schedule a future closure. The CSM should:

1. Set a reminder for the requested termination date.
2. Follow the _Account Termination Steps_ SOP when the date arrives.

**Temporary service inactivation**

Temporary service inactivation is not currently supported. However, Latchel does support temporary subscription changes, and the CSM must be involved in the process. On the AR side, we cover only the billing effects — the actual change to the service is handled separately. AR provides prorated billing, so please provide the applicable dates and rates for accurate invoicing.
