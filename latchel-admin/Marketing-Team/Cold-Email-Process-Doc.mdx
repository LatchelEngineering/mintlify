    -   Phone Number

**Cleaning the Files and Uploading to Woodpecker:**

-   Once we have a completed file from Cherry, we clean the list by doing the following:
    
    -   Remove duplicate email addresses & domains that did not have sufficient data.
        
-   We upload into woodpecker and tie the excel fields together to match Woodpecker fields:
    
    -   First,Last Name
        
    -   Company
        
    -   Phone Number
        
    -   Title
        
    -   Email Address
        
    -   Tag - Where the list came from, ie. Buildum List
        

**Creating the Campaigns**

Depending on what type of campaign we are running, we’ll set up a drip of usually 3 - 5 emails to go out over a series of days/weeks. Cadence is as follows:

Send Email 1 –> wait 3 days → Send email 2 → wait 6 days → send email 3 → mark as unresponsive.

Fundamental best practices are:

-   Split the total list between the team, ie if we have a list of 1,000, send 250 emails from each of the 4 team members.
    
-   Send emails Monday - Friday 8am - 5pm
    
-   Send 100-150 emails per day
    

Email components:

-   After the pitch, include a call to action asking them to reply to the email to schedule a time to chat.
    
-   Make sure the signature is of the person who you are sending the email from.
    
-   Include a line at the bottom of the email giving opportunity to opt out:
    
    -   _“Please message me back if you want to be removed from this email.”_
        
-   Emails with this line have better response rates:
    
    -   _“Please excuse typos. Sent from my iPhone.”_
