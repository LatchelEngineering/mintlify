title: "How to Fill Out and Use the Monthly Minimum Tracker"
---

## **Purpose**

The Monthly Minimum Tracker lists every customer on a monthly minimum contract, so AR knows what to charge them each month and when their contract needs renewing.

A monthly minimum contract means the customer pays at least a set amount every month. The tracker is used to:

- Make sure a new contract is created to replace each one that has ended.
- Guide invoicing, especially when invoices are created manually, by showing what to charge customers who don't reach their minimum.
- [AR Contract Minimum Renewals Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1fI8nVm54AoZGNChVlmVLKWRswOitz6I9s7Oen0IO7Ro/edit?gid=0#gid=0)

## How the monthly minimum works

If the invoice total (unit count × subscription price) is below the customer's minimum, invoice the minimum. If it is above, invoice the actual total.

The minimum is usually \$200, but some contracts set it at \$150 or \$100.

| **Contract minimum** | **Calculated total** | **Amount to invoice** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| \$200 | \$140 | \$200 (minimum applies) |
| \$200 | \$260 | \$260 (charged as is) |

## Filling out the columns

| **Column** | **What to enter** | **Why it matters** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| PM | The customer's company name | Identifies the account |
| Minimum Amount | The monthly minimum in the contract: \$200 (most common), \$150 or \$100 | Sets the lowest amount to invoice |
| Status | Where the customer is in their contract term (see the status list below) | Shows who is new, modified, renewed or gone |
| Rillet Contract Start | The billing start date | Determines the contract end date |
| Rillet Contract End | The date the contract term ends | Shows when a new contract is due |
| New contract needed for… | The month a new contract is needed | AR filters this at month end to find renewals |
| Notes | Special terms, such as a discount ending, a price increase, or a subscription change after a set date | Flags changes to apply when invoicing or renewing |

### **Status Values**

- First term: a new customer in their first contract term.
- Modified first term: the contract changed mid-term, for example, an upgrade or downgrade. A modification usually resets the term start to the modification date, so confirm with the CSM whether the original term continues or a new term begins. Example: Campbell had a 12-month term and changed their service in month 6.
- Renewed: the customer is on their second term or later. The goal is for every customer to finish their term and renew.
- Terminated: the customer ended their contract, either mid-term or after the term ended.

### <br />**Using the tracker**

At the end of every month (renewals):

1. Filter the New contract needed for… column by next month's name.
2. The filtered list is every customer who needs a renewal contract next month.
3. Create a new contract for each one, so no ended contract is left without a replacement.
4. Check Notes first for any discount ending, price increase or subscription change to include.

When creating invoices manually:

1. Calculate the total: unit count × subscription price.
2. Compare it with the customer's Minimum Amount.
3. Invoice the minimum if the total is lower; otherwise invoice the total as is.
4. Apply anything flagged in Notes that takes effect this month.
