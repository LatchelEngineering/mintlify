## **What Is an Overpayment?**

An overpayment happens when a customer sends a payment that is more than the invoiced amount. It can also occur as a double or duplicate payment — for example, when a customer pays the same invoice twice, usually unintentionally.

## **Process**

Use these steps to match an overpayment in the Cash Reconciliation tab.

1. Highlight the payment. Highlight the payment line item on the left of the Cash Reconciliation tab. Make sure the total amount paid (lower right) is already totaled together with the processing fee.
2. Find the invoice and note the excess. Search for the invoice number in the right tab. The lower right shows the excess — the difference between the payment received and the invoiced amount. Take note of this difference.
3. Match and Adjust. Click Match and Adjust and fill out the pop-up:
   - Vendor: optional, but recommended — enter the customer's name so the excess is tracked to the right customer, then click Create.
   - Account: select Over Payment from the dropdown.
4. Create the journal entry. Click Create and Match to create the journal entry for the overpayment. The matched line item then disappears from the Cash Reconciliation tab.
   - Verify: go to the Balance Sheet (Reporting tab in Rillet), scroll to the Over Payment header, and click the current-month amount to display all customers with excess payments — including the one you just created.
5. Track it. Record the overpayment on the AR Credit and Overpayment Tracker 2026 and fill out the necessary columns. In Column K, indicate that the reason for the credit is an overpayment. See[ <u>How to Fill Out and Use the AR Credit and Overpayment Tracker 2026</u>](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/accounting/How-to-Fill-Out-and-Use-the-AR-Credit-and-Overpayment-Tracker-2026) for details.
6. Apply it to the next invoice. Apply the overpayment through a credit memo with the correct account on the customer's next invoice. See[ <u>How To Create Monthly Subscription Invoices</u>](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/accounting/How-To-Create-Monthly-Subscription-Invoices) for how to apply overpayment credit memos.

## **Example — SureWay Property Management LLC**

SureWay sent a payment that exceeded their January invoice:

- **Payment received:** \$2,535.61 (February 10)
- **Invoice INV000006338 (January):** \$2,449.89
- **Overpayment:** \$85.72

Steps taken:

- Highlight the \$2,535.61 payment in the Cash Reconciliation tab and match it to invoice INV000006338; note the \$85.72 excess.
- Click Match and Adjust, tag SureWay as the vendor, and select the Overpayment account.
- Click Create and Match to generate the journal entry; the line item then clears from the Cash Reconciliation tab.
- Track the \$85.72 on the AR Credit and Overpayment Tracker 2026 (reason: overpayment, Column K).
- Apply the \$85.72 as a credit memo on SureWay's next invoice.
