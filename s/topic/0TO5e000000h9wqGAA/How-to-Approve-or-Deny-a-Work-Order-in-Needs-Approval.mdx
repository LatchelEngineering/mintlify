---
title: "How to Approve or Deny a Work Order in Needs Approval"
description: "When a new maintenance request matches a Needs Approval rule in your Intake Settings, or no rule matches and your fallback is set to Needs Approval, the work order is held until you approve or deny it."
---

When a resident submits a maintenance request, Latchel checks it against your **Intake Settings**. If the request matches one of your **Needs Approval** rules, or if no rule matches and your **"If no rules match"** outcome is set to **Needs Approval**, the work order is placed in **Needs Approval** status.

While a work order is in Needs Approval, **Latchel will not dispatch a service provider** until you approve it.

<Note>
  This guide covers work orders held for approval at intake. If the work order was flagged as a possible duplicate of an existing job, see [How to Approve or Deny a Duplicate Work Order](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Approve-or-Deny-a-Duplicate-Work-Order).
</Note>

### **Finding Work Orders That Need Your Approval**

You can find work orders waiting for your approval in any of these places:

- **Action Required** section of your dashboard. Use the **State** filter and select **Needs Approval** to see only these work orders.
- **Task Page**, which you can open from the **task icon** (box with a check mark) in the upper right corner of the portal.
- **Notifications**, if your team has set up email or SMS alerts for work orders that need approval. The notification includes a link to the work order.

### **Reviewing the Work Order**

Before you decide, open the work order and review:

- The **issue details**, photos, and the resident's answers to troubleshooting questions.
- The **Intake Processing Details**, which show which rule was applied and why the work order needs your approval.

<Tip>
  If the work order shouldn't have needed approval, use the **thumbs down** button next to the **Intake Processing Details** to let us know. If you want similar requests to skip approval in the future, update your rules in [Managing Intake Settings](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Managing-Intake-Settings).
</Tip>

### **Approving or Denying the Work Order**

**Step 1 — Open the approval**

From the work order page, use either option:

- **Option 1:** On the right side of the work order page, find the message **This work order requires your approval** and click **Approve**.
- **Option 2:** On the right side of the work order page, find the open task **Work Order Requires Approval** and click **View Details**. You'll be taken to the **Activity** tab, where you can choose **Approve** or **Deny**.

**Step 2 — Approve or deny**

- **To approve:** Click **Approve**, add a note, confirm the **approved budget**, and click **Submit**. Latchel will begin finding a service provider and scheduling the work order.
- **To deny:** Click **Deny**, add a note explaining why, and click **Submit**. The work order will be **canceled**.

<Warning>
  Work orders in Needs Approval stay on hold until you take action. Review them promptly so residents aren't left waiting.
</Warning>
