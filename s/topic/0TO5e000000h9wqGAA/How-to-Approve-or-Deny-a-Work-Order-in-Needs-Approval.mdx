---
title: "How to Approve or Deny a Work Order in Needs PM Approval"
description: "When a new maintenance request requires your approval based on your Intake Settings, the work order is placed in Needs PM Approval. As the Property Manager, you can approve or deny it directly from the work order page."
---

When a resident submits a request that matches a **Needs Approval** rule in your **Intake Settings**, or no rule matches and your **"If no rules match"** outcome is set to **Needs Approval**, the work order is placed on a pending state (**Needs PM Approval**).

Latchel will **not dispatch a service provider** until you take action. As the Property Manager, you will have to either **Deny**, or **Approve** the work order so it can move forward to scheduling.

<Note>
  The steps are the same as approving or denying a duplicate work order. If the work order was flagged as a possible duplicate of an existing job, see [How to Approve or Deny a Duplicate Work Order](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Approve-or-Deny-a-Duplicate-Work-Order).
</Note>

Option 1: On the right side of the work order page, you will notice a pop window that says **This work order requires your approval. Click on Approve.**

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/rtaImage-149.jpg"/>
</Frame>

Option 2: On the right side of the work order page, look at Open Task: Work Order Requires Approval, then click on **View Details.**

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/rtaImage-150.jpg"/>
</Frame>

You will be directed to **ACTIVITY**. This view will allow you to review the information of the work order and choose between **Approve** or **Deny**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/rtaImage-151.jpg"/>
</Frame>

Regardless of the option selected, the Property Manager has the authority to either Approve or Deny the work order. Upon choosing to **Approve**, please add a note, specify the approved budget, and click **Submit**. This will initiate the scheduling process for the work order.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/rtaImage-152.jpg"/>
</Frame>

But if you choose **Deny**, indicate a note, and hit **Submit**, then the work order will be canceled.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/rtaImage-153.jpg"/>
</Frame>

<Tip>
  **Want to see why a work order needed your approval?** Check the **Intake Processing Details** on the work order to see which rule was applied. To change which requests need your approval in the future, update your rules in [Managing Intake Settings](/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Managing-Intake-Settings).
</Tip>
