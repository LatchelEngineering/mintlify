Tired of texts like "I scheduled this for tomorrow" landing in your queue when there's nothing for you to do? With **AI Messaging**, text and in-app messages from residents and service providers become **Conversations** that Latchel AI handles for you. You only get pulled in when a message actually needs a person.

Every conversation is tagged to the right work order automatically, and the full message history is saved on the work order, so you always have the whole picture.

<Check>

  Latchel AI answers resident and service provider messages for you, keeps each one tied to the right work order, and only sends you the ones that need a person.
</Check>

## **How Conversations Work for Residents and Service Providers**

- When we text a resident or service provider about a work order, the text includes a link to their **conversation**. It opens in their phone's browser, with no app to install.
- There's one conversation per person, per work order.
- If they reply to the text instead of tapping the link, we point them to their conversation and save the text they sent in it, so nothing is lost.
- Residents can also open a conversation from their dashboard by choosing **Talk to Us About this Work Order** in the work order's **Action Menu**.

## **When a Conversation Needs a Person**

Latchel AI handles messages about maintenance and resident benefits. When someone asks for a person, or a message is outside what Latchel AI can handle (a leasing question, for example), the conversation is handed off and the resident or service provider is told they're being passed to a person:

- On work orders **Latchel manages**, it goes to the Latchel team.
- On work orders **you manage**, it goes to your team.

## **How to Message a Resident or Service Provider**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the work order's Conversations tab." icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={1} titleSize="h4">
    Open the work order, go to the **Conversations** tab, and choose the **Resident** or **Service provider** tab. Click **Open Conversation**.

    {/* TODO: screenshot of the work order Conversations tab */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who handles the reply." icon="2" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={2} titleSize="h4">
    Use **Handled by** to choose whether you or Latchel AI answers their next reply. Pick yourself when you need their answer to decide what happens next.

    {/* TODO: confirm the Handled by option names in the PM view and add a screenshot */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your message." icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={3} titleSize="h4">
    Type your message and send it. They'll get a text with a link to the conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also start a conversation from a resident's or service provider's profile, or their tab on the work order, by clicking **Message Resident** or **Message SP**.
</Tip>

## **Things to Remember**

<Note>
  You don't need to tag conversations to work orders or end them anymore. If someone writes again about the same work order, the conversation simply reopens as unhandled.
</Note>

<Note>
  AI Messaging doesn't change your automated notices, like appointment reminders and scheduling links. Those go out the same as before.
</Note>

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which messages does Latchel AI handle?">
    Text and in-app messages from residents and service providers about maintenance and resident benefits. If someone asks for a person, or the message is about something else, it's handed off to a person.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I still need to tag conversations to work orders?">
    No. Conversations are tagged to the right work order automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I close a conversation?">
    You don't need to. Replying or clicking **Mark as Handled** marks it handled, and there's no end step.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I see all the messages on a work order?">
    Open the work order's **Conversations** tab. It shows each resident and service provider conversation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my team answer every message instead of Latchel AI?">
    Yes. Email [**success@latchel.com**](mailto:success@latchel.com) and we can turn off AI replies for your company. Keep in mind that your team will then handle every resident and service provider message.
  </Accordion>
