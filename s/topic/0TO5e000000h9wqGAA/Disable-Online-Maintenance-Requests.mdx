---
title: "Disable Online Maintenance Requests"
description: "You can disable the option for residents to submit maintenance requests directly in your property management system so that all requests are routed through Latchel."
---

To do this, create a property group that includes all your properties, then turn off online maintenance requests for that group. This ensures a centralized workflow and lets Latchel handle all maintenance efficiently.

## **Create Property Group**

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Open Property Groups**" icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={1} titleSize="p">
    Go to the **dashboard** and in the **Tasks Menu** on the right-hand side, find **Account** section near the bottom and click **Property Groups**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Click New Property Group**" icon="2" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={2} titleSize="p">
    In the Tasks menu on the right click **New Property Group**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Name the group and select properties**" icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={3} titleSize="p">
    Enter a **Property Group Name**, then select all of the **Properties** you want in the group.

    <Warning>
      Do not use any special characters in the name of the group. Only use letters and numbers.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Filter the properties**" icon="4" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={4} titleSize="p">
    Click **"Click here to search"** to filter all properties by the following categories:
       - **Property Name** - Search by a property name to pull matching results.
       - **Address or Zip** - Search by property address or zip code to find all matching results.
       - **Property Group** - Search by all properties within another, existing property group to find all matching results.
       - **Portfolio** - Search by all properties within a portfolio to find all matching results.
       - **Owner** - Search by properties owned by a specific owner to find all matching results.
       - **State** - Search by properties within a specific state to find all matching results.
       - **Selection** - Select this option to limit the view to "Show only selected properties" and/or "Show hidden properties" within the new property group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Click Save**" icon="5" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={5} titleSize="p">
    Once you've selected all of your properties click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## **Disable Online Maintenance Requests**

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Open General Settings**" icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={1} titleSize="p">
    Click your **name** in the upper right corner of your database and select **General Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Click Work Order Settings**" icon="2" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={2} titleSize="p">
    In the **Maintenance** section, click **Work Order Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Disable for selected property groups**" icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={3} titleSize="p">
    Scroll down to **"Online Maintenance Requests"** then click **Disable for selected property groups**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Choose your property group**" icon="4" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={4} titleSize="p">
    Choose the **name** of the **property group** you created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Click Save**" icon="5" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={5} titleSize="p">
    Click **Save** once done.

    <Warning>
      Only Users who can edit Company Settings can edit the maintenance settings.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>
