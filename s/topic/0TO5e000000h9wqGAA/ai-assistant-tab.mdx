Wondering how much work Latchel AI is taking off your team's plate? The **AI Assistant** tab shows it all in one place: the messages and vendor conversations it handled, the time it spent (including time on the phone), and how much of that happened after hours.

<Check>
  **In other words:**

  The AI Assistant tab is your scorecard for Latchel AI. It shows what the AI handled for you and when.
</Check>

## **What You'll See**

  <Card title="Messages and vendor conversations" color="#ff9b8a" icon="message-bot" iconType="duotone">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Time spent" color="#7ed5d5" icon="clock" iconType="duotone">

  </Card>
