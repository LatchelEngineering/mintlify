This section allows property managers to quickly export key data such as resident lists, invoices, and property records. Having this information readily available makes it easier to review portfolio activity, share reports with stakeholders, and maintain accurate records for operational or financial tracking.

### **Types of Reports**

Click **Reports**, then choose from **Work Orders, Residents, Properties, and Invoices.**

### **Time Sheets Section**

No more guessing how long a job actually took. The Time Sheets report shows the clock-in and clock-out times your in-house techs log on work orders, and you can download it like any other report.

- **Actual vs. reported hours:** Compare the hours a tech was actually clocked in with the hours they reported.
- **Two time zones:** Times appear in both the property's time zone and UTC.
- **Automatic flags:** Big gaps between actual and reported clock-in or clock-out times are highlighted for you. So are unusual cases, like a clock-in and clock-out that span overnight.
