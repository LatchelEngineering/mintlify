### **New Features**

1. **A Paper Trail for Your Payment & Service Provider Settings**

If someone opts a property in or out of a payment setting, flips a toggle, or changes a threshold, you can now see exactly who made the change and when — right in your company, portfolio, or property activity log. No more guessing who touched a setting or when it happened.

- **What's covered:** Preferred Service Provider Payments, Latchel Service Provider Dispatch & Payments, the Vendor Network (LVN) toggle, and the invoice auto-approval threshold
- **Card removals, too:** if a card gets removed in Stripe, that now shows up in the log for every company, portfolio, and property that used it — previously it just disappeared with no record

2. **Get Bids From New Service Providers with Thumbtack**

Running short on service providers in an area? Our new partnership with **Thumbtack** puts a wider bench of local pros right inside your workflow — no jumping to another app to go find help.

- **Where to find it:** if your auto-queue comes up empty, we'll automatically suggest looking for a pro on Thumbtack. You can also start a search any time from the Other Actions menu on a work order that doesn't have a service provider assigned yet
- **Get real quotes, not just names:** answer a few quick questions about the job — we'll pre-fill what we can — then message one or more nearby pros for quotes, right from a new Thumbtack tab on the work order
- **Grow your network as you go:** liked how a pro handled the job? Add them as a preferred service provider once it's done, so they're easy to bring back next time

### **Resolved Bugs**

**Vendors Can Add Notes to Work Orders Again**

<Warning>
  **What was happening:**

  A handful of service providers stopped seeing the **Add Note** option under Provide Work Order Update on their assigned jobs — it just wasn't there, with no error message. That meant vendors couldn't leave notes on a job for PMs and owners to see, breaking a documented communication path.
</Warning>

<Check>
  **What's fixed:**

  Add Note is back for every affected vendor, so service providers can once again document updates directly on the work order.
</Check>
