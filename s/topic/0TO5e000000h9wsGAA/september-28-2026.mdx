### **Automated Vendor Status Calls Are Turning On for Autopilot Customers**

Following up with vendors is one of the most repetitive parts of running maintenance. If you're already an **Autopilot** customer, starting after the 9/30 launch, you won't have to do it yourself.

<Warning>
  **What's changing:**

  - After the webinar, we'll turn on **automatic daily outbound calls** to vendors for all existing Autopilot customers.
  - Every business day, Latchel builds an agenda and calls each vendor to get status on their open work orders.
  - Latchel writes the result back to every work order it discussed. If it deliberately skipped a job, it leaves a note explaining why.
</Warning>

<Check>
  **What this means for you:**

  - Status chasing comes off your team's to-do list. You only get pulled in when something needs a human decision.
  - **You don't need to do anything.** If you'd rather not have this turned on, reach out to [success@latchel.com](mailto:success@latchel.com) and we'll take care of it.
</Check>
