### **Resolved Bugs**

1. **Work orders no longer stall after your vendor marks a job complete**

<Warning>
  **What was happening:**

  When a service provider confirmed a job was finished on an occupied unit and an invoice was already on file, the work order waited on       the resident to confirm as well. If the resident never replied, it sat in that state indefinitely — someone had to spot it and push it through by hand
</Warning>

<Check>
  **What's fixed:**

  If the resident hasn't confirmed within 24 hours of your vendor's confirmation, Latchel now completes the work order automatically. Jobs close out on their own instead of waiting on a response that may never come.
</Check>

2. **Work orders no longer remain in "Finding Service Provider" after you've assigned a vendor**

<Warning>
  **What was happening:**

  If you assigned a service provider through **Take Action → Set a New Confirmed Work Date** and closed the workflow before picking an appointment time, the work order could remain in **Finding Service Provider** — even though your vendor was already assigned. Worse, nothing appeared in the activity log to explain it, and manually changing the status wouldn't stick.
</Warning>

<Check>
  **What's fixed:**

  Assigning a Service Provider now moves the work order forward automatically, whether or not you set an appointment time at the time of assignment. If you don’t, automated scheduling outreach to the vendor starts right away.
</Check>

3. **Residents only see open requests from current tenants in the portal**

<Warning>
  **What was happening:**

  When a resident started a new maintenance request, the "Is this a new issue?" screen could show work orders left behind by people who had already moved out.
</Warning>

<Check>
  **What's fixed:**

  That screen now lists only requests belonging to people currently living at the property, plus any you opened directly. Cleaner for the resident, and no more visibility into a former occupant's history.
</Check>
