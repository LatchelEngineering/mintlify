<Note>
  **This guide is for on-call technicians.** It applies when a property manager has added you to a contact rotation and you've accepted an on-call alert. If you have questions about your rotation, contact the property manager who set it up.
</Note>

Need to talk to the resident after you accept an on-call alert? You can call them right from the alert page. Latchel places the call and connects you, so you don't have to look up or share your personal number.

<Check>
  **In other words:**

  Tap **Call Resident**, confirm, answer the call from Latchel, and press 1. You'll be connected to the resident.
</Check>

## **What This Feature Does**

  <Card title="Calls from the alert page" color="#ff9b8a" icon="phone-arrow-up-right" iconType="duotone">
    A **Call Resident** button appears on the on-call alert page after you accept the alert.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Keeps your number private" color="#7ed5d5" icon="user-shield" iconType="duotone">
    The resident sees the property manager's Latchel-assigned number, and your number is hidden from the resident's job screen.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Logged on the alert" color="#7ed5d5" icon="clipboard-list" iconType="duotone">
    A log entry noting that you called the resident, with a link to the call, is added to the on-call alert page.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Works on your job screen too" color="#ff9b8a" icon="mobile-screen" iconType="duotone">
    The **Call Resident** button is also available on your public job screen.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## **How to Call a Resident From an On-Call Alert**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept the on-call alert." icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={1} titleSize="h4">
    Open the on-call alert and accept it. Once it's accepted, the **Call Resident** button appears on the alert page.

    {/* TODO: screenshot of the on-call alert page showing the Call Resident button */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Call Resident." icon="2" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={2} titleSize="h4">
    You'll see a short explanation of what will happen next.

    {/* TODO: screenshot of the Call Resident explanation with the Confirm button */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Confirm." icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={3} titleSize="h4">
    Latchel calls your phone. The call comes from the on-call number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the call and press 1." icon="4" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={4} titleSize="h4">
    Pressing 1 lets Latchel know you're on the line. Latchel then connects you to the resident.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## **Things to Remember**

<Note>
  The **Call Resident** button stays available for 72 hours after you accept the alert. It only shows when both you and the resident have a phone number on file.
</Note>

<Warning>
  All calls are recorded, and everyone on the call hears a short message saying so.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Backup contacts on the rotation get the same **Call Resident** button, so whoever accepts the alert can reach the resident.
</Tip>

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the resident see my phone number?">
    No. The resident sees the property manager's Latchel-assigned number when you call, and your number is hidden from the resident's job screen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do I have to press 1?">
    Pressing 1 confirms that a real person answered and not your voicemail. Latchel connects you to the resident only after you press 1.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why don't I see the Call Resident button?">
    The button shows only after you accept the alert, only for 72 hours after that, and only when both you and the resident have a phone number on file. If something still looks wrong, email [**success@latchel.com**](mailto:success@latchel.com).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
