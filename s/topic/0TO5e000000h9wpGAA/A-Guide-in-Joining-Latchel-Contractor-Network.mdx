<Note>
  **Latchel no longer offers direct sign-ups to our service provider network.** Instead, we rely on partnerships such as **Thumbtack** to connect property managers with service providers.
</Note>

### **How Property Managers Find New Service Providers**

When a property manager using Latchel needs a new pro for a job, they can search Thumbtack right from the work order, request quotes, and assign the pro they choose.

Property managers can also add the service providers they already work with to their own Latchel account.

### **Already Working With a Property Manager on Latchel?**

If a property manager already sends you work orders through Latchel, you can manage them from your Latchel portal.
