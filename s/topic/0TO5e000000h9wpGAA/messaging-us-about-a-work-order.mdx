Need to share an update on a job, or have a question about a work order? Message us right from the work order. Latchel AI answers right away, and if you need a person, we'll bring one in.

<Check>
  **In other words:**

  Tap the link in our text to open your conversation about a work order. You can send updates and take care of the job right there, and Latchel AI passes you to a person when needed.
</Check>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your conversation." icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={1} titleSize="h4">
    Tap the link in any text we send you about a work order. Your conversation opens in your phone's browser, with no app to download.

  <Step title="Take care of the work order in the conversation." icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" stepNumber={3} titleSize="h4">

  </Step>
</Steps>

## **Things to Remember**

<Note>
  Replied to our text instead of tapping the link? No problem. We'll send you the link to your conversation and save your message there.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Each work order has its own conversation, so you'll always be messaging about the right job.
</Tip>

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who am I talking to?">
    Latchel AI answers first. If you ask for a person, or your message is something it can't help with, it lets you know and passes you to a person.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to download an app?">
    No. Your conversation opens in your phone's web browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I work on several jobs. How do I keep them straight?">
    Each work order has its own conversation. Use the link from the text about that job.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
